# Revelation — Matthew Henry Concise Commentary

# Contents

* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-1|Revelation 1 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The Divine origin, the design, and the importance of this book. (1–3). The apostle John salutes the seven churches of Asia. (4–8). Declares when, where, and how, the revelation was made to him. (9–11). His vision, in which he saw Christ appear. (12–20).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-10|Revelation 10 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The Angel of the covenant presents a little open book, which is followed with seven thunders. (1–4). At the end of the following prophecies, time should be no more. (5–7). A voice directs the apostle to eat the book; (8–10). and tells him he must prophesy further. (11).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-11|Revelation 11 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The state of the church is represented under the figure of a temple measured. (1, 2). Two witnesses prophesy is sackcloth. (3–6). They are slain, after which they arise and ascend to heaven. (7–13). Under the seventh trumpet, all antichristian powers are to be destroyed and there will be a glorious state of Christ's kingdom upon earth. (14–19).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-12|Revelation 12 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: A description of the church of Christ and of Satan, under the figures of a woman and of a great red dragon. (1–6). Michael and his angels fight against the devil and his angels, who are defeated. (7–12). The dragon persecutes the church. (13, 14). His vain endeavours to destroy her, He renews his war against her seed. (14–17).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-13|Revelation 13 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: A wild beast rises out of the sea, to whom the dragon gives his power. (1–10). Another beast, which has two horns like a lamb, but speaks as a dragon. (11–15). It obliges all to worship its image, and receive its mark, as persons devoted to it. (16–18).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-14|Revelation 14 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Those faithful to Christ celebrate the praises of God. (1–5). Three angels; one proclaiming the everlasting gospel; another, the downfal of Babylon; and a third, the dreadful wrath of God on the worshippers of the beast. The blessedness of those who die in the Lord. (6–13). A vision of Christ with a sickle, and of a harvest ripe for cutting down. (14–16). The emblem of a vintage fully ripe, trodden in the wine-press of God's wrath. (17–20).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-15|Revelation 15 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: A song of praise is sung by the church. (1–4). Seven angels with the seven plagues; and to them one of the living creatures gives seven golden vials full of the wrath of God. (5–8).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-16|Revelation 16 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The first vial is poured out on the earth, the second on the sea, the third on the rivers and fountains. (1–7). The fourth on the sun, the fifth on the seat of the beast. (8–11). The sixth on the great river Euphrates. (12–16). And the seventh on the air, when shall follow the destruction of all antichristian enemies. (17–21).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-17|Revelation 17 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: One of the angels who had the vials, explains the meaning of the former vision of the antichristian beast that was to reign 1260 years, and then to be destroyed. (1–6). And interprets the mystery of the woman, and the beast that had seven heads and ten horns. (7–18).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-18|Revelation 18 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Another angel from heaven proclaims the fall of mystical Babylon. (1–3). A voice from heaven admonishes the people of God, lest they partake of her plagues. (4–8). The lamentations over her. (9–19). The church called upon to rejoice in her utter ruin. (20–24).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-19|Revelation 19 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The church in heaven and that on earth triumph, and praise the Lord for his righteous judgments. (1–10). A vision of Christ going forth to destroy the beast and his armies. (11–21).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-2|Revelation 2 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Epistles to the churches in Asia, with warnings and encouragements, To the church at Ephesus; (1–7). at Smyrna; (8–11). at Pergamos; (12–17). and at Thyatira. (18–29).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-20|Revelation 20 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Satan is bound for a thousand years. (1–3). The first resurrection; those are blessed that have part therein. (4–6). Satan loosed, Gog and Magog. (7–10). The last and general resurrection. (11–15).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-21|Revelation 21 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: A new heaven, and new earth: the new Jerusalem where God dwells, and banishes all sorrow from his people. (1–8). Its heavenly origin, glory, and secure defence. (9–21). Its perfect happiness, as enlightened with the presence of God and the Lamb, and in the free access of multitudes, made holy. (22–27).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-22|Revelation 22 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: A description of the heavenly state, under the figures of the water and the tree of life, and of the throne of God and the Lamb. (1–5). The truth and certain fulfilling of all the prophetic visions, The Holy Spirit, and the bride, the church, invite, and say, Come. (6–19). The closing blessing. (20, 21).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-3|Revelation 3 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Epistles to the church at Sardis; (1–6). at Philadelphia; (7–13). and Laodicea. (14–22).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-4|Revelation 4 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: A vision of God, as on his glorious throne, around which were twenty-four elders and four living creatures. (1–8). Whose songs, and those of the holy angels, the apostle heard. (9–11).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-5|Revelation 5 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: A book sealed with seven seals, which could be opened by none but Christ, who took the book to open it. (1–7). Upon which all honour is ascribed to him, as worthy to open it. (8–14).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-6|Revelation 6 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The opening of the seals, The first, second, third, and fourth. (1–8). The fifth. (9–11). The sixth. (12–17).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-7|Revelation 7 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: A pause between two great periods. (1–3). The peace, happiness, and safety of the saints, as signified by an angel's sealing 144,000. (4–8). A song of praise. (9–12). The blessedness and glory of those that suffered martyrdom for Christ. (13–17).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-8|Revelation 8 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The seventh seal is opened and seven angels appear with seven trumpets, ready to proclaim the purposes of God. (1, 2). Another angel casts fire on the earth, which produces terrible storms of vengeance. (3–5). The seven angels prepare to sound their trumpets. (6). Four sound them. (7–12). Another angel denounces greater woes to come. (13).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-9|Revelation 9 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The fifth trumpet is followed by a representation of another star as falling from heaven and opening the bottomless pit, out of which come swarms of locusts. (1–12). The sixth trumpet is followed by the loosing of four angels bound in the great river Euphrates. (13–21).
