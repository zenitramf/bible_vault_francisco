# Mark — Matthew Henry Concise Commentary

* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/mark/chapter-1|Mark 1 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The office of John the Baptist. (1–8). The baptism and temptation of Christ. (9–13). Christ preaches and calls disciples. (14–22). He casts out an unclean spirit. (23–28). He heals many diseased. (29–39). He heals a leper. (40–45).

(46–52).

(41–44).

* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/mark/chapter-14|Mark 14 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Christ anointed at Bethany. (1–11). Christ before the high priest. (53–65). Peter denies Christ. (66–72).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/mark/chapter-15|Mark 15 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Christ before Pilate. (1–14). Christ led to be crucified. (15–21). The crucifixion. (22–32). The death of Christ. (33–41). His body buried. (42–47).

Levi's call, and the entertainment given to Jesus. (13–17). Why Christ's disciples did not fast. (18–22). He justifies his disciples for plucking corn on the sabbath. (23–28).
