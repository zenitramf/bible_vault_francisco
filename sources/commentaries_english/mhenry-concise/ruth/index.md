# Ruth — Matthew Henry Concise Commentary

# Contents

* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ruth/chapter-1|Ruth 1 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Elimelech and his sons die in the land of Moab. (1–5). Naomi returns home. (6–14). Orpah stays behind, but Ruth goes with Naomi. (15–18). They come to Bethlehem. (19–22).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ruth/chapter-2|Ruth 2 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Ruth gleans in the field of Boaz. (1–3). The kindness of Boaz to Ruth. (4–16). Ruth returns to her mother-in-law. (17–23).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ruth/chapter-3|Ruth 3 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The directions given to Ruth by Naomi. (1–5). Boaz acknowledges the duty of a kinsman. (6–13). Ruth's return to her mother-in-law. (14–18).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ruth/chapter-4|Ruth 4 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The kinsman refuses to redeem Ruth's inheritance. (1–8). Boaz marries Ruth. (9–12). Birth of Obed. (13–22).
