# Luke — Matthew Henry Concise Commentary

Interview of Mary and Elisabeth. (39–56). The birth of John the Baptist. (57–66). The song of Zacharias. (67–80).

* [Luke 12 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online](chapter-12.md) - In this chapter: Christ reproves the interpreters of the law. (1–12). A caution against covetousness, The parable of the rich man. (13–21). Worldly care reproved. (22–40). Watchfulness enforced. (41–53). A warning to be reconciled to God. (54–59).
* [Luke 13 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online](chapter-13.md) - In this chapter: Christ exhorts to repentance from the case of the Galileans and others. (1–5). Christ's reproof to Herod, and to the people of Jerusalem. (31–35).
* [Luke 14 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online](chapter-14.md) - In this chapter: Christ heals a man on the sabbath. (1–6). He teaches humility. (7–14). Parable of the great supper. (15–24). The necessity of consideration and self-denial. (25–35).
* [Luke 15 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online](chapter-15.md) - In this chapter: Parables of the lost sheep, and the piece of silver. (1–10). The prodigal son, his wickedness and distress. (11–16). His repentance and pardon. (17–24). The elder brother offended. (25–32).
* [Luke 16 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online](chapter-16.md) - In this chapter: The parable of the unjust steward. (1–12). Christ reproves the hypocrisy of the covetous Pharisees. (13–18). The rich man and Lazarus. (19–31).
* [Luke 17 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online](chapter-17.md) - In this chapter: To avoid offences, To pray for increase of faith, Humility taught. (11–19). Ten lepers cleansed. (1–10). Christ's kingdom. (20–37).
* [Luke 18 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online](chapter-18.md) - In this chapter: The parable of the importunate widow. (1–8). The Pharisee and the publican. (9–14). * [Luke 19 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online](chapter-19.md) - In this chapter: The conversion of Zaccheus. (1–10). The parable of the nobleman and his servants. (11–27). Christ enters Jerusalem. (28–40). Christ laments over Jerusalem. (41–48).
