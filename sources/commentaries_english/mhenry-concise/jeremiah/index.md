# Jeremiah — Matthew Henry Concise Commentary

# Contents

* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-1|Jeremiah 1 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Jeremiah's call to the prophetic office. (1-10). A vision of an almond-tree and of a seething-pot, Divine protection is promised. (11-19).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-10|Jeremiah 10 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The absurdity of idolatry. (1-16). Destruction denounced against Jerusalem. (17-25).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-11|Jeremiah 11 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The disobedient Jews reproved. (1-10). Their utter ruin. (11-17). The people would be destroyed who sought the prophet's life. (18-23).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-12|Jeremiah 12 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Jeremiah complains of the prosperity of the wicked. (1-6). The heavy judgments to come upon the nation. (7-13). Divine mercy to them, and even to the nations around. (14-17).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-13|Jeremiah 13 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The glory of the Jews should be marred. (1-11). All ranks should suffer misery, An earnest exhortation to repentance. (12-17). An awful message to Jerusalem and its king. (18-27).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-14|Jeremiah 14 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: A drought upon the land of Judah. (1-7). A confession of sin in the name of the people. (8-9). The Divine purpose to punish is declared. (10-16). The people supplicate. (17-22).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-15|Jeremiah 15 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The destruction of the wicked described. (1-9). The prophet laments such messages, and is reproved. (10-14). He supplicates pardon, and is promised protection. (15-21).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-16|Jeremiah 16 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Prohibitions given to the prophet. (1-9). The justice of God in these judgments. (10-13). Future restoration of the Jews, and the conversion of the Gentiles. (14-21).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-17|Jeremiah 17 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The fatal consequences of the idolatry of the Jews. (1-4). The happiness of the man that trusts in God; the end of the opposite character. (5-11). The malice of the prophet's enemies. (12-18). The observance of the sabbath. (19-27).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-18|Jeremiah 18 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: God's power over his creatures is represented by the potter. (1-10). The Jews exhorted to repentance, and judgments foretold. (11-17). The prophet appeals to God. (18-23).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-19|Jeremiah 19 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: By the type of breaking an earthen vessel, Jeremiah is to predict the destruction of Judah. (1-15).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-2|Jeremiah 2 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: God expostulates with his people. (1-8). Their revolt beyond example. (9-13). Guilt the cause of sufferings. (14-19). The sins of Judah. (20-28). Their false confidence. (29-37).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-20|Jeremiah 20 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The doom of Pashur, who ill-treated the prophet. (1-6). Jeremiah complains of hard usage. (7-13). He regrets his ever having been born. (14-18).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-21|Jeremiah 21 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The only way of deliverance is to be surrendering to the Babylonians. (1-10). The wickedness of the king and his household. (11-14).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-22|Jeremiah 22 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Justice is recommended, and destruction threatened in case of disobedience. (1-9). The captivity of Jehoiakim, and the end of Jeconiah. (10-19). The doom of the royal family. (20-30).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-23|Jeremiah 23 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The restoration of the Jews to their own land. (1-8). The wickedness of the priests and prophets of Judah, The people exhorted not to listen to false promises. (9-22). The pretenders to inspiration threatened. (23-32). Also the scoffers at true prophecy. (33-40).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-24|Jeremiah 24 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Good and bad figs represent the Jews in captivity, and those who remain in their own land. (1-10).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-25|Jeremiah 25 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The Jews rebuked for not obeying calls to repentance. (1-7). Their captivity during seventy years is expressly foretold. (8-14). Desolations upon the nations shown by the emblem of a cup of wrath. (15-29). The judgments again declared. (30-38).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-26|Jeremiah 26 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The destruction of the temple and city foretold. (1-6). Jeremiah's life is threatened. (7-15). He is defended by the elders. (16-24).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-27|Jeremiah 27 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The neighbouring nations to be subdued. (1-11). Zedekiah is warned to yield. (12-18). The vessels of the temple to be carried to Babylon, but afterwards to be restored. (19-22).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-28|Jeremiah 28 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: A false prophet opposes Jeremiah. (1-9). The false prophet warned of his approaching death. (10-17).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-29|Jeremiah 29 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Two letters to the captives in Babylon; In the first, they are recommended to be patient and composed. (1-19). In the second, judgments are denounced against the false prophets who deceived them. (20-32).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-3|Jeremiah 3 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Exhortations to repentance. (1-5). Judah more guilty than Israel. (6-11). But pardon is promised. (12-20). The children of Israel express their sorrow and repentance. (21-25).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-30|Jeremiah 30 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Troubles which shall be before the restoration of Israel. (1-11). Encouragement to trust Divine promises. (12-17). The blessings under Christ, and the wrath on the wicked. (18-24).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-31|Jeremiah 31 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The restoration of Israel. (1-9). Promises of guidance and happiness; Rachel lamenting. (10-17). Ephraim laments his errors. (18-20). The promised Saviour. (21-26). God's care over the church. (27-34). Peace and prosperity in gospel time. (35-40).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-32|Jeremiah 32 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Jeremiah buys a field. (1-15). The prophet's prayer. (16-25). God declares that he will give up his people, but promises to restore them. (26-44).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-33|Jeremiah 33 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The restoration of the Jews. (1-13). The Messiah promised; happiness of his times. (14-26).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-34|Jeremiah 34 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Zedekiah's death at Babylon foretold. (1-7). The Jews reproved for compelling their poor brethren to return to unlawful bondage. (8-22).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-35|Jeremiah 35 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The obedience of the Rechabites. (1-11). The Jews' disobedience to the Lord. (12-19).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-36|Jeremiah 36 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Baruch is to write the prophecies of Jeremiah. (1-8). The princes advise them to hide themselves. (9-19). The king having heard a part, burns the roll. (20-32).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-37|Jeremiah 37 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The Chaldean army will return. (1-10). Jeremiah is imprisoned. (11-21).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-38|Jeremiah 38 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Jeremiah is cast into a dungeon, from whence he is delivered by an Ethiopian. (1-13). He advises the king to surrender to the Chaldeans. (14-28).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-39|Jeremiah 39 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The taking of Jerusalem. (1-10). Jeremiah used well. (11-14). Promises of safety to Ebed-melech. (15-18).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-4|Jeremiah 4 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Exhortations and promises. (1-2). Judah exhorted to repentance. (3-4). Judgements denounced. (5-18). The approaching ruin of Judah. (19-31).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-40|Jeremiah 40 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Jeremiah is directed to go to Gedaliah. (1-6). A conspiracy against Gedaliah. (7-16).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-41|Jeremiah 41 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Ishmael murders Gedaliah. (1-10). Johanan recovers the captives, and purposes to retire to Egypt. (11-18).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-42|Jeremiah 42 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Johanan desires Jeremiah to ask counsel of God. (1-6). They are assured of safety in Judea, but of destruction in Egypt. (7-22).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-43|Jeremiah 43 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The leaders carry the people to Egypt. (1-7). Jeremiah foretells the conquest of Egypt. (8-13).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-44|Jeremiah 44 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The Jews in Egypt persist in idolatry. (1-14). They refuse to reform. (15-19). Jeremiah then denounces destruction upon them. (20-30).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-45|Jeremiah 45 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: An encouragement sent to Baruch. (1-5).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-46|Jeremiah 46 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The defeat of the Egyptians. (1-12). Their overthrow after the siege of Tyre. (13-26). A promise of comfort to the Jews. (27-28).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-47|Jeremiah 47 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The calamities of the Philistines. (1-7).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-48|Jeremiah 48 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Prophecies against Moab for pride and security. (1-13). For carnal confidence and contempt of God. (14-47).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-49|Jeremiah 49 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Prophecies relative to the Ammonites. (1-6). The Edomites. (7-22). The Syrians. (23-27). The Kedarenes. (28-33). The Elamites. (34-39).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-5|Jeremiah 5 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The Jews' profession of religion was hypocritical. (1-9). The cruel proceedings of their enemies. (10-18). Their apostacy and idolatry. (19-31).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-50|Jeremiah 50 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The ruin of Babylon. (1-3, 8-16, 21-32, 35-46) . The redemption of God's people. (4-7, 17-20, 33, 34).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-51|Jeremiah 51 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Babylon's doom; God's controversy with her; encouragements from thence to the Israel of God. (1-58). The confirming of this. (59-64).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-52|Jeremiah 52 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The fate of Zedekiah. (1-11). The destruction of Jerusalem. (12-23). The captivities. (24-30). The advancement of Jehoiachin. (31-34).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-6|Jeremiah 6 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The invasion of Judea. (1-8). The justice of God's proceedings. (9-17). All methods used to amend them had been without success. (18-30).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-7|Jeremiah 7 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Confidence in the temple is vain. (1-16). The provocation by persisting in idolatry. (17-20). God justifies his dealings with them. (21-28). And threatens vengeance. (29-34).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-8|Jeremiah 8 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The remains of the dead exposed. (1-3). The stupidity of the people, compared with the instinct of the brute creation. (4-13). The alarm of the invasion, and lamentation. (14-22).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-9|Jeremiah 9 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The people are corrected, Jerusalem is destroyed. (1-11). The captives suffer in a foreign land. (12-22). God's loving-kindness, He threatens the enemies of his people. (23-26).
