# Jonah — Matthew Henry Concise Commentary

# Contents

* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jonah/chapter-1|Jonah 1 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Jonah, sent to Nineveh, flees to Tarshish. (1–3). He is stayed by a tempest. (4–7). His discourse with the mariners. (8–12). He is cast into the sea, and miraculously preserved. (13–17).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jonah/chapter-2|Jonah 2 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The prayer of Jonah. (1–9). He is delivered from the fish. (10).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jonah/chapter-3|Jonah 3 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Jonah sent again to Nineveh, preaches there. (1–4). Nineveh is spared upon the repentance of the inhabitants. (5–10).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jonah/chapter-4|Jonah 4 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Jonah repines at God's mercy to Nineveh, and is reproved. (1–4). He is taught by the withering of a gourd, that he did wrong. (5–11).
