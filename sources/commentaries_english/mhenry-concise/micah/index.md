# Micah — Matthew Henry Concise Commentary

# Contents

* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/micah/chapter-1|Micah 1 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The wrath of God against Israel. (1–7). Also against Jerusalem and other cities, Their precautions vain. (8–16).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/micah/chapter-2|Micah 2 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The sins and desolations of Israel. (1–5). Their evil practices. (6–11). A promise of restoration. (12, 13).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/micah/chapter-3|Micah 3 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The cruelty of the princes, and the falsehood of the prophets. (1–8). Their false security. (9–12).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/micah/chapter-4|Micah 4 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The peace of the kingdom of Christ. (1–8). The judgments to come upon Jerusalem, but the final triumph of Israel. (9–13).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/micah/chapter-5|Micah 5 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The birth of Christ and conversion of the Gentiles. (1–6). The triumphs of Israel. (7–15).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/micah/chapter-6|Micah 6 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: God's controversy with Israel. (1–5). The duties God requires. (6–8). The wickedness of Israel. (9–16).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/micah/chapter-7|Micah 7 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The general prevalence of wickedness. (1–7). Reliance on God, and triumph over enemies. (8–13). Promises and encouragements for Israel. (14–20).
