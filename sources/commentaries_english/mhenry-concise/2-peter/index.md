# 2 Peter — Matthew Henry Concise Commentary

# Contents

* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-peter/chapter-1|2 Peter 1 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Exhortations to add the exercise of various other graces to faith. (1–11). The apostle looks forward to his approaching decease. (12–15). And confirms the truth of the gospel, relating to Christ's appearing to judgment. (16–21).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-peter/chapter-2|2 Peter 2 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Believers are cautioned against false teachers, and the certainty of their punishment shown from examples. (1–9). An account of these seducers, as exceedingly wicked. (10–16). But as making high pretences to liberty and purity. (17–22).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-peter/chapter-3|2 Peter 3 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The design here is to remind of Christ's final coming to judgement. (1–4). He will appear unexpectedly, when the present frame of nature will be dissolved by fire. (5–10). From thence is inferred the need for holiness, and stedfastness in the faith. (11–18).
