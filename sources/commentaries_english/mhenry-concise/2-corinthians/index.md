# 2 Corinthians — Matthew Henry Concise Commentary

# Contents

* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-corinthians/chapter-1|2 Corinthians 1 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The apostle blesses God for comfort in, and deliverance out of troubles. (1–11). He professes his own and his fellow-labourers' integrity. (12–14). Gives reasons for his not coming to them. (15–24).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-corinthians/chapter-10|2 Corinthians 10 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The apostle states his authority with meekness and humility. (1–6). Reasons with the Corinthians. (7–11). Seeks the glory of God, and to be approved of him. (12–18).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-corinthians/chapter-11|2 Corinthians 11 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The apostle gives the reasons for speaking in his own commendation. (1–14). Shows that he had freely preached the gospel. (5–15). Explains what he was going to add in defence of his own character. (16–21). He gives an account of his labours, cares, sufferings, dangers, and deliverances. (22–33).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-corinthians/chapter-12|2 Corinthians 12 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The apostle's revelations. (1–6). Which were improved to his spiritual advantage. (7–10). The signs of an apostle were in him, His purpose of making them a visit; but he expresses his fear lest he should have to be severe with some. (11–21).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-corinthians/chapter-13|2 Corinthians 13 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The apostle threatens obstinate offenders. (1–6). He prays for their reformation. (7–10). And ends the epistle with a salutation and blessing. (11–14).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-corinthians/chapter-2|2 Corinthians 2 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Reasons for the apostle not coming to Corinth. (1–4). Directions about restoring the repentant offender. (5–11). An account of his labours and success in spreading the gospel of Christ. (12–17).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-corinthians/chapter-3|2 Corinthians 3 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The preference of the gospel to the law given by Moses. (1–11). The preaching of the apostle was suitable to the excellency and evidence of the gospel, through the power of the Holy Ghost. (12–18).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-corinthians/chapter-4|2 Corinthians 4 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The apostles laboured with much diligence, sincerity, and faithfulness. (1–7). Their sufferings for the gospel were great, yet with rich supports. (8–12). Prospects of eternal glory keep believers from fainting under troubles. (13–18).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-corinthians/chapter-5|2 Corinthians 5 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The apostle's hope and desire of heavenly glory. (1–8). This excited to diligence. The reasons of his being affected with zeal for the Corinthians. (9–15). The necessity of regeneration, and of reconciliation with God through Christ. (16–21).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-corinthians/chapter-6|2 Corinthians 6 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The apostle, with others, proved themselves faithful ministers of Christ, by their unblamable life and behaviour. (1–10). By affection for them, And by earnest concern, that they might have no fellowship with unbelievers and idolaters. (11–18).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-corinthians/chapter-7|2 Corinthians 7 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: An exhortation to holiness, and the whole church entreated to bear affection to the apostle. (1–4). He rejoiced in their sorrowing to repentance. (5–11). And in the comfort they and Titus had together. (12–16).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-corinthians/chapter-8|2 Corinthians 8 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The apostle reminds them of charitable contributions for the poor saints. (1–6). Enforces this by their gifts, and by the love and grace of Christ. (7–9). By the willingness they had shown to this good work. (10–15). He recommends Titus to them. (16–24).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-corinthians/chapter-9|2 Corinthians 9 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The reason for sending Titus to collect their alms. (1–5). The Corinthians to be liberal and cheerful, The apostle thanks God for his unspeakable gift. (6–15).
