# Habakkuk — Matthew Henry Concise Commentary

# Contents

* [Habakkuk 1 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online](chapter-1.md) - In this chapter: The wickedness of the land. The fearful vengeance to be executed. (1–11). These judgments to be inflicted by a nation more wicked than themselves. (12–17).
* [Habakkuk 2 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online](chapter-2.md) - In this chapter: Habakkuk must wait in faith. (1–4). Judgments upon the Chaldeans. (5–14). Also upon drunkenness and idolatry. (15–20).
* [Habakkuk 3 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online](chapter-3.md) - In this chapter: The prophet beseeches God for his people. (1, 2). He calls to mind former deliverances. (3–15). His firm trust in the Divine mercy. (16–19).
