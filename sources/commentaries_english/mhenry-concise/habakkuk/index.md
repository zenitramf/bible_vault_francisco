# Contents

* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/habakkuk/chapter-1|Habakkuk 1 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The wickedness of the land. The fearful vengeance to be executed. (1–11). These judgments to be inflicted by a nation more wicked than themselves. (12–17).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/habakkuk/chapter-2|Habakkuk 2 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Habakkuk must wait in faith. (1–4). Judgments upon the Chaldeans. (5–14). Also upon drunkenness and idolatry. (15–20).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/habakkuk/chapter-3|Habakkuk 3 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The prophet beseeches God for his people. (1, 2). He calls to mind former deliverances. (3–15). His firm trust in the Divine mercy. (16–19).
