# Colossians — Matthew Henry Concise Commentary

# Contents

* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/colossians/chapter-1|Colossians 1 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The apostle Paul salutes the Colossians, and blesses God for their faith, love, and hope. (1–8). Prays for their fruitfulness in spiritual knowledge. (9–14). Gives a glorious view of Christ. (15–23). And sets out his own character, as the apostle of the Gentiles. (24–29).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/colossians/chapter-2|Colossians 2 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The apostle expresses his love to, and joy in believers. (1–7). He cautions against the errors of heathen philosophy; also against Jewish traditions, and rites which had been fulfilled in Christ. (8–17). Against worshipping angels; and against legal ordinances. (18–23).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/colossians/chapter-3|Colossians 3 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The Colossians exhorted to be heavenly-minded; (1–4). to mortify all corrupt affections; (5–11). to live in mutual love, forbearance, and forgiveness; (12–17). and to practise the duties of wives and husbands, children, parents, and servants. (18–25).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/colossians/chapter-4|Colossians 4 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: Masters to do their duty towards servants. (1). Persons of all ranks to persevere in prayer, and Christian prudence. (2–6). The apostle refers to others for an account of his affairs. (7–9). Sends greetings; and concludes with a blessing. (10–18).
