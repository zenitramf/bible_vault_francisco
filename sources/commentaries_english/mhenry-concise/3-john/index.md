# 3 John — Matthew Henry Concise Commentary

# Contents

* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/3-john/chapter-1|3 John 1 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The apostle commends Gaius for piety and hospitality. (1–8). Cautions him against siding with Diotrephes, who was a turbulent spirit; but recommends Demetrius as a man of excellent character. (9–12). He hopes soon to see Gaius. (13, 14).
