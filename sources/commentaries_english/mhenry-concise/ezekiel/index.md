* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-18|Ezekiel 18 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: God has no respect of persons. (1-20). The Divine providence is vindicated. (21-29). A gracious invitation to repentance. (30-32).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-19|Ezekiel 19 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: A parable lamenting the ruin of Jehoahaz and Jehoiakim. (1-9). Another describing the desolation of the people. (10-14).
* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-2|Ezekiel 2 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The prophet is directed what he is to do. (1-5). And encouraged to be resolute, faithful, and devoted. (6-10).

* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-21|Ezekiel 21 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The ruin of Judah under the emblem of a sharp sword.
