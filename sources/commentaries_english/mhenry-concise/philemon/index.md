# Philemon — Matthew Henry Concise Commentary

# Contents

* [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/philemon/chapter-1|Philemon 1 | Read the Matthew Henry Concise Bible Commentary Online]] - In this chapter: The apostle's joy and praise for Philemon's steady faith in the Lord Jesus, and love to all the saints. (1–7). He recommends Onesimus as one who would make rich amends for the misconduct of which he had been guilty; and on behalf of whom the apostle promises to make up any loss Philemon had sustained. (8–22). Salutations and a blessing. (23–25).
