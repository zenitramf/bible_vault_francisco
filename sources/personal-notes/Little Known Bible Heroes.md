## Little Known Bible Heroes

- [ ] **Jochebed** — *Exodus 2:1-10* — Moses' mother who hid and saved him from Pharaoh's decree.
- [x] **Naaman's wife's handmaiden** — *2 Kings 5:1-4* — A captured girl who pointed Naaman to the prophet Elisha.
- [x] **Dorcas (Tabitha)** — *Acts 9:36-42* — A generous disciple known for caring for widows and the poor.
- [x] **Rhoda** — *Acts 12:12-17* — The servant girl who recognized Peter's voice and testified that he was at the door.
- [ ] **Ebed-Melech** — *Jeremiah 38:7-13; 39:15-18* — An Ethiopian official who rescued Jeremiah from the cistern.
- [ ] **Huldah** — *2 Kings 22:14-20; 2 Chronicles 34:22-28* — A prophetess who confirmed God's word during King Josiah's reforms.
- [x] **Shiphrah/Puah** — *Exodus 1:15-21* — Another faithful midwife who protected Hebrew infants.
- [ ] **Obed-Edom** — *2 Samuel 6:10-12; 1 Chronicles 13:13-14* — He hosted the ark of the covenant and was blessed.
- [ ] **Barzillai** — *2 Samuel 17:27-29; 19:31-39* — An older supporter who provided for David during exile.
- [ ] **Abiathar** — *1 Samuel 22:20-23; 1 Kings 1:7-8* — A priest who escaped massacre and served David.
- [ ] **Ittai the Gittite** — *2 Samuel 15:19-22* — A foreign warrior who chose loyalty to David.
- [x] **Shunammite woman** — *2 Kings 4:8-37; 8:1-6* — She showed hospitality to Elisha and trusted God in grief.
- [ ] **Widow of Zarephath** — *1 Kings 17:8-16* — She shared her last meal with Elijah and saw God's provision.
- [ ] **Widow with two mites** — *Mark 12:41-44; Luke 21:1-4* — She gave all she had, and Jesus praised her sacrifice.
- [ ] **Phoebe** — *Romans 16:1-2* — A servant and benefactor who supported the church.
- [ ] **Priscilla** — *Acts 18:24-26; Romans 16:3-5* — She helped instruct Apollos more accurately in the faith.
- [ ] **Aquila** — *Acts 18:1-3, 24-26; Romans 16:3-5* — A tentmaker and ministry partner of Paul.
- [ ] **Epaphras** — *Colossians 1:7-8; 4:12-13* — A faithful prayer warrior and worker for the Colossians.
- [ ] **Joseph of Arimathea** — *Matthew 27:57-60; John 19:38-42* — He courageously provided Jesus' burial tomb.
