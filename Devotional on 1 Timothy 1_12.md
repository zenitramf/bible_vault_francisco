# 1 Timothy 1:12

> *1 Timothy 1:12 — And I thank Christ Jesus our Lord, who hath enabled me, for that he counted me faithful, putting me into the ministry;*

## The Focus of our Ministry

> "And I thank God..."

- Christ is worthy of our service, of our time, of our resources, of our energies. (Rev. 5:12)

> *Revelation 5:12 — Saying with a loud voice, Worthy is the Lamb that was slain to receive power, and riches, and wisdom, and strength, and honour, and glory, and blessing.*


- Our focus is Gospel-focused. We do not have ministries for the sake of having a ministry, but that the gospel would be disseminated, that by discipleship and fellowship we should encourage the brethren and reach others for Christ. (Phil. 2:11)

> *Philippians 2:11 — And that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.*

- **Anything else is "sinking sand" if we do not let Christ have the pre-eminence in our ministries.**
## The Force of our Ministry

> "Who hath enabled me."

- Our sufficiency for work in our ministries comes from Christ. (2 Cor. 3:5)

> *2 Corinthians 3:5 — Not that we are sufficient of ourselves to think any thing as of ourselves; but our sufficiency is of God;*

- Our weakness shows Christ's power. (2 Cor. 12:9-10)

> *2 Corinthians 12:9 — And he said unto me, My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness. Most gladly therefore will I rather glory in my infirmities, that the power of Christ may rest upon me.*

> *2 Corinthians 12:10 — Therefore I take pleasure in infirmities, in reproaches, in necessities, in persecutions, in distresses for Christ’s sake: for when I am weak, then am I strong.*


## The Fortitude of our Ministry

> "For that he counted..."

- With every ministry there is a mandate for faithfulness.
- These ministries have been "committed to our trust." (1 Tim. 1:11)

> *1 Timothy 1:11 — According to the glorious gospel of the blessed God, which was committed to my trust.*

- God has placed us into our ministries — not Pastor.
- We have been "counted" — or "reckoned" or "charged" — as faithful.
  - This is not only an accounting of our faithfulness in the small things, but also an attribution, or an expectation for our future conduct.

---

## The Take-away

1. We are stewards of our ministries
2. As stewards, we must be counted faithful with our Master's resources (time, talents, money)
3. Christ is our Enabler, and the source of Strength for our work.
