# Church

## Summary

The sources present the church as one body animated by one Spirit, called to unity and built up by Christ's gifts. Local gatherings of even two or three in Christ's name have his promised presence. Romans 12–16 (Henry Concise) adds living-sacrifice identity, diverse gifts in one body, mutual forbearance of weak and strong, practical care for co-workers, and vigilance against dividers who depart from sound doctrine.

## Core claims

- There is one body and one Spirit, one Lord, one faith, one baptism, and one God and Father of all; therefore Christians must endeavor to keep the unity of the Spirit. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-6/ephesians/chapter-4#Exhortation to Unity; Persuasives to Unity (61 AD)|Matthew Henry on Ephesians 4]]
- Christ gives apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors and teachers for the perfecting of the saints, the work of ministry, and the edifying of the body of Christ. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-6/ephesians/chapter-4#Exhortation to Unity; Persuasives to Unity (61 AD)|Matthew Henry on Ephesians 4]]
- Where two or three are gathered in Christ's name, he is in the midst of them, sanctioning church and prayer meetings. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-sermons/volume-30/sermon_1761|Spurgeon, The Lord with Two or Three]]
- The house of God is the church of the living God, the pillar and ground of the truth. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-6/1-timothy/chapter-3#The Mystery of Godliness (64 AD)|Matthew Henry on 1 Timothy 3]]
- All the saints make up one body in Christ with differing gifts to be used humbly and faithfully. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-12#Verses 3–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 12]]
- Weak and strong believers must not despise or judge one another over indifferent matters; peace and edification govern liberty. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-14#Verses 1–6|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 14]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-14#Verses 19–23|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 14]]
- Christians should help one another in practical affairs; named co-workers and helpers (such as Phoebe) matter to the body’s life. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-16#Verses 1–16|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 16]]
- Whatever differs from the sound doctrine of the Scriptures opens a door to divisions; the church must be wise against seducers. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-16#Verses 17–20|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 16]]

## Agreements and tensions

Phase 4 full-corpus volume/month source-notes are linked under Related pages for inventory coverage; doctrinal claims remain grounded in prior multi-source synthesis (no false consensus).

- Henry emphasizes structural unity and gifted ministry for maturation of the body. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-6/ephesians/chapter-4#Exhortation to Unity; Persuasives to Unity (61 AD)|Matthew Henry on Ephesians 4]]
- Spurgeon highlights the sufficiency of Christ's presence with small assemblies, not only large institutions. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-sermons/volume-30/sermon_1761|Spurgeon, The Lord with Two or Three]]
- Romans concise stresses concrete relational ethics (weak/strong, co-workers, anti-division) more than formal polity.
- Local/catholic church relations and ordinances still need later sources.
