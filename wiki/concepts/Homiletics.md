# Homiletics

## Summary

Homiletics here is the charge and posture of *preaching the Word*—public ministry that feeds, warns, and builds up under Christ—distinct from [[wiki/concepts/Pastoral Ministry|Pastoral Ministry]] (office, qualifications, flock oversight) and from [[wiki/concepts/Christ-Centered Ministry|Christ-Centered Ministry]] (call, sufficiency, focus). It depends on [[wiki/concepts/Hermeneutics|Hermeneutics]]: what is preached should not outrun careful reading. Seed synthesis joins McGee’s “Preach the word” rallying cry and Scripture-equipped man of God, Henry and Spurgeon on feeding the flock, Gómez on feed-and-warn duty, Nehemiah 8 on reading and expounding so people understand, and Spurgeon’s edges of privilege-with-humility and gospel fidelity (no dressed-up other gospel).

## Core claims

- “Preach the word” is the last-days rallying cry: be ready; convict, rebuke, and exhort with long-suffering and teaching (2 Tim 4:2). [[sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/1-2_Timothy-Titus-Philemon.pdf.extract.md|McGee Notes & Outlines on Pastoral Epistles]] [[wiki/source-notes/McGee Notes and Outlines on Pastoral Epistles and Philemon|McGee Pastoral Epistles source-note]] [[wiki/passages/2 Timothy 4|2 Timothy 4]]
- People turn from searching, plain preaching to fables when they will not endure the plain gospel of Christ. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-timothy/chapter-4#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Timothy 4]]
- In days of apostasy the Scriptures alone fit the man of God: every passage is God-breathed and profitable for teaching, conviction, setting right, and discipline so he is complete for every good work (2 Tim 3:16–17). [[sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/1-2_Timothy-Titus-Philemon.pdf.extract.md|McGee Notes & Outlines on Pastoral Epistles]] [[wiki/concepts/Word of God|Word of God]]
- Elders are to feed the flock of God, taking oversight willingly, not for filthy lucre, and not as lords over God’s heritage but as ensamples. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-6/1-peter/chapter-5#Advice to Elders (66 AD)|Matthew Henry on 1 Peter 5]] [[wiki/passages/1 Peter 5|1 Peter 5]]
- McGee on 1 Peter 5: the elder’s business is to feed the flock willingly and not for money; the leader is an example; the Chief Shepherd will reward his own. [[sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/1_Peter.pdf.extract.md|McGee Notes & Outlines on 1 Peter]] [[wiki/source-notes/McGee Notes and Outlines on 1 Peter|McGee source-note]]
- Christ’s charge “Feed my sheep” turns fishers into shepherds who must love and feed the flock—ministry of the Word is flock-care, not platform-building. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-sermons/volume-56/sermon_3211|Spurgeon, Feed My Sheep]]
- Personal pastoral teaching presents sheep as dependent and easily lost, and pastors as responsible to feed, warn, and guide for the church’s perfection under Ephesians 4:11–16. [[sources/personal-notes/THE PASTOR AND HIS CHURCH - Pastor Andrés Gómez#La condición de las ovejas|Pastor Andrés Gómez notes]] [[sources/personal-notes/THE PASTOR AND HIS CHURCH - Pastor Andrés Gómez#Las responsabilidades de un pastor|Pastor Andrés Gómez notes]]
- Paul counted it great privilege to preach the gospel, not drudgery; yet success humbled him—the fuller the vessel, the deeper it sinks. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-mae/march/march-2|Spurgeon Morning and Evening, March 2]]
- Nehemiah 8 joins public reading and expounding: praying and preaching are services of religion; the law is made plain so hearers understand. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/nehemiah/chapter-8#Verses 1–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Nehemiah 8]] [[wiki/passages/Nehemiah 8|Nehemiah 8]]
- God’s altar of unhewn stones forbids human skill dressing the cross into another gospel congenial to fallen taste—content fidelity bounds homiletic creativity. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-mae/july/july-14|Spurgeon Morning and Evening, July 14]]

## Agreements and tensions

- Spurgeon *Morning and Evening* is brief and applicative: pastoral color, not a full homiletic textbook.
- McGee frames “Preach the word” inside a last-days/apostasy program; Henry stresses plain, searching preaching against itching ears more generally—keep both without forcing a single eschatological system onto every sermon charge.
- Feeding claims overlap [[wiki/concepts/Pastoral Ministry|Pastoral Ministry]]; this page owns the *ministry of the Word* (charge, aim, expounding, gospel fidelity), not elder qualification grids or Diotrephes polity warnings.
- Homiletics depends on [[wiki/concepts/Hermeneutics|Hermeneutics]] and the living authority of [[wiki/concepts/Word of God|Word of God]]; do not treat sermon craft as independent of text and Spirit.
- Do not invent outline templates, lectionary systems, or named preaching schools beyond what sources assert.

## Biblical passages

- 2 Timothy 4:1–5 (primary charge)
- 2 Timothy 3:16–17 (equipment of the man of God)
- 1 Peter 5:1–4 (feed the flock)

- Nehemiah 8 (read and expound)
- Ephesians 4:11–16 (gifts for edification)
