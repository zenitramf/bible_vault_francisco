# Prayer

## Summary

The retrieved sources present prayer as sincere address to God rather than public performance, taught by Christ in a pattern of childlike dependence, and sustained by the Spirit's help in weakness. Christ's own intercession in John 17 stands as both sample and ground of Christian praying. Prayer therefore belongs to ordinary discipleship and to the mediatorial work of Christ and the Spirit.

## Core claims

- Prayer must be free of hypocrisy: not practiced to be seen by people, but offered secretly to the Father who sees in secret. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-6#Verses 5–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 6]]
- Christ teaches how to pray, centering address on the Father and joining petition with forgiveness of others. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-6#Verses 9–15|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 6]]
- John 17 is peculiarly Christ's prayer as Mediator, a specimen of his intercession, yet useful for instruction and encouragement in prayer. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-5/john/chapter-17#Christ's Intercessory Prayer|Matthew Henry on John 17]]
- Believers often do not know what to pray for as they ought; the Spirit helps their infirmities and intercedes with groanings that cannot be uttered. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-sermons/volume-26/sermon_1532|Spurgeon, The Holy Spirit's Intercession]]

## Agreements and tensions

- Henry and Spurgeon agree that Christian prayer depends on divine help rather than human eloquence.
- Henry distinguishes the Lord's Prayer taught to disciples from Christ's unique mediatorial prayer; the two are related but not identical. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-5/john/chapter-17#Christ's Intercessory Prayer|Matthew Henry on John 17]]
- Spurgeon's Romans 8 treatment emphasizes the Spirit's interior aid in weakness more than the form and pattern of petition. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-sermons/volume-26/sermon_1532|Spurgeon, The Holy Spirit's Intercession]]

## Biblical passages

Key loci: Matthew 6:5–15; John 17; Romans 8:26–27.
