# Christ-Centered Ministry

## Summary

Christ-centered ministry is Christ's work before it is the minister's career: he puts men into the ministry, fits them for it, and counts them faithful with a trust. The personal vault devotional on 1 Timothy 1:12 organizes the same truth as focus (Christ's worth and gospel), force (Christ's enablement in weakness), and fortitude (stewardship and faithfulness). Henry's 1 Timothy 1 and 2 Corinthians expositions supply the commentary spine; Spurgeon's "Feed My Sheep" and the church/pastoral theme batch keep flock-care under Christ rather than institutional self-promotion.

## Core claims

- It is Christ's work to put men into the ministry; those he puts in he fits for it, and he gives not only ability but fidelity—he counted Paul faithful when putting him into the ministry. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-6/1-timothy/chapter-1|Matthew Henry Complete on 1 Timothy 1]]
- A call to the ministry is a great favor for which those called ought to thank Jesus Christ. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-6/1-timothy/chapter-1|Matthew Henry Complete on 1 Timothy 1]]
- Christ is worthy of the minister's time, strength, and resources; ministry must retain his preeminence and remain gospel-focused rather than existing for its own institutional sake. [[sources/personal-notes/Devotional on 1 Timothy 1_12#The Focus of our Ministry|Devotional on 1 Timothy 1:12]]
- Ministers are not sufficient of themselves to think anything as of themselves; all sufficiency is of God, to whom the praise of every good work belongs. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-6/2-corinthians/chapter-3|Matthew Henry Complete on 2 Corinthians 3]] [[sources/personal-notes/Devotional on 1 Timothy 1_12#The Force of our Ministry|Devotional on 1 Timothy 1:12]]
- God's strength is made perfect in weakness; when grace is sufficient, the minister has no reason to complain if the thorn remains. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-corinthians/chapter-12|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Corinthians 12]] [[sources/personal-notes/Devotional on 1 Timothy 1_12#The Force of our Ministry|Devotional on 1 Timothy 1:12]]
- Ministry is a stewardship committed to trust; the minister must be counted faithful with the Master's resources. [[sources/personal-notes/Devotional on 1 Timothy 1_12#The Fortitude of our Ministry|Devotional on 1 Timothy 1:12]] [[sources/personal-notes/Devotional on 1 Timothy 1_12#The Take-away|Devotional on 1 Timothy 1:12]]
- Christ's charge "Feed my sheep" turns fishers into shepherds who must love and feed the flock—ministry centered on Christ is still flock-care, not platform-building. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-sermons/volume-56/sermon_3211|Spurgeon, Feed My Sheep]]

## Agreements and tensions

- The personal devotional, Henry, and Spurgeon agree that ministry is Christ-initiated, Christ-enabled, and accountable under Christ rather than self-sufficient professionalism.
- Henry stresses formal calling language (Christ puts men into the ministry and qualifies them); the personal note stresses practical focus/force/fortitude for local ministers; Spurgeon stresses loving pastoral feeding. These emphases complement rather than cancel one another.
- Christ-Centered Ministry overlaps [[wiki/concepts/Pastoral Ministry|Pastoral Ministry]] (office, qualifications, flock) and [[wiki/concepts/Stewardship|Stewardship]] (entrusted goods and self-giving) without replacing either: this page holds the *Christ-focus and Christ-enablement* axis.
- This page does not yet settle ordination polity, pay, or spiritual-gift inventories; open those only as sources allow.
