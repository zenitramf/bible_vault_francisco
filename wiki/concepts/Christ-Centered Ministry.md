# Christ-Centered Ministry

## Summary

Christ-centered ministry takes Christ's worth and gospel as its purpose, depends upon Christ for strength, and receives ministry as a stewardship requiring faithfulness. These three emphases guard against treating ministry as self-promotion, mere institutional activity, or a work sustained by human adequacy alone.

## Core claims

- The aim of ministry is the honor of Christ and the spread of the gospel. [[wiki/source-notes/Devotional on 1 Timothy 1 12#Core claims|Source note]]
- The power for ministry is received from Christ rather than generated by natural sufficiency. [[wiki/source-notes/Devotional on 1 Timothy 1 12#Core claims|Source note]]
- The minister is a steward who must be faithful with entrusted responsibilities and resources. [[wiki/source-notes/Devotional on 1 Timothy 1 12#Core claims|Source note]]
