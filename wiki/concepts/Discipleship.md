# Discipleship

## Summary

In the retrieved sources, a disciple is one who comes after Christ. Discipleship requires self-denial, taking up the cross, and following Jesus rather than prescribing to him. Spurgeon presses the severity of the terms: Christ must outrank even closest natural loves, and no one should begin without counting the cost of finishing.

## Core claims

- If anyone will come after Christ, he must deny himself, take up his cross, and follow him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-5/matthew/chapter-16#The Value of the Soul|Matthew Henry on Matthew 16]]
- A true disciple follows Christ in duty and shall follow him to glory; he does not prescribe the way to Christ. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-5/matthew/chapter-16#The Value of the Soul|Matthew Henry on Matthew 16]]
- The cross stands for providential afflictions and persecutions for righteousness' sake that the disciple must bear. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-5/matthew/chapter-16#The Value of the Soul|Matthew Henry on Matthew 16]]
- Christ requires supreme love: natural attachments must not rival allegiance to him. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-sermons/volume-45/sermon_2650|Spurgeon, Characteristics of Christ's Disciples]]
- Would-be disciples must count the cost lest they begin and be unable to finish. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-sermons/volume-20/sermon_1159|Spurgeon, Counting the Cost]]
- Matthew's concise exposition marks self-denial as necessary after Peter's confession and Christ's prediction of suffering. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-16#Verses 24–28|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 16]]

## Agreements and tensions

- Henry and Spurgeon agree that discipleship is costly and Christ-centered rather than casual association.
- Spurgeon's Luke 14 material is especially severe on rival loves and unfinished commitment; Henry's Matthew 16 treatment balances self-denial with the value of the soul and life lost/found. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-sermons/volume-45/sermon_2650|Spurgeon, Characteristics of Christ's Disciples]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-5/matthew/chapter-16#The Value of the Soul|Matthew Henry on Matthew 16]]
- This seed does not yet develop formation practices (teaching, baptism, community) beyond the call to follow.
