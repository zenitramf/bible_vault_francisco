# Discipleship

## Summary

In the retrieved sources, a disciple is one who comes after Christ. Discipleship requires self-denial, taking up the cross, and following Jesus rather than prescribing to him. Spurgeon presses the severity of the terms: Christ must outrank even closest natural loves, and no one should begin without counting the cost of finishing. Matthew’s concise atlas adds Sermon-on-the-Mount formation, mission among wolves, childlike greatness, and the risen commission to make disciples of all nations. Romans 12 frames lifelong offering; personal notes add study habits and hidden faithfulness.

## Core claims

- If anyone will come after Christ, he must deny himself, take up his cross, and follow him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-5/matthew/chapter-16#The Value of the Soul|Matthew Henry on Matthew 16]]
- A true disciple follows Christ in duty and shall follow him to glory; he does not prescribe the way to Christ. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-5/matthew/chapter-16#The Value of the Soul|Matthew Henry on Matthew 16]]
- The cross stands for providential afflictions and persecutions for righteousness' sake that the disciple must bear. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-5/matthew/chapter-16#The Value of the Soul|Matthew Henry on Matthew 16]]
- Christ requires supreme love: natural attachments must not rival allegiance to him. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-sermons/volume-45/sermon_2650|Spurgeon, Characteristics of Christ's Disciples]]
- Would-be disciples must count the cost lest they begin and be unable to finish. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-sermons/volume-20/sermon_1159|Spurgeon, Counting the Cost]]
- Matthew's concise exposition marks self-denial as necessary after Peter's confession and Christ's prediction of suffering. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-16#Verses 24–28|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 16]]
- Disciples are salt and light; they must do the word, not merely hear it, if their house is to stand. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-5#Verses 13–16|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 5]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-7#Verses 21–29|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 7]]
- Sent ones go as sheep among wolves; they fear God more than men and confess Christ before men. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-10#Verses 16–42|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 10]]
- The risen Christ commissions disciples to make disciples of all nations, baptizing and teaching, under his authority and presence. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-28#Verses 16–20|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 28]]
- By the mercies of God, believers present their bodies a living sacrifice and are transformed by renewing of the mind rather than conformity to the world. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-12#Verses 1, 2|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 12]]
- Disciplined study asks contextual questions: when written, to whom, and by whom. [[sources/personal-notes/Ayudas en el estudio biblico#El contexto del pasaje|Ayudas en el estudio bíblico]]
- Hidden faithfulness in lesser-known biblical servants models ordinary discipleship. [[sources/personal-notes/Little Known Bible Heroes|Little Known Bible Heroes]]

## Agreements and tensions

Phase 4 full-corpus volume/month source-notes are linked under Related pages for inventory coverage; doctrinal claims remain grounded in prior multi-source synthesis (no false consensus).

- Henry and Spurgeon agree that discipleship is costly and Christ-centered rather than casual association.
- Spurgeon's Luke 14 material is especially severe on rival loves and unfinished commitment; Henry's Matthew 16 treatment balances self-denial with the value of the soul and life lost/found. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-sermons/volume-45/sermon_2650|Spurgeon, Characteristics of Christ's Disciples]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-5/matthew/chapter-16#The Value of the Soul|Matthew Henry on Matthew 16]]
- Romans 12 shifts from entrance terms (cross) to lifelong offering (body, mind, gifts)—same discipleship, different angle.
- Formation practices (baptism, community, teaching) are begun in Romans 6/12 links but still need fuller development.
