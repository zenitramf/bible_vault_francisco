# Concepts

Live database view: [[wiki/concepts/index.base|Folder base]]. Static OKF list remains below.

# Contents

* [[wiki/concepts/Atonement|Atonement]] - Christ's propitiatory, substitutionary work dealing with guilt—the gospel crux beside redemption as price-paid deliverance (Rom 3; Isa 53; Mt 27).
* [[wiki/concepts/Christ|Christ]] - Christ as the eternal Word made flesh, God with us, and the suffering Messiah who bears iniquity (Jn 1; Mt 1; Isa 53).
* [[wiki/concepts/Christ-Centered Ministry|Christ-Centered Ministry]] - Ministry put into men by Christ, focused on his worth and gospel, enabled by his sufficiency, and exercised as faithful stewardship (1 Tim 1:12; 2 Cor 3:5; 12:9–10).
* [[wiki/concepts/Church|Church]] - The church as one body in Christ, gifted for edification, and gathered in his name with his presence (Eph 4; Mt 18).
* [[wiki/concepts/Covenant|Covenant]] - God-initiated relationship sealed by promise and obligation, with Christ as mediator of the new covenant (Gen 15–17; Jer 31; Heb 8).
* [[wiki/concepts/Creation|Creation]] - God's free, ordered work by word and Spirit, establishing his ownership and humanity in his image (Gen 1).
* [[wiki/concepts/Discipleship|Discipleship]] - Following Christ through self-denial, cross-bearing, and counted cost (Mt 16:24–26; Lk 14:26–30).
* [[wiki/concepts/Faith|Faith]] - Trust in God's promise and in Christ; the means of justification and the principle of the life that pleases God (Gen 15:6; Rom 4; Heb 11).
* [[wiki/concepts/Grace|Grace]] - God's free, undeserved favor in Christ that grounds salvation, justification, and good works without purchasing merit (Rom 3–5; Eph 2).
* [[wiki/concepts/Holiness|Holiness]] - Conformity to the holy God who calls and sanctifies his people through truth (1 Pet 1; Jn 17:17; Heb 10:14).
* [[wiki/concepts/Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]] - The promised Comforter who indwells, convicts, guides into truth, and empowers the church (Jn 14–16; Acts 2; Rom 8:26).
* [[wiki/concepts/Hope|Hope]] - Confident expectation of glory, resurrection, and God's promise under present suffering—not optimism but faith-forward waiting (Rom 5; Rom 8; 1 Cor 15).
* [[wiki/concepts/Intercession|Intercession]] - Spirit-helped prayer within believers and Christ's mediatorial prayer for his people (Rom 8:26–27; Jn 17).
* [[wiki/concepts/Justice|Justice]] - God's righteous requirement to do justly, love mercy, and defend the oppressed (Mic 6:6–8; Ps 9).
* [[wiki/concepts/Justification|Justification]] - God's free declaration that sinners are righteous by grace through faith in Christ, not by works of the law (Rom 3–5).
* [[wiki/concepts/Pastoral Ministry|Pastoral Ministry]] - Qualified oversight and feeding of Christ's flock for the church's edification (1 Tim 3; 1 Pet 5; Jn 21:16).
* [[wiki/concepts/Prayer|Prayer]] - Humble approach to the Father, taught by Christ, helped by the Spirit, and perfected in Christ's intercession (Mt 6:5–15; Jn 17; Rom 8:26–27).
* [[wiki/concepts/Prophecy|Prophecy]] - God's word spoken through holy messengers, tested against false prophecy, and used for church edification (2 Pet 1; Jer 23; 1 Cor 14).
* [[wiki/concepts/Redemption|Redemption]] - Price-paid deliverance through Christ, foundational for belonging to God and freedom from sin (1 Cor 6:19–20; Rom 3:24–26).
* [[wiki/concepts/Repentance|Repentance]] - Turning to God in contrite confession, pleading mercy through Christ's blood, not self-satisfaction for sin (Ps 51; Mt 3).
* [[wiki/concepts/Resurrection|Resurrection]] - Christ's rising from the dead as gospel foundation and the believer's assured future rising (1 Cor 15; gospel passion narratives).
* [[wiki/concepts/Salvation|Salvation]] - God's free grace in Christ, received by faith and not by works of the law (Acts 15:11; Rom 3–4; Eph 2).
* [[wiki/concepts/Sin and the Fall|Sin and the Fall]] - Humanity's fall through temptation and transgression, bringing misery, exclusion from Eden, and the need of the promised Seed (Gen 3; Rom 3; Rom 5).
* [[wiki/concepts/Spiritual Warfare|Spiritual Warfare]] - The Christian life as a real war against the devil, principalities and powers, fought with spiritual weapons and the whole armor of God (Eph 6:10–18; 1 Pe 5:8–9; Jas 4:7; Mt 4:1–11).
* [[wiki/concepts/Stewardship|Stewardship]] - Entrusted life, ministry, and goods—first giving oneself to the Lord, then faithful management and generous care for others (2 Cor 8; 1 Tim 1:12).
* [[wiki/concepts/Suffering|Suffering]] - Affliction under God's hand—permitted trial, present groan, and hope of glory; not proof of hypocrisy (Job 1; Rom 8).
* [[wiki/concepts/Surrender|Surrender]] - The whole-person yielding of the will to God — humiliation, acceptance, obedience, and Spirit-sensitivity — that the Bible presents as the only path to life and salvation (Jas 4:7; Ro 6:13; Mt 16:24–26; Ac 9:6).
* [[wiki/concepts/Wisdom|Wisdom]] - God-given skill for living that outranks all earthly wealth; in its highest form, the personal Wisdom who is Christ (Pr 16:16; Pr 3:14; Pr 8:10–11, 19; Jb 28; Ps 119:98–100; Col 2:3).
* [[wiki/concepts/Word of God|Word of God]] - Divine revelation as wisdom and rule for religion—published, plain, and to be studied in context (Pr 8; personal study method).
* [[wiki/concepts/Worship|Worship]] - Spirit-and-truth approach to the Father in praise, sanctuary service, and accepted offering (Jn 4:20–24; Ps 134).
