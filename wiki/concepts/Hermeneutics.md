# Hermeneutics

## Summary

Hermeneutics in this vault is the *method* of faithful reading—how Scripture is situated, opened, and understood—distinct from [[wiki/concepts/Word of God|Word of God]], which owns the *nature and authority* of revelation. Seed synthesis draws a short Spanish context checklist, McGee’s restraint against private/isolated prophecy, Spurgeon’s Emmaus and diligence edges (Christ opens Scripture and understanding; search with Spirit-candled care; Christ is the Bible’s Alpha and Omega), and Nehemiah 8’s public reading with sense given so people understand. Exegesis here is the practical twin of these principles: draw meaning from the text in its setting before leaping to application. What is rightly read then feeds [[wiki/concepts/Homiletics|Homiletics]].

## Core claims

- When studying a passage, ask when it was written. [[sources/personal-notes/Ayudas en el estudio biblico#El contexto del pasaje|Ayudas en el estudio bíblico]] [[wiki/source-notes/Ayudas en el estudio biblico|Ayudas source-note]]
- Ask to whom it was written (the audience). [[sources/personal-notes/Ayudas en el estudio biblico#El contexto del pasaje|Ayudas en el estudio bíblico]]
- Ask who wrote it. [[sources/personal-notes/Ayudas en el estudio biblico#El contexto del pasaje|Ayudas en el estudio bíblico]]
- Disciplined context questions belong *before* application—not as a substitute for the text’s authority, but so meaning is not invented from need alone. [[sources/personal-notes/Ayudas en el estudio biblico#El contexto del pasaje|Ayudas en el estudio bíblico]] [[wiki/source-notes/Ayudas en el estudio biblico|Ayudas source-note]]
- McGee on 2 Peter: a more sure prophetic word outranks even Transfiguration eyewitness; no prophecy is interpreted in isolation; holy men were carried by the Spirit like a sailboat by the wind. [[sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/2_Peter.pdf.extract.md|McGee Notes & Outlines on 2 Peter]] [[wiki/source-notes/McGee Notes and Outlines on 2 Peter|McGee 2 Peter source-note]] [[wiki/passages/2 Peter 1|2 Peter 1]]
- On the Emmaus road the risen Christ opened the Scriptures concerning himself—the best tutor makes the Word yield Christ; disciples need him to interpret the written word. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-mae/january/january-18|Spurgeon Morning and Evening, January 18]] [[wiki/passages/Luke 24|Luke 24]]
- He who opens Scripture also opens the understanding; illumination is Christ’s work so that the mind grasps what is written. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-mae/january/january-19|Spurgeon Morning and Evening, January 19]]
- Seek a more intelligent understanding of Scripture so as to teach others and resist every wind of doctrine; constantly ask the Spirit’s teaching as Author of the Word. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-mae/february/february-21|Spurgeon Morning and Evening, February 21]]
- Search the Scriptures with gold-hunter diligence—not a superficial chapter skim but Spirit-candled, close, curious enquiry. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-mae/june/june-9|Spurgeon Morning and Evening, June 9]]
- Jesus Christ is the Alpha and Omega of the Bible; from creation and the woman’s seed through types to the end, the pages testify of him. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-mae/june/june-10|Spurgeon Morning and Evening, June 10]]
- In Nehemiah 8 the law is read and expounded so the people understand; those who diligently search find things written which they had forgotten. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/nehemiah/chapter-8#Verses 1–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Nehemiah 8]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/nehemiah/chapter-8#Verses 13–18|Matthew Henry Concise on Nehemiah 8]] [[wiki/passages/Nehemiah 8|Nehemiah 8]]

## Agreements and tensions

- Ayudas is a seed checklist only; it does not yet develop literary genre, canonical context, or a full Christ-centered reading grid. It complements commentary rather than replacing it.
- Spurgeon *Morning and Evening* is brief and applicative: use for experiential edges of illumination and Christ-centered reading; do not overweight against fuller Henry/sermon treatments of the same doctrines.
- McGee’s “not in isolation” and Spirit-carried writers protect against private prophecy games; hold with Word of God’s bibliology of the more sure word without inventing a full critical method.
- Nehemiah 8 bridges reading and public expounding—hermeneutics of sense-giving feeds [[wiki/concepts/Homiletics|Homiletics]] without collapsing the two pages.
- Do not invent genre systems, textual-criticism programs, or named school labels (e.g. redemptive-historical vs allegorical) beyond what sources assert.
