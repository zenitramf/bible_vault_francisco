# Holiness

## Summary

In the retrieved sources, holiness is not optional polish for advanced believers but the moral and spiritual calling of those elected and redeemed. God who is holy requires a holy manner of life; Christ prays for his people's sanctification through the truth; the gospel that justifies also aims at a purified people.

## Core claims

- Those called by a holy God are commanded to be holy in all manner of conversation, because it is written, "Be ye holy; for I am holy." [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-6/1-peter/chapter-1#Sobriety and Holiness; Exhortation to Brotherly Love (66 AD)|Matthew Henry on 1 Peter 1]]
- Election is through sanctification of the Spirit unto obedience and sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-6/1-peter/chapter-1#Inscription (66 AD)|Matthew Henry on 1 Peter 1]]
- Christ's high-priestly prayer asks the Father to sanctify his people through the truth; the word is truth. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-sermons/volume-32/sermon_1890|Spurgeon, Our Lord's Prayer for His People's Sanctification]]
- Sanctification is so valuable that it occupies a central place in Christ's intercession. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-sermons/volume-32/sermon_1890|Spurgeon, Our Lord's Prayer for His People's Sanctification]]
- By one offering Christ has perfected forever those who are sanctified, joining finished atonement with sanctified people. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-sermons/volume-5/sermon-232|Spurgeon, Perfection in Faith]]

## Agreements and tensions

- Henry stresses the ethical demand flowing from God's own holiness and from costly redemption. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-6/1-peter/chapter-1#Sobriety and Holiness; Exhortation to Brotherly Love (66 AD)|Matthew Henry on 1 Peter 1]]
- Spurgeon emphasizes Christ's prayer and the sanctifying instrumentality of truth/word, and also the once-for-all perfecting of the sanctified in Hebrews 10:14. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-sermons/volume-32/sermon_1890|Spurgeon, Our Lord's Prayer for His People's Sanctification]] [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-sermons/volume-5/sermon-232|Spurgeon, Perfection in Faith]]
- The relation between positional perfection and progressive purification is present in the sources but not fully systematized on this seed page.

## Biblical passages

Key loci: 1 Peter 1:13–21; John 17:17; Hebrews 10:14.
