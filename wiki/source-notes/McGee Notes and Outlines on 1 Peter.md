# McGee Notes and Outlines on 1 Peter

## Summary

Intentional ingest of **J. Vernon McGee, Thru the Bible Notes & Outlines** on 1 Peter (immutable PDF under `sources/personal-notes/ingest/`; original raw path `raw/1_Peter.pdf`). McGee presents Simon Peter as writer (1 Peter 1:1), dates the letter about A.D. 64 (2 Peter a short time later; martyrdom within A.D. 64–67), and argues a **literal Babylon** origin (1 Peter 5:13) against the Rome-as-Babylon reading. Theme is **Christian hope in the time of trial**—suffering (with cognates, sixteen times) tied to hope; Peter is called the apostle of hope. Outline moves through security and joy (1:1–9), Scriptures and holiness (1:10–25), Christ’s suffering producing separation, conduct, and obedience (chs. 2–4), and the Second Coming producing service, humility, and patience (ch. 5). High-quality pdftotext extract supports book-level synthesis.

## Core claims

- Writer Simon Peter (1:1); the epistles show a transformed Peter—once impetuous, now patient—through the gospel’s power. Date about A.D. 64 (within 64–67). [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_Peter.pdf|McGee Notes & Outlines on 1 Peter]]
- Place: Babylon (5:13) taken literally; east-to-west address list (1:1), direct style, and pre-Revelation timing argue against symbolic Rome; large Jewish colony in ancient Babylon fits the theme of trial under Nero-era pressure. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_Peter.pdf|McGee on place of writing]]
- Theme: Christian hope in the time of trial. Great gospel words open the letter (1:2)—elect, foreknowledge, sanctification, obedience, blood, Trinity—plus salvation, revelation, glory, faith, and hope; suffering is the word that carries the theme. Peter apostle of hope; Paul of faith; John of love. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_Peter.pdf|McGee on theme]]
- Outline: (I) Suffering and security of believers produce joy (1:1–9); (II) Suffering and the Scriptures produce holiness (1:10–25); (III) Suffering and the suffering of Christ (chs. 2–4)—separation (2), Christian conduct (3), obedience to God’s will (4); (IV) Suffering and the Second Coming (ch. 5)—service and hope (5:1–4), humility and patience (5:5–14). Alternate outline: rejoicing, holiness, fellowship, glory, others. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_Peter.pdf|McGee outline]]
- 1:1–2: Addressed to elect sojourners (Jews out of Palestine in Asia Minor). Trinity in salvation—Father’s foreknowledge/election, Spirit’s sanctification, Son’s blood and obedience; sprinkling pictures Passover blood on doorposts; grace and peace formula as in Paul. Test of election: Have you come? (John 6:37). [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_Peter.pdf|McGee on 1 Peter 1:1–2]]
- 1:3–5: Past—begotten again to a living hope by Christ’s resurrection; future—incorruptible, undefiled, unfading inheritance reserved/guarded in heaven; present—kept by the power of God. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_Peter.pdf|McGee on 1 Peter 1:3–5]]
- 1:6–9: Key verse cluster—rejoicing in trials; testing refines faith as furnace refines metal; “precious” used seven times; believers love Christ though they have not seen him as Peter did. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_Peter.pdf|McGee on 1 Peter 1:6–9]]
- 1:10–12: Prophets searched salvation; they saw suffering (grace) and glory (sovereignty) as two mountain peaks without the valley (church age) between Cross and Crown; apostles preached what angels desired to look into. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_Peter.pdf|McGee on 1 Peter 1:10–12]]
- 1:13–25: Hope from Scriptures; holiness as spiritual health/maturity—only holiness is in Christ; Judge motive for holy fear; redemption by Christ’s precious blood, not corruptible things—life must be worth the price; Word is living instrument of new birth; man’s frailty vs eternal Word (Psalm 119:89). Scofield-quoted order: omniscience → divine decision (foreordination/election/predestination) → foreknowledge, in eternal agreement. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_Peter.pdf|McGee on 1 Peter 1:13–25]]
- Ch. 2 separation: lay aside malice (congealed anger) and guile; desire pure spiritual milk; living Stone is Christ (Acts 4:11; Isa 28:16); believers living stones and a spiritual house/priesthood; Christ stepping-stone or stumbling stone; chosen generation, royal priesthood, holy nation, people for God’s own possession—to publish his virtues; honest life, civil submission, labor fairness; Christ’s vicarious suffering as example and for sins; “by whose stripes ye were healed” (Isa 53) is healing from sin, not primarily physical. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_Peter.pdf|McGee on 1 Peter 2]]
- Ch. 3 conduct: wives’ voluntary subjection may win unbelieving husbands by behavior, not outward adornment or sex appeal; husbands honor wives intelligently—prayers hindered when marriage is wrong; church: like-minded, tender, humble, no retaliation; ready to give a reason for hope; suffer for good not evil. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_Peter.pdf|McGee on 1 Peter 3:1–17]]
- 3:18–22: Christ suffered for sins once; preaching to spirits in prison was in **Noah’s day**—Noah preached; those rejecters’ spirits were in prison in Christ’s day; baptism is identification with Christ by resurrection; believers seated with him in heaven. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_Peter.pdf|McGee on 1 Peter 3:18–22]]
- Ch. 4: Suffering can restrain from sin; unbeliever’s old life left behind; coming of the Lord incentives sobriety, love, and Spirit-powered gifts; fiery trial is normal, not accidental—partaking of Christ’s sufferings; suffer as Christian not as sinner; judgment begins at God’s house (judgment seat); righteous saved only by faith in Christ; commit souls to faithful Creator. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_Peter.pdf|McGee on 1 Peter 4]]
- Ch. 5: Peter a fellow elder, not superior; elders feed the flock willingly, not for money—test of racket is self-spending; leaders as examples; Chief Shepherd’s crown; humility under God’s hand in view of Christ’s coming; cast anxiety on him; be sober/intelligent; resist the devil (cf. James 4:7); suffering is on God’s agenda; Babylon literal in closing greeting. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_Peter.pdf|McGee on 1 Peter 5]]

## Agreements and tensions

- Agrees with Henry Concise/Complete atlas on election and Trinity in 1:2, living hope and inheritance, holiness call, living stone and priesthood, household and civil conduct, Christ’s example in suffering, elder feeding without lordship, humility, and sober resistance of the devil ([[wiki/source-notes/Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Peter|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Peter]]).
- Adds sharper hope-in-trial theme word-counts, literal-Babylon provenance argument, Cross/Crown mountain-peak prophecy scheme, Passover-sprinkling and Isa 53 sin-healing readings, Noah-day spirits-in-prison view, baptism-as-identification, and elder “religious racket” money test—useful to [[wiki/concepts/Hope|Hope]], [[wiki/concepts/Suffering|Suffering]], [[wiki/concepts/Holiness|Holiness]], [[wiki/concepts/Christ|Christ]], [[wiki/concepts/Redemption|Redemption]], [[wiki/concepts/Word of God|Word of God]], [[wiki/concepts/Pastoral Ministry|Pastoral Ministry]], and [[wiki/concepts/Spiritual Warfare|Spiritual Warfare]].
- Literal Babylon vs Rome, Scofield election/foreknowledge logical order, and spirits-preached-in-Noah’s-day should be held beside other sources, not as the vault’s sole word.
- Corpus is single-author outline notes for Thru the Bible radio listeners, not full critical commentary. PDF sits under personal-notes ingest; optional promotion into `sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/` remains open (same pattern as other McGee notes).
