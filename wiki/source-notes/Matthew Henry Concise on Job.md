## Summary

Intentional ingest of **Matthew Henry Concise** on Job as a chapter-by-chapter backbone for the wiki passage atlas (42 chapters). Claims on each chapter page are drawn from the concise commentary headings and pastoral exposition. Part of campaign Phase 3.2 (OT remainder).
