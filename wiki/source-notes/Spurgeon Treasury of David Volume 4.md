# Spurgeon Treasury of David Volume 4

## Summary

Intentional **volume** ingest of Spurgeon's *Treasury of David* covering roughly Psalms 79–103 (25 source files in volume tree). This phase enriches existing [[wiki/source-notes/Matthew Henry Concise on Psalms|Matthew Henry Concise Psalms]] passage pages on hubs; it does not create one wiki page per verse.

## Core claims

- Title — A Psalm of Praise; or rather of thanksgiving. This is the only psalm bearing this precise inscription. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-tod/volume-4/psalm-100|Treasury of David, psalm-100]]
- This is just such a psalm as the man after God's own heart would compose when he was about to become king in Israel. It is David all over, straight forward, resolute, devout; there is no trace of policy or vacillation, the Lord has appointed him to be king, and he knows it,… [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-tod/volume-4/psalm-101|Treasury of David, psalm-101]]
- Title — A prayer of the afflicted, when he is overwhelmed, and poureth out his complaint before the Lord. This Psalm is a prayer far more in spirit than in words. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-tod/volume-4/psalm-102|Treasury of David, psalm-102]]

## Agreements and tensions
