# McGee Notes and Outlines on 2 Peter

## Summary

Intentional ingest of **J. Vernon McGee, Thru the Bible Notes & Outlines** on 2 Peter (immutable PDF under `sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/`; original raw path `raw/2_Peter.pdf`). McGee defends Simon Peter as writer (2 Peter 1:1) against the strongest NT authorship challenge, dates the letter about A.D. 66 shortly after 1 Peter and before martyrdom, and treats the epistle as Peter’s **swan song** parallel to Paul’s 2 Timothy—warning of coming apostasy and anchoring the church on the Scriptures. Characteristic word is **knowledge** (sixteen times with cognates); the epitome is grow in grace and in the knowledge of the Lord and Savior Jesus Christ (2 Peter 3:18). Outline moves through addition of graces and assurance (1:1–14), authority of Scripture by fulfilled prophecy (1:15–21), apostasy by false teachers (ch. 2), attitude toward the Lord’s return as a test of apostates (3:1–4), God’s agenda for past/present/future worlds (3:5–13), and admonition to believers (3:14–18). High-quality firecrawl-parse extract (13 pages) supports book-level synthesis.

## Core claims

- Writer Simon Peter (1:1); Petrine authorship once the most challenged NT book, now well established among conservatives; internal autobiographical evidence at 1:13–14, 16–18; 3:1. Date about A.D. 66, shortly after 1 Peter and before martyrdom (1:13–14). [[sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/2_Peter.pdf.extract.md|McGee Notes & Outlines on 2 Peter]]
- Theme: swan song like 2 Timothy—warning sign of apostasy (Peter warns of heresy among teachers; Paul among the laity); both speak joyfully of approaching death and anchor the church on the Scriptures. Characteristic word *knowledge* (sixteen times with cognates); true gnosticism is to know Jesus Christ as revealed in the Word (John 17:3), not esoteric formula or password. Epitome: grow in grace and knowledge of the Lord and Savior (3:18). [[sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/2_Peter.pdf.extract.md|McGee on theme]]
- Outline: (I) Addition of Christian graces gives assurance (1:1–14); (II) Authority of the Scriptures attested by fulfilled prophecy (1:15–21); (III) Apostasy brought in by false teachers (ch. 2); (IV) Attitude toward return of the Lord is a test of apostates (3:1–4); (V) Agenda of God for the world—past (3:5–6), present (3:7–12), future (3:13); (VI) Admonition to believers (3:14–18). [[sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/2_Peter.pdf.extract.md|McGee outline]]
- 1:1–14: “Simon” appears here (not in 1 Peter 1:1); Christ never stands alone—Jesus, Savior, Lord added. Blessings, precious promises, and partaking of the divine nature come through the knowledge of him. Graces (virtue/excellence with energy, knowledge, temperance, patience, godliness, brotherly kindness, charity) supplied in orderly sequence. Barren = idle; sterility may lead to forgetting one has been saved. Make calling and election *more* sure: security of the believer is objective; assurance of salvation is subjective. Peter stirs saints lest spiritual senility set in; tradition of crucifixion head-down (John 21:18–19). [[sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/2_Peter.pdf.extract.md|McGee on 2 Peter 1:1–14]]
- 1:15–21: “Decease” = exodus. Eyewitness of the Transfiguration (explains Matthew 16:28) is real, yet “a more sure word of prophecy” is more reliable; Scripture is light focused on the day star. No prophecy interpreted apart from other references to the same subject. Holy men carried along by the Holy Spirit like a sailboat by the wind. [[sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/2_Peter.pdf.extract.md|McGee on 2 Peter 1:15–21]]
- Ch. 2: False prophets brought heresy to Israel; false teachers bring it into the church—mark includes denying Christ’s redemption for them. False followers go after false teachers; God’s elect cannot be permanently deceived (1 Cor 11:19 on why cults are permitted). Covetousness another mark. Three past apostate types: (1) angels that sinned; (2) ungodly of Noah’s day; (3) Sodom and Gomorrah immorality. Lot was righteous though Genesis implies otherwise; God delivers the godly and keeps the unjust for judgment. Apostates like animals; way of Balaam is covetous sale of gift; head knowledge without love of the truth; “prodigal pig” (sow that was washed) vs prodigal son—see Jude. [[sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/2_Peter.pdf.extract.md|McGee on 2 Peter 2]]
- 3:1–4: Second letter confirmed; pure/sincere minds. Holy prophets (OT) and apostles (NT) warn of scoffers who ridicule Christ’s coming on the false premise that there has been no great cataclysm since creation. [[sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/2_Peter.pdf.extract.md|McGee on 2 Peter 3:1–4]]
- 3:5–13: Past world (creation to Noah) destroyed by water; present world (Noah to new heaven/earth) reserved for fire; future world never destroyed. Seeming delay is not postponement (3:8–9). Day of the Lord: *rhoizedon* noise; elements = basic material/atoms; melt = dissolve; fervent heat = energy—imagery of atomic explosion. Inevitable future makes believers’ lives serious; hastening unto the day. New heaven and new earth. [[sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/2_Peter.pdf.extract.md|McGee on 2 Peter 3:5–13]]
- 3:14–18: Leads to holy living; patient waiting is mental adjustment; Peter affirms Paul wrote of these things in depth; coming of Christ makes for stable steadfast life; grow in grace and knowledge—Paul said “Study,” Peter said “Grow”; both point to Bible study. [[sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/2_Peter.pdf.extract.md|McGee on 2 Peter 3:14–18]]

## Agreements and tensions

- Agrees with Henry Concise/Complete atlas on adding graces to faith, sure prophetic word and inspiration, warning against false teachers with OT judgment examples, scoffers at the coming, dissolution by fire, and holiness/growth admonition ([[wiki/source-notes/Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Peter|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Peter]]).
- Adds sharper knowledge-theme count, swan-song parallel to 2 Timothy, objective security vs subjective assurance distinction, Transfiguration-plus-more-sure-prophecy hierarchy, sailboat Spirit-inspiration image, false-teachers-vs-false-prophets church/Israel distinction, prodigal-pig apostate reading, three-worlds agenda, and atomic-explosion reading of 3:10–12—useful to [[wiki/concepts/Prophecy|Prophecy]], [[wiki/concepts/Word of God|Word of God]], [[wiki/concepts/Grace|Grace]], [[wiki/concepts/Salvation|Salvation]], [[wiki/concepts/Holiness|Holiness]], and [[wiki/concepts/Christ|Christ]].
- Atomic-explosion modern analogy, head-knowledge apostates returning like a washed sow, and security/assurance split should be held beside other sources, not as the vault’s sole word.
- Corpus is single-author outline notes for Thru the Bible radio listeners, not full critical commentary. PDF and extract already live under `sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/`; optional chapter-level split remains open (same pattern as 1 Peter).
