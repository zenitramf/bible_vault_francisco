## Core claims

- Israel’s weary wanderings were all over, and the promised rest was attained. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-mae/january/january-1|Spurgeon, January 1]]
- These words are for the humbling of God’s people; they are called God’s vine, but what are they by nature more than others? [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-mae/january/january-22|Spurgeon, January 22]]
- For several days we have been dwelling upon the Saviour’s passion, and for some little time to come we shall linger there. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-mae/april/april-1|Spurgeon, April 1]]
- Jesus, our Lord, once crucified, dead and buried, now sits upon the throne of glory. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-mae/april/april-22|Spurgeon, April 22]]
