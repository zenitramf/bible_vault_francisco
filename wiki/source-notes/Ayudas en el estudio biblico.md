# Ayudas en el estudio bíblico

## Summary

This brief Spanish personal note frames Bible study as asking disciplined questions about a passage’s context. It models a simple hermeneutical habit: situate the text historically before leaping to application.

## Core claims

- When studying a passage, ask when it was written. [[sources/personal-notes/Ayudas en el estudio biblico#El contexto del pasaje|Source]]
- Ask to whom it was written (the audience). [[sources/personal-notes/Ayudas en el estudio biblico#El contexto del pasaje|Source]]
- Ask who wrote it. [[sources/personal-notes/Ayudas en el estudio biblico#El contexto del pasaje|Source]]

## Agreements and tensions

The note is a seed checklist only; it does not yet develop literary genre, canonical context, or Christ-centered reading. It complements fuller commentary synthesis rather than replacing it.

## Biblical passages

No single primary passage; method applies to any text under study.
