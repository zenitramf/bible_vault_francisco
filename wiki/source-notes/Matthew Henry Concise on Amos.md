# Matthew Henry Concise on Amos

## Summary

Intentional ingest of **Matthew Henry Concise** on Amos as a chapter-by-chapter backbone for the wiki passage atlas (9 chapters). Claims on each chapter page are drawn from the concise commentary headings and pastoral exposition. Part of campaign Phase 3.2 (OT remainder). McGee Notes & Outlines on Amos and Obadiah now thicken the same atlas with lay-prophet calling, privilege-creates-responsibility, day-of-the-LORD, and restoration frames ([[wiki/source-notes/McGee Notes and Outlines on Amos and Obadiah|McGee Amos–Obadiah]]).

## Core claims

- Amos is synthesized chapter-by-chapter from Matthew Henry Concise for wiki retrieval without source RAG. See [[wiki/passages/Amos 1|Amos 1]] through [[wiki/passages/Amos 9|Amos 9]].
- Material claims on each passage page cite the corresponding concise chapter file. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/amos/chapter-1|Matthew Henry Concise, Amos 1 sample]]

## Agreements and tensions

Single-corpus (Henry Concise) as atlas spine. Concept pages and passage hubs now also hold McGee and Henry Complete claims; this atlas supplies the book spine and should be thickened, not treated as exclusive. McGee’s dispensational day-of-the-LORD and millennial readings of Amos 5 and 9 sit beside Henry’s pastoral and gospel-gathering applications.
