# McGee Notes and Outlines on 1 John

## Summary

Intentional ingest of **J. Vernon McGee, Thru the Bible Notes & Outlines** on 1 John (immutable PDF under `sources/personal-notes/ingest/`; original raw path `raw/1_John.pdf`). McGee frames the letter as the family epistle—the *sanctum sanctorum* of the NT—written by John the Apostle about A.D. 90–100 after the Gospel and before Revelation. Purpose centers on fellowship, full joy, not sinning, knowing eternal life, and believing on the Son’s name (1 John 1:3–4; 2:1; 5:13). Outline: God is light (1:1–2:2), God is love (2:3–4:21), God is life (ch. 5). Key words: light, love, life, fellowship, know—true knowledge against Gnostic denial of Christ’s full humanity. High-quality pdftotext extract supports book-level synthesis.

## Core claims

- Writer John the Apostle; date A.D. 90–100; Gospel first, then epistles, then Revelation before death about A.D. 100. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_John.pdf|McGee Notes & Outlines on 1 John]]
- Fivefold purpose: fellowship with Father and Son and believers (1:3); full joy (1:4); that ye sin not (2:1); know that ye have eternal life (5:13); believe on the name of the Son of God (5:13). Gospel purpose is life through believing (John 20:30–31); epistle purpose is assurance for those who already believe. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_John.pdf|McGee on purpose]]
- Family of God vs church body: Paul writes to the church (positional blessings in Christ, Eph 1:3); John writes to the family where relationship can be broken and restored by confession (1:9). Family is larger than the church; both sit inside the Kingdom but are not synonymous. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_John.pdf|McGee on family of God]]
- Written against first heresy (Gnosticism): boasted super-knowledge; accepted deity, denied humanity of Jesus (some said Spirit came at baptism and left at the cross). John asserts full personality of Jesus—heard, seen, gazed upon, handled (1:1–2). [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_John.pdf|McGee on prologue and Gnosticism]]
- God is light (1:5)—moral character, holiness that penetrates darkness; darkness is hostile chaos, not mere opposite. Fellowship (*koinonia*) is sharing the things of Christ by walking in light (1:3–7); blood of Christ cleanses as the believer discovers sin in the Word’s light. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_John.pdf|McGee on 1 John 1:3–7]]
- Confession (*homologomen*) means saying the same thing God says about specific sins; He is faithful and just to forgive and cleanse (1:9). Claiming no sin is self-deception; claiming we have not sinned makes God a liar (1:8, 10). [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_John.pdf|McGee on 1 John 1:8–10]]
- Christ’s advocacy (2:1–2): attorney/comforter for the sinning believer against the accuser; propitiation is expiation, the mercy-seat basis on which God extends mercy. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_John.pdf|McGee on 1 John 2:1–2]]
- Obedience and brother-love are experiential tests of knowing Christ against Gnostic esoteric knowledge (2:3–11); keep his word includes whatever pleases him; full commitment is love for Christ. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_John.pdf|McGee on 1 John 2:3–11]]
- Spiritual ages: *teknia* (dear children—all forgiven believers); fathers (mature); young men (overcome by the Word); *paidia* (know they are sons but little more) (2:12–14). [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_John.pdf|McGee on 1 John 2:12–14]]
- Do not love the world-system (*kosmos* as satanic order of greed, ambition, pleasure, deceit—not material creation or mankind as in John 3:16). Lust of flesh, eyes, and pride of life mirror Eve’s temptation; the system is passing; Antichrist is coming (against and imitation of Christ); many antichrists already (2:15–23). Departure from the Word identifies false professors (2:19). Holy Spirit anoints and teaches (2:20, 27). Believers may be ashamed at the rapture if disobedient (2:28). [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_John.pdf|McGee on 1 John 2:15–28]]
- Father’s love names us children of God—and we are (3:1); we shall be like Christ when we see him (3:2); hope purifies now (3:3). [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_John.pdf|McGee on 1 John 3:1–3]]
- Two natures: new nature born of God does not practice sin; old nature remains (avocado-tree illustration). Continuing in sin marks an unsaved person; practicing righteousness and brother-love mark God’s children (3:4–24). Love is action, not sentiment; love in action gives prayer confidence (3:16–22). [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_John.pdf|McGee on 1 John 3:4–24]]
- Test the spirits: false teachers deny the incarnation and redemption that flows from it—spirit of Antichrist (4:1–6). Greater is he that is in you; elect cannot permanently be deceived. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_John.pdf|McGee on 1 John 4:1–6]]
- God is love: prior love shown in sending the Son as propitiation; believers love because he first loved; perfect love (Christ’s propitiatory work) gives boldness in judgment and casts out fear; loving God without loving the brethren is a lie (4:7–21). [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_John.pdf|McGee on 1 John 4:7–21]]
- Victory over the world by faith (*nike* only here in NT)—Jericho pattern of believing, not fighting (5:1–5). Water (Word), blood (Christ’s death), and Spirit bear witness; key verse 5:13—written that believers may know they have eternal life. Prayer boldness hinges on asking according to his will/Word (5:14–15). Sin unto death is physical death chastening for some believers (Ananias, Corinth, Absalom, Moses/Aaron examples) (5:16–17). New nature never sins; whole world lies asleep in the evil one; keep from idols (5:18–21). [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_John.pdf|McGee on 1 John 5]]

## Agreements and tensions

- Agrees with Henry Concise/Complete atlas on incarnation witness, light/holiness and confession, advocacy and propitiation, brother-love, testing spirits, and assurance of eternal life ([[wiki/source-notes/Matthew Henry Concise on 1 John|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 John]]).
- Adds sharper family-vs-church/body frame, Gnostic polemic, two-natures avocado illustration, world-system taxonomy (three *kosmos* senses), Antichrist catalog, *nike*/Jericho victory, and physical “sin unto death” reading—useful to [[wiki/concepts/Holiness|Holiness]], [[wiki/concepts/Salvation|Salvation]], [[wiki/concepts/Christ|Christ]], [[wiki/concepts/Faith|Faith]], [[wiki/concepts/Atonement|Atonement]], [[wiki/concepts/Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]], [[wiki/concepts/Prayer|Prayer]], and [[wiki/concepts/Church|Church]].
- Dispensational rapture shame (2:28), cessation-of-practice “does not sin” as two-natures, and sin-unto-death as physical only should be held beside other sources, not as the vault’s sole word.
- McGee equates propitiation with “expiation” and mercy seat; keep that wording as his gloss beside the vault’s propitiatory emphasis on [[wiki/concepts/Atonement|Atonement]].
- Corpus is single-author outline notes for Thru the Bible radio listeners, not full critical commentary. PDF sits under personal-notes ingest; optional promotion into `sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/` remains open (same pattern as other McGee notes).
