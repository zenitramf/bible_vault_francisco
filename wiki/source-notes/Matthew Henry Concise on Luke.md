# Matthew Henry Concise on Luke

## Summary

Intentional ingest of **Matthew Henry Concise** on Luke as a chapter-by-chapter backbone for the wiki passage atlas (24 chapters). Claims on each chapter page are drawn from the concise commentary headings and pastoral exposition.

## Core claims

- Luke is synthesized chapter-by-chapter from Matthew Henry Concise for wiki retrieval without source RAG. See [[wiki/passages/Luke 1|Luke 1]] through [[wiki/passages/Luke 24|Luke 24]].
- Material claims on each passage page cite the corresponding concise chapter file. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/luke/chapter-1|Matthew Henry Concise, chapter 1 sample]]

## Agreements and tensions

Single-corpus (Henry Concise). Concept pages may already hold multi-source claims (Henry Complete, Spurgeon, personal notes); this atlas supplies the book spine and should be thickened, not treated as exclusive.
