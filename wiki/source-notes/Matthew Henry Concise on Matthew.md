# Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew

## Summary

This source-note marks the intentional ingest of **Matthew Henry Concise** on Matthew as a chapter-by-chapter backbone for the Gospel passage atlas. The concise commentary traces Jesus as Messiah from genealogy and incarnation through teaching (especially the Sermon on the Mount), miracles, discipleship cost, passion, and resurrection commission.

## Core claims

- Jesus is Son of David and God with us; Gentile seekers may worship him while rulers rage. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-1|Matthew 1]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-2|Matthew 2]]
- He fulfills righteousness, conquers temptation by Scripture, and preaches the kingdom with authority. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-3|Matthew 3]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-4|Matthew 4]]
- The Sermon on the Mount forms heart-righteousness, sincere piety, prayer, and doing the word. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-5|Matthew 5]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-6|Matthew 6]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-7|Matthew 7]]
- Authority over disease, demons, and nature pairs with costly discipleship and mission instructions. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-8|Matthew 8]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-10|Matthew 10]]
- Confession of Christ, the cross, and self-denial define true disciples; the church is built on this confession. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-16|Matthew 16]]
- Passion, atoning death, riven veil, and resurrection commission close the Gospel with all authority and promised presence. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-26|Matthew 26]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-27|Matthew 27]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-28|Matthew 28]]

## Agreements and tensions

Single-corpus (Henry Concise). Prior wiki material already used Henry Complete and Spurgeon on select Matthew texts (e.g. prayer in ch. 6, discipleship in ch. 16, temptation in ch. 4). Concise atlas pages should be thickened, not replaced, by those multi-source concept pages.
