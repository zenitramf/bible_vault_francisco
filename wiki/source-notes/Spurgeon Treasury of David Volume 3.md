# Spurgeon Treasury of David Volume 3

## Summary

Intentional **volume** ingest of Spurgeon's *Treasury of David* covering roughly Psalms 53–78 (26 source files in volume tree). This phase enriches existing [[wiki/source-notes/Matthew Henry Concise on Psalms|Matthew Henry Concise Psalms]] passage pages on hubs; it does not create one wiki page per verse.

## Core claims

- If the leader of the choir is privileged to sing the jubilates of divine grace, he must not disdain to chant the miseries of human depravity. This is the second time he has had the same Psalm entrusted to him, see Psalm 14, and he must, therefore, be the more careful in… [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-tod/volume-3/psalm-53|Treasury of David, psalm-53]]
