# Spurgeon Treasury of David Volume 1

## Summary

Intentional **volume** ingest of Spurgeon's *Treasury of David* covering roughly Psalms 1–26 (26 source files in volume tree). This phase enriches existing [[wiki/source-notes/Matthew Henry Concise on Psalms|Matthew Henry Concise Psalms]] passage pages on hubs; it does not create one wiki page per verse.

## Core claims

- Title — This Psalm may be regarded as The Preface Psalm having in it a notification of the contents of the entire Book. It is the psalmists's desire to teach us the way to blessedness, and to warn us of the sure destruction of sinners. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-tod/volume-1/psalm-1|Treasury of David, Psalm 1]]
- Title — "To the chief Musician upon Aijeleth Shahar. A Psalm of David." This ode of singular excellence was committed to the most excellent of the temple songsters; the chief among ten thousand is worthy to be extolled by the chief Musician; no meaner singer must have charge… [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-tod/volume-1/psalm-22|Treasury of David, Psalm 22]]
- There is no inspired title to this psalm, and none is needed, for it records no special event, and needs no other key than that which every Christian may find in his own bosom. It is David's Heavenly Pastoral; a surpassing ode, which none of the daughters of music can excel. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-tod/volume-1/psalm-23|Treasury of David, Psalm 23]]

## Agreements and tensions

ToD is more homiletical and citation-rich than Henry Concise. Where they differ in emphasis (e.g. Christological reading intensity), record both on the passage page.

## Biblical passages

Psalms 1–26 (volume scope); hubs prioritized for mesh.
