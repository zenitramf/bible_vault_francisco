# Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis

## Summary

Intentional ingest of **Matthew Henry Concise** on Genesis as a chapter-by-chapter backbone for the wiki passage atlas (50 chapters). Claims on each chapter page are drawn from the concise commentary headings and pastoral exposition. Part of campaign Phase 3.1 (OT priority).

## Core claims

- Genesis is synthesized chapter-by-chapter from Matthew Henry Concise for wiki retrieval without source RAG. See [[wiki/passages/Genesis 1|Genesis 1]] through [[wiki/passages/Genesis 50|Genesis 50]].
- Material claims on each passage page cite the corresponding concise chapter file. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-1|Matthew Henry Concise, Genesis 1 sample]]

## Agreements and tensions

Single-corpus (Henry Concise). Concept pages may already hold multi-source claims (Henry Complete, Spurgeon, personal notes); this atlas supplies the book spine and should be thickened, not treated as exclusive.

## Biblical passages

Genesis (full book scope in the atlas).
