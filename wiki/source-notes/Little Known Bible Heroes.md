# Little Known Bible Heroes

## Summary

This personal working list catalogs lesser-known biblical figures whose brief appearances display faithfulness, courage, hospitality, loyalty, or quiet service. Several entries are already marked as studied (for example Naaman’s wife’s maid, Dorcas, Rhoda, the midwives, and the Shunammite woman); others remain open for later people or passage pages.

## Core claims

- Scripture highlights obscure servants whose faith and courage matter to the larger redemptive story (for example Jochebed hiding Moses; the Hebrew midwives protecting infants). [[sources/personal-notes/Little Known Bible Heroes|Source]]
- Gentile and minor characters can model loyalty and hospitality (Ittai the Gittite; the Shunammite woman; the widow of Zarephath). [[sources/personal-notes/Little Known Bible Heroes|Source]]
- New Testament co-workers and quiet disciples (Phoebe, Priscilla and Aquila, Epaphras, Rhoda, Dorcas) exemplify service, accurate teaching, prayer, and testimony. [[sources/personal-notes/Little Known Bible Heroes|Source]]
- The list intentionally tracks study progress (checked vs unchecked) as a pastoral discipleship resource, not a finished biographical encyclopedia. [[sources/personal-notes/Little Known Bible Heroes|Source]]

## Agreements and tensions

This is a personal inventory, not a commentary. It should seed future [[wiki/people/index|people]] pages and passage studies rather than be treated as exhaustive historical judgment on each figure.

## Biblical passages

Sample loci from the list: Exodus 1–2; 2 Kings 4–5; Acts 9; Acts 12; Jeremiah 38–39; Romans 16; Colossians 1; 4.
