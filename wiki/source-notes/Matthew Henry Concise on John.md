# Matthew Henry Concise on John

## Summary

Intentional ingest of **Matthew Henry Concise** on John as a chapter-by-chapter backbone for the wiki passage atlas (21 chapters). Claims on each chapter page are drawn from the concise commentary headings and pastoral exposition.

## Core claims

- John is synthesized chapter-by-chapter from Matthew Henry Concise for wiki retrieval without source RAG. See [[wiki/passages/John 1|John 1]] through [[wiki/passages/John 21|John 21]].
- Material claims on each passage page cite the corresponding concise chapter file. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/john/chapter-1|Matthew Henry Concise, chapter 1 sample]]
