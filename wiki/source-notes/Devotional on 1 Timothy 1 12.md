# Devotional on 1 Timothy 1:12

## Summary

The devotional organizes Christian ministry around three aspects of 1 Timothy 1:12: Christ is the focus of ministry, Christ supplies its force or enablement, and Christ entrusts ministry with an expectation of faithfulness. Ministry therefore exists for Christ and the gospel rather than for its own institutional continuation.

## Core claims

- Christ is worthy of the minister's time, strength, and resources, so ministry must retain his preeminence. [[sources/personal-notes/Devotional on 1 Timothy 1_12#The Focus of our Ministry|Source]]
- Sufficiency for ministry comes from Christ; human weakness becomes an occasion for Christ's power to be displayed. [[sources/personal-notes/Devotional on 1 Timothy 1_12#The Force of our Ministry|Source]]
- Ministry is a stewardship committed by God, carrying both a mandate and an expectation of faithfulness. [[sources/personal-notes/Devotional on 1 Timothy 1_12#The Fortitude of our Ministry|Source]]

## Agreements and tensions

This seed summary represents one devotional source. It should not yet be treated as a comprehensive account of calling, ordination, pastoral authority, or the church's role in recognizing ministers.
