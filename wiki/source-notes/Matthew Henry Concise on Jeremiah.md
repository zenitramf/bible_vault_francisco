# Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah

## Summary

Intentional ingest of **Matthew Henry Concise** on Jeremiah as a chapter-by-chapter backbone for the wiki passage atlas (52 chapters). Claims on each chapter page are drawn from the concise commentary headings and pastoral exposition. Part of campaign Phase 3.2 (OT remainder).

- Jeremiah is synthesized chapter-by-chapter from Matthew Henry Concise for wiki retrieval without source RAG. See [[wiki/passages/Jeremiah 1|Jeremiah 1]] through [[wiki/passages/Jeremiah 52|Jeremiah 52]].
- Material claims on each passage page cite the corresponding concise chapter file. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-1|Matthew Henry Concise, Jeremiah 1 sample]]
