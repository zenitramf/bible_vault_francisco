# Matthew Henry Concise on Romans

## Summary

This source-note marks the intentional ingest of **Matthew Henry Concise** on Romans as a chapter-by-chapter backbone for the wiki passage atlas. The concise commentary is pastoral and doctrinal: it charges all flesh with sin, locates deliverance in faith in Christ, moves from justification to holiness and Spirit-given life, treats Israel and the nations under God’s wise plan, and closes with concrete church life, mutual forbearance, and doxology.

## Core claims

- Romans opens with gospel righteousness from faith to faith and the exposure of Gentile (and then Jewish) guilt under wrath and false privilege. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-1|Romans 1]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-2|Romans 2]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-3|Romans 3]]
- Justification is free by grace through faith, illustrated in Abraham, fruiting in peace and access, and grounded in Christ’s death over against Adam’s offense. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-4|Romans 4]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-5|Romans 5]]
- Free grace does not license sin; believers die to sin, struggle with remaining corruption, and live by the Spirit with no condemnation in Christ. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-6|Romans 6]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-7|Romans 7]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-8|Romans 8]]
- Israel, election, and Gentile inclusion call for humility and adoration rather than pride. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-9|Romans 9]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-10|Romans 10]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-11|Romans 11]]
- Practical Christianity is living sacrifice, sincere love, civil and mutual duties, care for the weak, cooperative mission, and resistance to dividers. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-12|Romans 12]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-14|Romans 14]] [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-16|Romans 16]]

## Agreements and tensions

This note is single-corpus (Henry Concise). Prior wiki seeds already used Henry Complete (e.g. Romans 3–4) and Spurgeon (e.g. Romans 8 prayer). Concise should not be treated as canceling Complete’s detail or Spurgeon’s experiential emphasis; later batches should record tensions on the relevant concept and passage pages.
