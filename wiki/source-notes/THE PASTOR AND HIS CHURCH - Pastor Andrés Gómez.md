# The Pastor and His Church — Pastor Andrés Gómez

## Summary

These Spanish conference notes (Fuegos de Evangelismo 2026) teach that a healthy church needs biblical leadership and a committed congregation working in mutual harmony. The pastorate is a gift of God for the church’s perfection and edification (Ephesians 4:11–16), not a business arrangement. Believers are sheep—dependent, easily distracted, unable to return alone—and the pastor must feed, warn, care for families, serve without worldly motives, model faithful life, and give account to the Chief Shepherd. The pastor also bears an interior spiritual war; the church must honor, pray for, and work with its pastors.

## Core claims

- The church is designed by God for mutual harmony: no healthy church without biblical leadership, and no successful ministry without a truly committed congregation. [[sources/personal-notes/THE PASTOR AND HIS CHURCH - Pastor Andrés Gómez#Resumen principal|Source]]
- The pastorate is a gift of God for the perfection and edification of the church, not a common job or business deal. [[sources/personal-notes/THE PASTOR AND HIS CHURCH - Pastor Andrés Gómez#Resumen principal|Source]]
- As sheep, believers are dependent, easily stray by distraction, and do not know how to return alone; salvation is the Shepherd seeking and bringing them back. [[sources/personal-notes/THE PASTOR AND HIS CHURCH - Pastor Andrés Gómez#La condición de las ovejas|Source]]
- The pastor’s first priority is to feed the flock with biblical truth and sound doctrine, not buildings or programs. [[sources/personal-notes/THE PASTOR AND HIS CHURCH - Pastor Andrés Gómez#Las responsabilidades de un pastor|Source]]
- Pastors must warn of danger, care for families, serve from servant motives (fleeing fame, money, and sexual immorality), be examples, and render account to God for doctrine and souls. [[sources/personal-notes/THE PASTOR AND HIS CHURCH - Pastor Andrés Gómez#Las responsabilidades de un pastor|Source]]
- The pastor walks between two worlds with silent wounds and satanic oppression; his wife often sustains him yet is among the most criticized. [[sources/personal-notes/THE PASTOR AND HIS CHURCH - Pastor Andrés Gómez#El peso interno y la guerra espiritual del pastor|Source]]
- The church must appreciate, honor, remember, pray for, and work united with its pastors in love and biblical submission. [[sources/personal-notes/THE PASTOR AND HIS CHURCH - Pastor Andrés Gómez#El deber de la iglesia|Source]]

## Agreements and tensions

This is a single Spanish conference note. It complements English commentary on Ephesians 4, 1 Peter 5, and spiritual warfare without replacing them. The “fama / feria / faldas” triad is pastoral and proverbial, not a full ethical treatise.
