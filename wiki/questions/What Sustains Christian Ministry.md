# What Sustains Christian Ministry?

## Summary

Christian ministry is sustained by Christ himself: his worth gives ministry its focus, his strength supplies its power, and his entrusting of responsibility gives it the character of faithful stewardship. The minister's resources and discipline matter, but they serve a work whose purpose and sufficiency come from Christ.

## Core claims

- Christ's glory and gospel supply the purpose that keeps ministry from becoming an end in itself. [[wiki/source-notes/Devotional on 1 Timothy 1 12#Core claims|Source note]]
- Christ's enablement answers the minister's insufficiency and weakness. [[wiki/source-notes/Devotional on 1 Timothy 1 12#Core claims|Source note]]
- Faithful stewardship governs the use of time, abilities, money, and entrusted responsibility. [[sources/personal-notes/Devotional on 1 Timothy 1_12#The Take-away|Source]]

## Agreements and tensions

This answer is a seed based on one devotional. It does not yet synthesize the roles of the Holy Spirit, the local church, fellow laborers, spiritual disciplines, rest, or material support.
