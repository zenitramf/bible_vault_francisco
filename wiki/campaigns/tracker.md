##### Locked promotion rubric (`reviewed`)

A concept may move `developing` → `reviewed` only when **all hard bars** pass. Soft bars are expected for hub concepts; document intentional soft misses under Open questions or defer.

| # | Check | Hard / soft | Pass criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Multi-claim | **Hard** | ≥5 material, source-linked Core-claim bullets (full-path Obsidian links under `sources/`). Hub gospel concepts should aim ≥7. |
| R2 | Multi-source | **Hard** | ≥2 **source families** (see family table) **and** ≥3 distinct source files; prefer ≥2 corpora (e.g. Henry + Spurgeon). |
| R3 | Evidence honesty | **Hard** | Every material claim traceable; no invented citations; quotes/paraphrases match linked sources. |
| R4 | Tensions | **Hard** | `## Agreements and tensions` has real agreements **and** at least one named tension or scoped limit (not Phase 4 inventory boilerplate alone). |
| R5 | Related pages | **Hard** | Curated mesh: peer concepts + key passages + parent source-notes. **No** Phase 4 volume-inventory dump as the bulk of Related. |
| R6 | `source_count` | **Hard** | Equals count of `sources/` wikilinks under the page Sources section; lint clean. |
| R7 | Open questions | **Hard** | Residual only (true unknowns / next thicken); not placeholders that admit the page is unfinished. |
| R8 | Summary quality | **Soft** | 1 short paragraph stating the concept’s spine without listing every claim. |
| R9 | Corpus balance | **Soft** | For gospel hubs: at least one Henry and one Spurgeon (or explicit defer why not). Personal notes alone never carry a page to `reviewed`. |
| R10 | Suite touchpoint | **Soft** | If the concept answers a [[wiki/questions/Core Query Suite|Core Query Suite]] item, that path still Hits after promotion. |

**Source families (for R2):** `mhenry-concise` · `mhenry-complete` · `chspurgeon-sermons` · `chspurgeon-tod` · `chspurgeon-fcb` · `chspurgeon-mae` · `personal-notes`. Concise+Complete alone can pass R2 for OT-heavy pages **if** claims are multi-file and tensions acknowledge Spurgeon gap.

**Anti-patterns (auto-fail):**

- Promoting because coverage is 100% or the page is old.
- Flattening Henry and Spurgeon into false consensus.
- Adding synonym concepts (e.g. Sanctification next to Holiness) to “look complete.”
- Padding claims with volume-inventory digests that do not assert doctrine.
- Using only wiki→wiki links as “evidence” without `sources/` paths.
