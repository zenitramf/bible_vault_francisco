# Psalm 143

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 143: David complains of his enemies and distresses. (1–6). He prays for comfort, guidance, and deliverance. (7–12).

## Core claims

- We have no righteousness of our own to plead, therefore must plead God's righteousness, and the word of promise which he has freely given us, and caused us to hope in. David, before he prays for the removal of his trouble, prays for the pardon of his sin, and depends upon mercy alone for it. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-143#Verses 1–6|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 143]]
- David prays that God would be well pleased with him, and let him know that he was so. He pleads the wretchedness of his case, if God withdrew from him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-143#Verses 7–12|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 143]]
