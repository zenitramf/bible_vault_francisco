# Psalm 48

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 48: The glories of the church of Christ..

## Core claims

- Jerusalem is the city of our God: none on earth render him due honour except the citizens of the spiritual Jerusalem. Happy the kingdom, the city, the family, the heart, in which God is great, in which he is all. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-48#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 48]]
- We have here the improvement which the people of God are to make of his glorious and gracious appearances for them. Let our faith in the word of God be hereby confirmed. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-48#Verses 8–14|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 48]]
- This psalm, as the two former, is a triumphant song; some think it was penned on occasion of Jehoshaphat's victory (2Ch 20:1-30), others of Sennacherib's defeat, when his army laid siege to Jerusalem in Hezekiah's… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-3/psalms/psalm-48|Matthew Henry Complete on Psalm 48]]
