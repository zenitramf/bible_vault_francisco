# Psalm 84

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 84: The psalmist expresses his affection to the ordinances of God. (1–7). His desire towards the God of the ordinances. (8–12).

## Core claims

- The ordinances of God are the believer's solace in this evil world; in them he enjoys the presence of the living God: this causes him to regret his absence from them. They are to his soul as the nest to the bird. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-84#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 84]]
- In all our addresses to God, we must desire that he would look on Christ, his Anointed One, and accept us for his sake: we must look to Him with faith, and then God will with favour look upon the face of the Anointed: we, without him, dare not show our faces. The psalmist pleads love to God's ordinances. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-84#Verses 8–12|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 84]]
