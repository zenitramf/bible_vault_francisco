# Revelation 16

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 16: The first vial is poured out on the earth, the second on the sea, the third on the rivers and fountains. (1–7). The fourth on the sun, the fifth on the seat of the beast. (8–11). The sixth on the great river Euphrates….

## Core claims

- We are to pray that the will of God may be done on earth as it is done in heaven. Here is a succession of terrible judgments of Providence; and there seems to be an allusion to several of the plagues of Egypt. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-16#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 16]]
- The heart of man is so desperately wicked, that the most severe miseries never will bring any to repent, without the special grace of God. - This probably shows the destruction of the Turkish power, and of idolatry, and that a way will be made for the return of the Jews. Or, take it for Rome, as mystical Babylon, the name of Babylon being put for Rome, which was meant, but was not then to be directly named. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-16#Verses 12–16|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 16]]
- The seventh and last angel poured forth his vial, and the downfal of Babylon was finished. The church triumphant in heaven saw it and rejoiced; the church in conflict on earth saw it and became triumphant. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-16#Verses 17–21|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 16]]
- In this chapter we have an account of the pouring forth of these vials that were filled with the wrath of God.
