# Isaiah 34

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 34: God's vengeance against the enemies of his church. (1-8). Their desolation. (9-17).

## Core claims

- Here is a prophecy of the wars of the Lord, all which are both righteous and successful. All nations are concerned. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-34#Verses 1-8|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 34]]
- Those who aim to ruin the church, can never do that, but will ruin themselves. What dismal changes sin can make! [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-34#Verses 9-17|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 34]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
