# 1 Samuel 16

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Samuel 16: Samuel sent to Bethlehem to Jesse. (1–5). David is anointed. (6–13). Saul troubled with an evil spirit, is quieted by David. (14–23).

## Core claims

- It appears that Saul was grown very wicked. Of what would he not be guilty, who durst think to kill Samuel? [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-samuel/chapter-16#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Samuel 16]]
- It was strange that Samuel, who had been so disappointed in Saul, whose countenance and stature recommended him, should judge of another man by that rule. We can tell how men look, but God can tell what they are. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-samuel/chapter-16#Verses 6–13|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Samuel 16]]
- Saul is made a terror to himself. The Spirit of the Lord departed from him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-samuel/chapter-16#Verses 14–23|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Samuel 16]]
- At this chapter begins the story of David, one that makes as great a figure in the sacred story as almost any of the worthies of the Old Testament, one that both with his sword and with his pen served the honour of God… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/1-samuel/chapter-16|Matthew Henry Complete on 1 Samuel 16]]
