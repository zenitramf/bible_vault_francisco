# Psalm 23

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 23: Confidence in God's grace and care. (1-6).

## Core claims

- “The Lord is my shepherd.” In these words, the believer is taught to express his satisfaction in the care of the great Pastor of the universe, the Redeemer and Preserver of men. With joy he reflects that he has a shepherd, and that shepherd is Jehovah. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-23#Verses 1-6|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 23]]

- There is no inspired title to this psalm, and none is needed, for it records no special event, and needs no other key than that which every Christian may find in his own bosom. It is David's Heavenly Pastoral; a surpassing ode, which none of the daughters of music can excel. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-tod/volume-1/psalm-23|Spurgeon, Treasury of David on Psalm 23]]

- Many of David's psalms are full of complaints, but this is full of comforts, and the expressions of delight in God's great goodness and dependence upon him. It is a psalm which has been sung by good Christians, and will be while the world stands, with a great deal of pleasure… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-3/psalms/psalm-23|Matthew Henry Complete on Psalm 23]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis began from Matthew Henry Concise and is thickened with Spurgeon's Treasury of David on the same psalm (Phase 3.4). Concise notes remain pastoral summaries; ToD adds homiletical depth and historical citations.
