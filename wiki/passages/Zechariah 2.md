# Zechariah 2

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Zechariah 2: The prosperity of Jerusalem. (1–5). The Jews called to return to their own land. (6–9). A promise of God's presence. (10–13).

## Core claims

- The Son of David, even the Man Christ Jesus, whom the prophet sees with a measuring line in his hand, is the Master-Builder of his church. God notices the extent of his church, and will take care that whatever number of guests are brought to the wedding-supper, there shall be room. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/zechariah/chapter-2#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on Zechariah 2]]
- If God will build Jerusalem for the people and their comfort, they must inhabit it for him and his glory. The promises and privileges with which God's people are blessed, should engage us to join them, whatever it costs us. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/zechariah/chapter-2#Verses 6–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Zechariah 2]]
- Here is a prediction of the coming of Christ in human nature. Many nations in that day would renounce idolatry, and God will own those for his people who join him with purpose of heart. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/zechariah/chapter-2#Verses 10–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Zechariah 2]]
- In this chapter we have, I. Another vision which the prophet saw, not for his own entertainment, but for his satisfaction and the edification of those to whom he was sent, Zec 2:1,2. II. A sermon upon it, in the rest… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/zechariah/chapter-2|Matthew Henry Complete on Zechariah 2]]
