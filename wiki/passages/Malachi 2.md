# Malachi 2

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Malachi 2: The priests reproved for neglecting their covenant. (1–9). The people reproved for their evil practices. (10–17).

## Core claims

- What is here said of the covenant of priesthood, is true of the covenant of grace made with all believers, as spiritual priests. It is a covenant of life and peace; it assures all believers of all happiness, both in this world and in that to come. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/malachi/chapter-2#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Malachi 2]]
- Corrupt practices are the fruit of corrupt principles; and he who is false to his God, will not be true to his fellow mortals. In contempt of the marriage covenant, which God instituted, the Jews put away the wives they had of their own nation, probably to make room for strange wives. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/malachi/chapter-2#Verses 10–17|Matthew Henry Concise on Malachi 2]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
