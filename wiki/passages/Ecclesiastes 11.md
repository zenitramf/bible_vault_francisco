# Ecclesiastes 11

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ecclesiastes 11: Exhortation to liberality. (1-6). An admonition to prepare for death, and to young persons to be religious. (7-10).

## Core claims

- Solomon presses the rich to do good to others. Give freely, though it may seem thrown away and lost. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ecclesiastes/chapter-11#Verses 1-6|Matthew Henry Concise on Ecclesiastes 11]]
- Life is sweet to bad men, because they have their portion in this life; it is sweet to good men, because it is the time of preparation for a better; it is sweet to all. Here is a caution to think of death, even when life is most sweet. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ecclesiastes/chapter-11#Verses 7-10|Matthew Henry Concise on Ecclesiastes 11]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
