# 1 Chronicles 18

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Chronicles 18: David's victories. (1-17).

## Core claims

- This chapter is the same as 2Sa 8. Our good fight of faith, under the Captain of our salvation, will end in everlasting triumph and peace. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-chronicles/chapter-18#Verses 1-17|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Chronicles 18]]
- David's piety and his prayer we had an account of in the foregoing chapter; here follows immediately that which one might reasonably expect, an account of his prosperity; for those that seek first the kingdom of God… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/1-chronicles/chapter-18|Matthew Henry Complete on 1 Chronicles 18]]
