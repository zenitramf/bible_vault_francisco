# Revelation 2

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 2: Epistles to the churches in Asia, with warnings and encouragements, To the church at Ephesus; (1–7). at Smyrna; (8–11). at Pergamos; (12–17). and at Thyatira. (18–29).

## Core claims

- These churches were in such different states as to purity of doctrine and the power of godliness, that the words of Christ to them will always suit the cases of other churches, and professors.
