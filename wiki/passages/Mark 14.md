# Mark 14

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Mark 14: Christ anointed at Bethany. (1–11). The passover, Jesus declares that Judas would betray him. (12–21). The Lord's supper instituted. (22–31). Christ's agony in the garden. (32–42). He is betrayed and taken. (43–52). C….

## Core claims

- Did Christ pour out his soul unto death for us, and shall we think any thing too precious for him? Do we give him the precious ointment of our best affections? [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/mark/chapter-14#Verses 1–11|Matthew Henry Concise on Mark 14]]
- Nothing could be less the result of human foresight than the events here related. But our Lord knows all things about us before they come to pass. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/mark/chapter-14#Verses 12–21|Matthew Henry Concise on Mark 14]]
- The Lord's supper is food for the soul, therefore a very little of that which is for the body, as much as will serve for a sign, is enough. It was instituted by the example and the practice of our Master, to remain in force till his second coming. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/mark/chapter-14#Verses 22–31|Matthew Henry Concise on Mark 14]]
- Christ's sufferings began with the sorest of all, those in his soul. He began to be sorely amazed; words not used in St. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/mark/chapter-14#Verses 32–42|Matthew Henry Concise on Mark 14]]
- Peter's denying Christ began by keeping at a distance from him. Those that are shy of godliness, are far in the way to deny Christ. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/mark/chapter-14#Verses 66–72|Matthew Henry Concise on Mark 14]]
