# Proverbs 10

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 10: Through the whole of the Proverbs, we are to look for somewhat beyond the first sense the passage may imply, and this we shall find to be Christ. He is the Wisdom so often spoken of in this book..

## Core claims

- The comfort of parents much depends on their children; and this suggests to both, motives to their duties. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-10#Verse 1|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 10]]
- Perhaps a righteous man has no more than what he works hard for, but that labour tends to life. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-10#Verse 16|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 10]]
- A good man discourses wisely for the benefit of others. But it is the sin, and will be the ruin of a wicked man, that he speaks what is displeasing to God, and provoking to those he converses with. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-10#Verses 31, 32|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 10]]
