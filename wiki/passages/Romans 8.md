# Romans 8

## Summary

Henry Concise presents Romans 8 as the security and privilege of those in Christ: freedom from condemnation, walk after the Spirit, adoption and witness, hope under tribulation, the Spirit’s help in prayer infirmity, God’s purpose for those who love him, and final triumph that nothing can separate from the love of God in Christ Jesus.

## Core claims

- Believers may be chastened but will not be condemned with the world; union with Christ through faith secures them, and the walk is after the Spirit rather than the flesh. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-8#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 8]]
- If the Spirit is in us, Christ is in us; the Spirit of adoption works the disposition of children and witnesses with our spirit. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-8#Verses 10–17|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 8]]
- Present sufferings are not worthy to be compared with the glory to be revealed; creation itself groans toward redemption’s hope. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-8#Verses 18–25|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 8]]
- Believers often do not know what to pray for as they ought; the Spirit helps their infirmities and intercedes according to the will of God. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-8#Verses 26, 27|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 8]]
- God’s love and purpose stand behind the believer’s security; if God is for us, who can be against us, and nothing shall separate from his love in Christ. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-8#Verses 28–31|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 8]]
- The apostle, having fully explained the doctrine of justification, and pressed the necessity of sanctification, in this chapter applies himself to the consolation of the Lord's people. Ministers are helpers of the joy… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-6/romans/chapter-8|Matthew Henry Complete on Romans 8]]
