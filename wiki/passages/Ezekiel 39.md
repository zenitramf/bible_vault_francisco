# Ezekiel 39

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 39: The destruction of Gog. (1-10). Its extent. (11-22). Israel again favoured. (23-29).

## Core claims

- The Lord will make the most careless and hardened transgressors know his holy name, either by his righteous anger, or by the riches of his mercy and grace. The weapons formed against Zion shall not prosper. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-39#Verses 1-10|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 39]]
- How numerous the enemies which God destroyed for the defence of his people Israel! Times of great deliverances should be times of reformation. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-39#Verses 11-22|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 39]]
- When the Lord shall have mercy on the whole house of Israel, by converting them to Christianity, and when they shall have borne the shame of being cast off for their sins, then the nations shall learn to know, worship, and serve him. Then Israel also shall know the Lord, as revealed in and by Christ. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-39#Verses 23-29|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 39]]
- This chapter continues and concludes the prophecy against Gog and Magog, in whose destruction God crowns his favour to his people Israel, which shines very brightly after the scattering of that black cloud in the close… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/ezekiel/chapter-39|Matthew Henry Complete on Ezekiel 39]]
