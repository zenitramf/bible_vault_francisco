# Hosea 9

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 9: The distress to come upon Israel. (1-6). The approach of the day of trouble. (7-10). Judgments on Israel. (11-17).

## Core claims

- Israel gave rewards to their idols, in the offerings presented to them. It is common for those who are niggardly in religion, to be prodigal upon their lusts. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/hosea/chapter-9#Verses 1-6|Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 9]]
- Time had been when the spiritual watchmen of Israel were with the Lord, but now they were like the snare of a fowler to entangle persons to their ruin. The people were become as corrupt as those of Gibeah, Judg. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/hosea/chapter-9#Verses 7-10|Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 9]]
- God departs from a people, or from a person, when he withdraws his goodness and mercy from them; and when the Lord is departed, what can the creature do? Even though, for the present, good things seem to remain, yet the blessing is gone if God is gone. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/hosea/chapter-9#Verses 11-17|Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 9]]
