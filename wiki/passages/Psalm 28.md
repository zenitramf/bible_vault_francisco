# Psalm 28

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 28: A prayer in distress. (1–5). Thanksgiving for deliverance. (6–9).

## Core claims

- David is very earnest in prayer. Observe his faith in prayer; God is my rock, on whom I build my hope. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-28#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 28]]
- Has God heard our supplications? Let us then bless his name. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-28#Verses 6–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 28]]
