## Core claims

- Sin makes men cowards. Whatever difficulties the righteous meet in the way of duty, they are not daunted. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-28#Verse 1|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 28]]
- A wicked ruler, whatever we may call him, this scripture calls a roaring lion, and a ranging bear. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-28#Verse 15|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 28]]
- When power is put into the hands of the wicked, wise men decline public business. If the reader will go diligently over this and the other chapters, in many places where at first he may suppose there is least of Christ, still he will find what will lead to him.
