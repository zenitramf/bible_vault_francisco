# Micah 5

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Micah 5: The birth of Christ and conversion of the Gentiles. (1–6). The triumphs of Israel. (7–15).

## Core claims

- Having showed how low the house of David would be brought, a prediction of the Messiah and his kingdom is added to encourage the faith of God's people. His existence from eternity as God, and his office as Mediator, are noticed. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/micah/chapter-5#Verses 1–6|Matthew Henry Concise on Micah 5]]
- The remnant of Israel, converted to Christ in the primitive times, were among many nations as the drops of dew, and were made instruments in calling a large increase of spiritual worshippers. But to those who neglected or opposed this salvation, they would, as lions, cause terror, their doctrine condemning them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/micah/chapter-5#Verses 7–15|Matthew Henry Concise on Micah 5]]
