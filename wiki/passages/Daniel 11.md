# Daniel 11

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Daniel 11: The vision of the Scriptures of truth. (1-45).

## Core claims

- The angel shows Daniel the succession of the Persian and Grecian empires. The kings of Egypt and Syria are noticed: Judea was between their dominions, and affected by their contests. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/daniel/chapter-11#Verses 1-30|Matthew Henry Concise on Daniel 11]]
- The remainder of this prophecy is very difficult, and commentators differ much respecting it. From Antiochus the account seems to pass to antichrist. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/daniel/chapter-11#Verses 31-45|Matthew Henry Concise on Daniel 11]]
- The angel Gabriel, in this chapter, performs his promise made to Daniel in the foregoing chapter, that he would "show him what should befal his people in the latter days," according to that which was "written in the… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/daniel/chapter-11|Matthew Henry Complete on Daniel 11]]
