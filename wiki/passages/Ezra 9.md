# Ezra 9

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezra 9: Ezra mourns for the Jews' conduct. (1–4). Ezra's confession of sins. (5–15).

## Core claims

- Many corruptions lurk out of the view of the most careful rulers. Some of the people disobeyed the express command of God, which forbade all marriages with the heathen, De 7. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezra/chapter-9#Verses 1–4|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezra 9]]
- The sacrifice, especially the evening sacrifice, was a type of the blessed Lamb of God, who in the evening of the world, was to take away sin by the sacrifice of himself. Ezra's address is a penitent confession of sin, the sin of his people. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezra/chapter-9#Verses 5–15|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezra 9]]
- The affairs of the church were in a very good posture, we may well suppose, now that Ezra presided in them. Look without; the government was kind to them.
