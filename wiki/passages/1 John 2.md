# 1 John 2

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise directs to Christ’s atonement for sinful infirmities, obedience and brother-love as effects of saving knowledge, address to little children through fathers, contempt of the world, and abiding truth. McGee structures the chapter under God is love: advocacy (2:1–2), walking in love and ages of believers (2:3–14), not loving the satanic world-system, Antichrist, and the Spirit’s teaching anointing (2:15–28).

## Core claims

- When have an Advocate with the Father; one who has undertaken, and is fully able, to plead in behalf of every one who applies for pardon and salvation in his name, depending on his pleading for them. He is “Jesus,” the Saviour, and “Christ,” the Messiah, the Anointed. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-john/chapter-2#Verses 1, 2|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 John 2]]
- What knowledge of Christ can that be, which sees not that he is most worthy of our entire obedience? And a disobedient life shows there is neither religion nor honesty in the professor. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-john/chapter-2#Verses 3–11|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 John 2]]
- As Christians have their peculiar states, so they have peculiar duties; but there are precepts and obedience common to all, particularly mutual love, and contempt of the world. The youngest sincere disciple is pardoned: the communion of saints is attended with the forgiveness of sins. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-john/chapter-2#Verses 12–14|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 John 2]]
- The things of the world may be desired and possessed for the uses and purposes which God intended, and they are to be used by his grace, and to his glory; but believers must not seek or value them for those purposes to which sin abuses them. - The truth of Christ, abiding in us, is a means to sever from sin, and unites us to the Son of God, Joh 15:3, 4. What value should we put upon gospel truth! [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-john/chapter-2#Verses 24–29|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 John 2]]
- Here the apostle encourages against sins of infirmity (1Jo 2:1,2), shows the true knowledge and love of God (1Jo 2:3-6), renews the precept of fraternal love (1Jo 2:7-11), addresses the several ages of Christians (1Jo… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-6/1-john/chapter-2|Matthew Henry Complete on 1 John 2]]
- McGee: Advocate is attorney/comforter for the sinning believer; propitiation is expiation/mercy-seat basis of mercy (2:1–2). Obedience is experiential knowledge vs Gnostic esoterica; brother-love vs hatred tests light; *teknia*/fathers/young men/*paidia* ages (2:3–14). [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_John.pdf|McGee on 1 John 2:1–14]]
- McGee: *Kosmos* here is satanic world-system (not creation or mankind); lust of flesh/eyes and pride of life echo Eve; system is passing; Antichrist is against and imitation of Christ; many antichrists already; departure identifies false professors (2:19); Spirit anoints and teaches (2:20, 27); denial of Father and Son marks Antichrist (2:22–23); possible shame at the rapture if disobedient (2:28). [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/1_John.pdf|McGee on 1 John 2:15–28]]

## Agreements and tensions

- Henry and McGee agree on advocacy, obedience/love tests, and not loving the world.
- McGee’s three-sense *kosmos*, Antichrist catalog, and rapture-shame note are outline distinctives—hold beside Henry, not as sole vault word.
- Complete deepens Concise; McGee is radio outline.
