# Psalm 80

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 80: The psalmist complains of the miseries of the church. (1–7). Its former prosperity and present desolation. (8–16). A prayer for mercy. (17–19).

## Core claims

- He that dwelleth upon the mercy-seat, is the good Shepherd of his people. But we can neither expect the comfort of his love, nor the protection of his arm, unless we partake of his converting grace. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-80#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 80]]
- The church is represented as a vine and a vineyard. The root of this vine is Christ, the branches are believers. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-80#Verses 8–16|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 80]]
- The Messiah, the Protector and Saviour of the church, is the Man of God's right hand; he is the Arm of the Lord, for all power is given to him. In him is our strength, by which we are enabled to persevere to the end. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-80#Verses 17–19|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 80]]
