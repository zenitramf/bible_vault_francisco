# Ezekiel 30

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 30: A prophecy against Egypt. (1-19). Another. (20-26).

## Core claims

- The prophecy of the destruction of Egypt is very full. Those who take their lot with God's enemies, shall be with them in punishment. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-30#Verses 1-19|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 30]]
- Egypt shall grow weaker and weaker. If lesser judgments do not prevail to humble and reform sinners, God will send greater. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-30#Verses 20-26|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 30]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
