## Core claims

- In the figurative language of Scripture, the blowing of the four winds together, means a dreadful and general destruction. But the destruction is delayed. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-7#Verses 1–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 7]]
- The first fruits of Christ having led the way, the Gentiles converted later follow, and ascribe their salvation to God and the Redeemer, with triumph. In acts of religious worship we come nigh to God, and must come by Christ; the throne of God could not be approached by sinners, were it not for a Mediator. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-7#Verses 9–12|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 7]]
- Faithful Christians deserve our notice and respect; we should mark the upright. Those who would gain knowledge, must not be ashamed to seek instruction from any who can give it. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-7#Verses 13–17|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 7]]
- The things contained in this chapter came in after the opening of the six seals, which foretold great calamities in the world; and before the sound of the seven trumpets, which gave notice of great corruptions arising… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-6/revelation/chapter-7|Matthew Henry Complete on Revelation 7]]
