# 2 Samuel 4

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Samuel 4: Ishbosheth murdered. (1–7). David puts to death the murderers. (8–12).

## Core claims

- See how Ishbosheth was murdered! When those difficulties dispirit us, which should sharpen our endeavours, we betray both our heavenly crowns and our earthly lives. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-samuel/chapter-4#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Samuel 4]]
- A person may be glad to obtain his just wishes, and yet really regret the means by which he receives them. He may be sorry for the death of a person by which he is a gainer. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-samuel/chapter-4#Verses 8–12|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Samuel 4]]
- When Abner was slain David was at a loss for a friend to perfect the reduction of those tribes that were yet in Ish-bosheth's interest. Which way to adopt for the accomplishment of it he could not tell; but here… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/2-samuel/chapter-4|Matthew Henry Complete on 2 Samuel 4]]
