# Psalm 132

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 132: David's care for the ark. (1–10). The promises of God. (11–18).

## Core claims

- David bound himself to find a place for the Lord, for the ark, the token of God's presence. When work is to be done for the Lord, it is good to tie ourselves to a time. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-132#Verses 1–10|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 132]]
- The Lord never turns from us when we plead the covenant with his anointed Prophet, Priest, and King. How vast is the love of God to man, that he should speak thus concerning his church! [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-132#Verses 11–18|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 132]]
