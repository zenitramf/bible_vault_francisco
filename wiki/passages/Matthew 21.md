# Matthew 21

## Summary

Triumphal entry, temple cleansing, fruitless profession judged, and the rejected Son of the vineyard owner.

## Core claims

- The triumphal entry fulfills prophecy; hosannas greet the meek King. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-21#Verses 1–11|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 21]]
- The temple is cleansed; prayer and praise belong there; fruitless profession is cursed like the fig tree. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-21#Verses 12–17|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 21]]
- Authority questioned is answered by John’s baptism dilemma and the parable of two sons. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-21#Verses 23–27|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 21]]
- Wicked husbandmen kill the son; the kingdom is given to a people producing fruits. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-21#Verses 33–46|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 21]]
- The death and resurrection of Jesus Christ are the two main hinges upon which the door of salvation turns.
