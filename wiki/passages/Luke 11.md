## Core claims

- “Lord, teach us to pray,” is a good prayer, and a very needful one, for Jesus Christ only can teach us, by his word and Spirit, how to pray. Lord, teach me what it is to pray; Lord, stir up and quicken me to the duty; Lord, direct me what to pray for; teach me what I should say. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/luke/chapter-11#Verses 1–4|Matthew Henry Concise on Luke 11]]
- Christ encourages fervency and constancy in prayer. We must come for what we need, as a man does to his neighbour or friend, who is kind to him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/luke/chapter-11#Verses 5–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Luke 11]]
- Christ's thus casting out the devils, was really the destroying of their power. The heart of every unconverted sinner is the devil's palace, where he dwells, and where he rules. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/luke/chapter-11#Verses 14–26|Matthew Henry Concise on Luke 11]]
- While the scribes and Pharisees despised and blasphemed the discourses of our Lord Jesus, this good woman admired them, and the wisdom and power with which he spake. Christ led the woman to a higher consideration. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/luke/chapter-11#Verses 27, 28|Matthew Henry Concise on Luke 11]]
- We should all look to our hearts, that they may be cleansed and new-created; and while we attend to the great things of the law and of the gospel, we must not neglect the smallest matter God has appointed.
