# Ezra 10

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezra 10: Ezra encourages to reformation. (1–5). He assembles the people. (6–14). Reformation effected. (15–44).

## Core claims

- Shechaniah owned the national guilt. The case is sad, but it is not desperate; the disease threatening, but not incurable. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezra/chapter-10#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezra 10]]
- There is hope concerning people, when they are convinced, not only that it is good to part with their sins, but that it is necessary; we must do it, or we are undone. - The best reformers can but do their endeavour; when the Redeemer himself shall come to Zion, he shall effectually turn away ungodliness from Jacob. And when sin is repented of and forsaken, God will forgive it; but the blood of Christ, our Sin-offering, is the only atonement which takes away our guilt.
