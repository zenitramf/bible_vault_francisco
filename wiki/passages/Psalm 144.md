# Psalm 144

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 144: David acknowledges the great goodness of God, and prays for help. (1–8). He prays for the prosperity of his kingdom. (9–15).

## Core claims

- When men become eminent for things as to which they have had few advantages, they should be more deeply sensible that God has been their Teacher. Happy those to whom the Lord gives that noblest victory, conquest and dominion over their own spirits. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-144#Verses 1–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 144]]
- Fresh favours call for fresh returns of thanks; we must praise God for the mercies we hope for by his promise, as well as those we have received by his providence. To be saved from the hurtful sword, or from wasting sickness, without deliverance from the dominion of sin and the wrath to come, is but a small advantage. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-144#Verses 9–15|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 144]]
