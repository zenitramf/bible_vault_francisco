## Core claims

- Every part of the truth and will of God should be set before all who profess the gospel, and be urged on their hearts and consciences. We should not be always speaking about outward things; these have their places and use, but often take up too much attention and time, which might be better employed. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/hebrews/chapter-6#Verses 1–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Hebrews 6]]
- There are things that are never separated from salvation; things that show the person to be in a state of salvation, and which will end in eternal salvation. And the things that accompany salvation, are better things than ever any dissembler or apostate enjoyed. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/hebrews/chapter-6#Verses 9, 10|Matthew Henry Concise on Hebrews 6]]
- The hope here meant, is a sure looking for good things promised, through those promises, with love, desire, and valuing of them. Hope has its degrees, as faith also. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/hebrews/chapter-6#Verses 11–20|Matthew Henry Concise on Hebrews 6]]
