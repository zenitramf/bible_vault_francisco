# Zechariah 5

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Zechariah 5: The vision of a flying roll. (1–4). The vision of a woman and an ephah. (5–11).

## Core claims

- The Scriptures of the Old and New Testament are rolls, in which God has written the great things of his law and gospel; they are flying rolls. God's word runs very swiftly, Ps 147:15. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/zechariah/chapter-5#Verses 1–4|Matthew Henry Concise on Zechariah 5]]
- In this vision the prophet sees an ephah, something in the shape of a corn measure. This betokened the Jewish nation. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/zechariah/chapter-5#Verses 5–11|Matthew Henry Concise on Zechariah 5]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
