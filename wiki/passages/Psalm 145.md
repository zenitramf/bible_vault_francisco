# Psalm 145

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 145: David extols the power, goodness, and mercy of the Lord. (1–9). The glory of God's kingdom, and his care of those that love him. (10–21).

## Core claims

- Those who, under troubles and temptations, abound in fervent prayer, shall in due season abound in grateful praise, which is the true language of holy joy. Especially we should speak of God's wondrous work of redemption, while we declare his greatness. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-145#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 145]]
- All God's works show forth his praises. He satisfies the desire of every living thing, except the unreasonable children of men, who are satisfied with nothing. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-145#Verses 10–21|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 145]]
