# Acts 1

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Acts 1: Proofs of Christ's resurrection. (1–5). Christ's ascension. (6–11). The apostles unite in prayer. (12–14). Matthias chosen in the place of Judas. (15–26). McGee’s Notes & Outlines add post-resurrection ministry emphasis, personal Acts 1:8 commission, Shekinah-cloud ascension, and the claim that Saul—not Matthias—is Judas’s Spirit-chosen successor.

## Agreements and tensions

Complete commentary on this chapter deepens the Concise atlas with fuller pastoral and doctrinal exposition (Phase 4.1). McGee agrees on waiting for the Spirit, ascension, and resurrection witness; he differs from Henry’s cautionary tone on the kingdom question by treating restoration to Israel as certain and not foolish, and he uniquely rejects Matthias as Judas’s lasting successor—hold as single-author outline opinion.
