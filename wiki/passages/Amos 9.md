# Amos 9

## Summary

Vision of inescapable judgment and sieve-dispersion without utter destruction closes in the raising of David’s fallen booth and permanent planting in the land. Henry reads gospel gathering of those called by Christ’s name; McGee reads worldwide dispersion literally fulfilled and 9:11–15 (quoted in Acts 15:16–17) as yet-future millennial kingdom.

## Core claims

- The prophet, in vision, saw the Lord standing upon the idolatrous altar at Bethel. Wherever sinners flee from God's justice, it will overtake them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/amos/chapter-9#Verses 1–10|Matthew Henry Concise on Amos 9]]
- Christ died to gather together the children of God that were scattered abroad, here said to be those who were called by his name. The Lord saith this, who doeth this, who can do it, who has determined to do it, the power of whose grace is engaged for doing it. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/amos/chapter-9#Verses 11–15|Matthew Henry Concise on Amos 9]]
- In this chapter we have, I. Judgment threatened, which the sinners shall not escape (Am 9:1-4), which an almighty power shall inflict (Am 9:5,6), which the people of Israel have deserved as a sinful people (Am 9:7,8);… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/amos/chapter-9|Matthew Henry Complete on Amos 9]]
- McGee: Israel would not be utterly destroyed (9:8); would be dispersed throughout the world (v. 9); the wicked judged by the sword (v. 10)—literally fulfilled and being fulfilled. Verse 11 was quoted by James at the Jerusalem council (Acts 15:16–17) and is yet to be fulfilled; vv. 12–15 are the setting up of the millennial kingdom. [[sources/personal-notes/ingest/Amos-Obadiah.pdf|McGee Notes & Outlines on Amos 9]]

## Agreements and tensions

Complete deepens Concise (Phase 4.1). Material tension: Henry applies 9:11–15 toward gospel gathering in Christ; McGee holds a yet-future millennial restoration after Acts 15 citation. Both affirm non-utter-destruction and divine restoration purpose—keep both readings linked, not collapsed.
