# Leviticus 20

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 20: Law against sacrificing children to Moloch, Of children that curse their parents. (1–9). Laws repeated, Holiness enjoined. (10–27).

## Core claims

- Are we shocked at the unnatural cruelty of the ancient idolaters in sacrificing their children? We may justly be so. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/leviticus/chapter-20#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 20]]
- These verses repeat what had been said before, but it was needful there should be line upon line. What praises we owe to God that he has taught the evil of sin, and the sure way of deliverance from it! [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/leviticus/chapter-20#Verses 10–27|Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 20]]
