# Numbers 29

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 29: The offering at the feats of trumpets, and on the day of atonement. (1–11). Offerings at the feast of tabernacles. (12–40).

## Core claims

- There were more sacred solemnities in the seventh month than in any other. It was the space between harvest and seed-time. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-29#Verses 1–11|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 29]]
- Soon after the day of atonement, the day in which men were to afflict their souls, followed the feast of Tabernacles, in which they were to rejoice before the Lord. Their days of rejoicing were to be days of sacrifices. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-29#Verses 12–40|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 29]]
- This chapter appoints the offerings that were to be made by fire unto the Lord in the three great solemnities of the seventh month. I. In the feast of trumpets on the first day of that month, Nu 29:1-6. II. In the day… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/numbers/chapter-29|Matthew Henry Complete on Numbers 29]]
