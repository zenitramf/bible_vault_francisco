# 1 Chronicles 28

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Chronicles 28: David exhorts the people to the fear of the Lord. (1–10). He gives instructions for the temple. (11–21).

## Core claims

- During David's last sickness, many chief priests and Levites were at Jerusalem. Finding himself able, David spoke of his purpose to build a temple for God, and of God's disallowing that purpose. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-chronicles/chapter-28#Verses 1–10|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Chronicles 28]]
- The temple must be a sacred thing, and a type of Christ; it must be framed by Divine teaching.
