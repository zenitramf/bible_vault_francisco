# Genesis 17

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 17: God renews the covenant with Abram. (1–6). Circumcision instituted. (7–14). Sarai's name changed, Isaac promised. (15–22). Abraham and his family are circumcised. (23–27).

## Core claims

- The covenant was to be accomplished in due time. The promised Seed was Christ, and Christians in him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-17#Verses 1–6|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 17]]
- Here is the promise made to Abraham of a son by Sarai, in whom the promise made to him should be fulfilled. The assurance of this promise was the change of Sarai's name into Sarah. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-17#Verses 15–22|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 17]]
- Abraham and all his family were circumcised; so receiving the token of the covenant, and distinguishing themselves from other families that had no part nor lot in the matter. It was an implicit obedience; he did as God said unto him, and did not ask why or wherefore. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-17#Verses 23–27|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 17]]
- This chapter contains articles of agreement covenanted and concluded upon between the great Jehovah, the Father of mercies, on the one part, and pious Abram, the father of the faithful, on the other part.
