# Matthew 18

## Summary

Childlike greatness, care for little ones, church discipline, and limitless forgiveness among brethren.

## Core claims

- Greatness in the kingdom is childlike humility; woe to those who cause little ones to stumble. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-18#Verses 1–6|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 18]]
- Church discipline proceeds step by step; where two or three gather in his name, he is present. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-18#Verses 15–20|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 18]]
- Forgiveness must be unlimited among brethren; the unmerciful servant is condemned. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-18#Verses 21–35|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 18]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later deepening should add Matthew Henry Complete on the same chapter and Spurgeon where he treats the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
