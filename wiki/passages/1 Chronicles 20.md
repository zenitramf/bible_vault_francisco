# 1 Chronicles 20

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Chronicles 20: David's wars. (1-8).

## Core claims

- Though the Lord will severely correct the sins of his believing people, he will not leave them in the hands of their enemies. His assistance will overcome all advantages of number and strength of those that defy his Israel.
