# 1 Chronicles 8

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Chronicles 8: Genealogies. (1-40).

## Core claims

- Here is a larger list of Benjamin's tribe. We may suppose that many things in these genealogies, which to us seem difficult, abrupt, and perplexed, were plain and easy at that time, and fully answered the intention for which they were published.
