# Isaiah 48

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 48: The Jews reproved for their idolatry. (1-8). Yet deliverance is promised them. (9-15). Solemn warnings of judgment upon those who persisted in evil. (16-22).

## Core claims

- The Jews valued themselves on descent from Jacob, and used the name of Jehovah as their God. They prided themselves respecting Jerusalem and the temple, yet there was no holiness in their lives. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-48#Verses 1-8|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 48]]
- We have nothing ourselves to plead with God, why he should have mercy upon us. It is for his praise, to the honour of his mercy, to spare. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-48#Verses 9-15|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 48]]
- The Holy Spirit qualifies for service; and those may speak boldly, whom God and his Spirit send. This is to be applied to Christ. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-48#Verses 16-22|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 48]]
- God, having in the foregoing chapter reckoned with the Babylonians, and shown them their sins and the desolation that was coming upon them for their sins, to show that he hates sin wherever he finds it and will not… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/isaiah/chapter-48|Matthew Henry Complete on Isaiah 48]]
