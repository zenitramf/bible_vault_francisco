# 1 Samuel 22

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Samuel 22: David at Adullam, Many resort to him. (1–5). Saul destroys the priests of Nob. (6–19). Abiathar escapes to David. (20–23).

## Core claims

- See what weak instruments God sometimes uses, to bring about his own purposes. The Son of David is ready to receive distressed souls, who will be commanded by him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-samuel/chapter-22#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Samuel 22]]
- See the nature of jealous malice and its pitiful arts. Saul looks upon all about him as his enemies, because they do not just say as he says. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-samuel/chapter-22#Verses 6–19|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Samuel 22]]
- David greatly lamented the calamity. It is great trouble to a good man to find himself any way the cause of evil to others. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-samuel/chapter-22#Verses 20–23|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Samuel 22]]
- David, being driven from Achish, returns into the land of Israel to be hunted by Saul. I.
