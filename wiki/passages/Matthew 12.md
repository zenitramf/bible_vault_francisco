# Matthew 12

## Summary

Sabbath mercy, the Spirit’s work, warnings about blasphemy and empty reform, and true kinship in doing the Father’s will.

## Core claims

- Mercy is preferred to sacrifice; the Son of Man is Lord of the sabbath. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-12#Verses 1–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 12]]
- Jesus is the chosen Servant who will not break the bruised reed; Gentiles hope in his name. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-12#Verses 14–21|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 12]]
- Blasphemy against the Holy Spirit is a fearful warning; a tree is known by its fruit. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-12#Verses 22–30|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 12]]
- An evil generation seeks signs; the sign of Jonah points to death and rising; empty reform without Christ is worse. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-12#Verses 38–45|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 12]]
- Those who do the Father’s will are Christ’s true family. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-12#Verses 46–50|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 12]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later deepening should add Matthew Henry Complete on the same chapter and Spurgeon where he treats the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
