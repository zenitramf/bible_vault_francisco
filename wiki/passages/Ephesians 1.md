## Core claims

- All Christians must be saints; if they come not under that character on earth, they will never be saints in glory. Those are not saints, who are not faithful, believing in Christ, and true to the profession they make of relation to their Lord. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ephesians/chapter-1#Verses 1, 2|Matthew Henry Concise on Ephesians 1]]
- Spiritual and heavenly blessings are the best blessings; with which we cannot be miserable, and without which we cannot but be so. - Blessings were made known to believers, by the Lord's showing to them the mystery of his sovereign will, and the method of redemption and salvation. But these must have been for ever hidden from us, if God had not made them known by his written word, preached gospel, and Spirit of truth. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ephesians/chapter-1#Verses 9–14|Matthew Henry Concise on Ephesians 1]]
- God has laid up spiritual blessings for us in his Son the Lord Jesus; but requires us to draw them out and fetch them in by prayer. Even the best Christians need to be prayed for: and while we hear of the welfare of Christian friends, we should pray for them.
