# Numbers 24

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 24: Balaam, leaving divinations, prophesies the happiness of Israel. (1–9). Balak dismisses Balaam in anger. (10–14). Balaam's prophecies. (15–25).

## Core claims

- Now Balaam spake not his own sense, but the language of the Spirit that came upon him. Many have their eyes open who have not their hearts open; are enlightened, but not sanctified. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-24#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 24]]
- This vain attempt to curse Israel is ended. Balak broke out into a rage against Balaam, and expressed great vexation. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-24#Verses 10–14|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 24]]
- Under the powerful influence of the Spirit of prophecy, Balaam foretold the future prosperity and extensive dominion of Israel. Balaam boasts that his eyes are open. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-24#Verses 15–25|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 24]]
- This chapter continues and concludes the history of the defeat of the counsels of Balak and Balaam against Israel, not by might, nor by power, but by the Spirit of the Lord of hosts; and as great an instance it is of… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/numbers/chapter-24|Matthew Henry Complete on Numbers 24]]
