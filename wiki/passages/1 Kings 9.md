# 1 Kings 9

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Kings 9: God's answer to Solomon. (1–9). The presents of Solomon and Hiram. (10–14). Solomon's buildings, His trade. (15–28).

## Core claims

- God warned Solomon, now he had newly built and dedicated the temple, that he and his people might not be high-minded, but fear. After all the services we can perform, we stand upon the same terms with the Lord as before. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-kings/chapter-9#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Kings 9]]
- Solomon gave Hiram twenty cities. Hiram did not like them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-kings/chapter-9#Verses 10–14|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Kings 9]]
- Here is a further account of Solomon's greatness. He began at the right end, for he built God's house first, and finished that before he began his own; then God blessed him, and he prospered in all his other buildings. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-kings/chapter-9#Verses 15–28|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Kings 9]]
