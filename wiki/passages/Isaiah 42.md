# Isaiah 42

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 42: The character and coming of Christ. (1-4). The blessings of his kingdom. (5-12). The prevalence of true religion. (13-17). Unbelief and blindness reproved. (18-25).

## Core claims

- This prophecy was fulfilled in Christ, Matt. 12:17. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-42#Verses 1-4|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 42]]
- The Lord will appear in his power and glory. He shall cry, in the preaching of his word. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-42#Verses 13-17|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 42]]
- Observe the call given to this people, and the character given of them. Multitudes are ruined for want of observing that which they cannot but see; they perish, not through ignorance, but carelessness. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-42#Verses 18-25|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 42]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
