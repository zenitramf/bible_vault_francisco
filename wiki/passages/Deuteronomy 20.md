# Deuteronomy 20

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Deuteronomy 20: Exhortation and proclamation respecting those who went to war. (1–9). Peace to be offered, What cities were to be devoted. (10–20).

## Core claims

- In the wars wherein Israel engaged according to the will of God, they might expect the Divine assistance. The Lord was to be their only confidence. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/deuteronomy/chapter-20#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Deuteronomy 20]]
- The Israelites are here directed about the nations on whom they made war. Let this show God's grace in dealing with sinners. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/deuteronomy/chapter-20#Verses 10–12|Matthew Henry Concise on Deuteronomy 20]]
- This chapter settles the militia, and establishes the laws and ordinances of war, I. Relating to the soldiers. 1. Those must be encouraged that were drawn up to battle, De 20:1-4. 2. Those must be dismissed and sent… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/deuteronomy/chapter-20|Matthew Henry Complete on Deuteronomy 20]]
