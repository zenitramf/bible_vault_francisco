# Leviticus 26

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 26: Promises upon keeping the precepts. (1–13). Threatenings against disobedience. (14–39). God promises to remember those that repent. (40–46).

## Core claims

- This chapter contains a general enforcement of all the laws given by Moses; by promises of reward in case of obedience, on the one hand; and threatenings of punishment for disobedience, on the other. While Israel maintained a national regard to God's worship, sabbaths, and sanctuary, and did not turn aside to… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/leviticus/chapter-26#Verses 1–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 26]]
- After God has set the blessing before them which would make them a happy people if they would be obedient, he here sets the curse before them, the evils which would make them miserable, if they were disobedient. Two things would bring ruin. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/leviticus/chapter-26#Verses 14–39|Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 26]]
- Among the Israelites, persons were not always prosperous or afflicted according to their obedience or disobedience. But national prosperity was the effect of national obedience, and national judgments were brought on by national wickedness. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/leviticus/chapter-26#Verses 40–46|Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 26]]
- This chapter is a solemn conclusion of the main body of the levitical law.
