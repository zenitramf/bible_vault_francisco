# Joshua 19

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 19: The lot of Simeon. (1–9). The lot of Zebulun. (10–16). The lot of Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and Dan. (17–51).

## Core claims

- The men of Judah did not oppose taking away the cities within their border, when convinced that they had more than was right. If a true believer has obtained an unintended and improper advantage in any thing, he will give it up without murmuring. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-19#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 19]]
- In the division to each tribe of Israel, the prophetic blessings of Jacob were fulfilled. They chose for themselves, or it was divided to them by lot, in the manner and places that he foresaw. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-19#Verses 10–16|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 19]]
- Joshua waited till all the tribes were settled, before he asked any provision for himself. He was content to be unfixed, till he saw them all placed, and herein is an example to all in public places, to prefer the common welfare before private advantage. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-19#Verses 17–51|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 19]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
