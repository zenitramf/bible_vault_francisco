## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 4: The furniture of the temple. (1-22).

## Core claims

- Here is a further account of the furniture of God's house. Both without doors and within, there was that which typified the grace of the gospel, and shadowed out good things to come, of which the substance is Christ.
