# Psalm 20

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 20: This psalm is a prayer for the kings of Israel, but with relation to Christ. (1-9).

## Core claims

- Even the greatest of men may be much in trouble. Neither the crown on the king's head, nor the grace in his heart, would make him free from trouble. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-20#Verses 1-9|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 20]]
