# Ezekiel 25

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 25: Judgments against the Ammonites. (1-7). Against the Moabites, Edomites, and Philistines. (8-17).

## Core claims

- It is wicked to be glad at the calamities of any, especially of God's people; it is a sin for which he will surely reckon. God will make it appear that he is the God of Israel, though he suffers them for a time to be captives in Babylon. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-25#Verses 1-7|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 25]]
- Though one event seem to the righteous and wicked, it is vastly different. Those who glory in any other defence and protection than the Divine power, providence, and promise, will, sooner or later, be ashamed of their glorying. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-25#Verses 8-17|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 25]]
- Judgment began at the house of God, and therefore with them the prophets began, who were the judges; but it must not end there, and therefore they must not.
