# 2 Samuel 8

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Samuel 8: David subdues the Philistines, the Moabites, and the Syrians. (1–8). The spoil dedicated. (9–14). David's government and officers. (15–18).

## Core claims

- David subdued the Philistines. They had long been troublesome to Israel. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-samuel/chapter-8#Verses 1–8|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Samuel 8]]
- All the precious things David was master of, were dedicated things; they were designed for building the temple. The idols of gold David destroyed, 2Sa 5:21, but the vessels of gold he dedicated. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-samuel/chapter-8#Verses 9–14|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Samuel 8]]
- David neither did wrong, nor denied or delayed right to any. This speaks his close application to business; also his readiness to admit all addresses and appeals made to him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-samuel/chapter-8#Verses 15–18|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Samuel 8]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
