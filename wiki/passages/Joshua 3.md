# Joshua 3

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 3: The Israelites come to Jordan. (1–6). The Lord encourages joshua—Joshua encourages the people. (7–13). The Israelites pass through Jordan on dry land. (14–17).

## Core claims

- The Israelites came to Jordan in faith, having been told that they should pass it. In the way of duty, let us proceed as far as we can, and depend on the Lord. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-3#Verses 1–6|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 3]]
- The waters of Jordan shall be cut off. This must be done in such a way as never was done, but in the dividing of the Red sea. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-3#Verses 7–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 3]]
- Jordan overflowed all its banks. This magnified the power of God, and his kindness to Israel. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-3#Verses 14–17|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 3]]
- This chapter, and that which follows it, give us the history of Israel's passing through Jordan into Canaan, and a very memorable history it is. Long afterwards, they are told to remember what God did for them between… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/joshua/chapter-3|Matthew Henry Complete on Joshua 3]]
