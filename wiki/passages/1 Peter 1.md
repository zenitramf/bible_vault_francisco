# 1 Peter 1

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Peter 1: The apostle blesses God for his special benefits through Christ. (1–9). Salvation by Christ foretold in ancient prophecy. (10–12). All are exhorted to holy conversation. (13–16). Such as is suitable to their principles. McGee’s Notes & Outlines add a hope-in-trial frame: Trinity in salvation (1:2), living hope and guarded inheritance (1:3–5), joy under refining trials as the epistle’s keynote (1:6–9), prophets’ Cross/Crown peaks with the church-age valley between (1:10–12), and holiness grounded in costly blood redemption and the living Word (1:13–25).

## Agreements and tensions

Complete commentary on this chapter deepens the Concise atlas with fuller pastoral and doctrinal exposition (Phase 4.1). McGee agrees with Henry on Trinitarian salvation, lively hope, prophetic inquiry into sufferings and glories, and the holiness call; he adds word-count theme framing (hope/suffering), the Cross/Crown mountain-peak scheme, and a Scofield-quoted logical order of omniscience → election → foreknowledge that should be held beside other sources. Concise notes and McGee outlines are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
