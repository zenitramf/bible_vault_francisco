# Isaiah 15

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 15: The Divine judgments about to come upon the Moabites. (1-9).

## Core claims

- This prophecy coming to pass within three years, would confirm the prophet's mission, and the belief in all his other prophecies. Concerning Moab it is foretold, 1. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-15#Verses 1-9|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 15]]
- This chapter, and that which follows it, are the burden of Moab—a prophecy of some great desolation that was coming upon that country, which bordered upon this land of Israel, and had often been injurious and vexatious… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/isaiah/chapter-15|Matthew Henry Complete on Isaiah 15]]
