# 2 Chronicles 16

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 16: Asa seeks the aid of the Syrians, His death. (1-14).

## Core claims

- A plain and faithful reproof was given to Asa by a prophet of the Lord, for making a league with Syria. God is displeased when he is distrusted, and when an arm of flesh is relied on, more than his power and goodness.
