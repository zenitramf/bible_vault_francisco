# 2 Chronicles 24

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 24: Joash, of Judah, The temple repaired. (1–14). Joash falls into idolatry, He is slain by his servants. (15–27).

## Core claims

- Joash is more zealous about the repair of the temple than Jehoiada himself. It is easier to build temples, than to be temples to God. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-24#Verses 1–14|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 24]]
- See what a great judgment on any prince or people, the death of godly, zealous, useful men is. See how necessary it is that we act in religion from inward principle. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-24#Verses 15–27|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 24]]
