# Proverbs 5

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 5: Exhortations to wisdom. The evils of licentiousness. (1–14). Remedies against licentiousness, The miserable end of the wicked. (15–23).

## Core claims

- Solomon cautions all young men, as his children, to abstain from fleshly lusts. Some, by the adulterous woman, here understand idolatry, false doctrine, which tends to lead astray men's minds and manners; but the direct view is to warn against seventh-commandment sins. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-5#Verses 1–14|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 5]]
- Lawful marriage is a means God has appointed to keep from these destructive vices. But we are not properly united, except as we attend to God's word, seeking his direction and blessing, and acting with affection. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-5#Verses 15–23|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 5]]
