# 2 Kings 24

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 24: Jehoiakim subdued by Nebuchadnezzar. (1–7). Jehoiachim captive in Babylon. (8–20).

## Core claims

- If Jehoiakim had served the Lord, he had not been servant to Nebuchadnezzar. If he had been content with his servitude, and true to his word, his condition had been no worse; but, rebelling against Babylon, he plunged himself into more trouble. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-kings/chapter-24#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 24]]
- Jehoiachin reigned but three months, yet long enough to show that he justly smarted for his fathers' sins, for he trod in their steps. His uncle was intrusted with the government. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-kings/chapter-24#Verses 8–20|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 24]]
- Things are here ripening for, and hastening towards, the utter destruction of Jerusalem. We left Jehoiakim on the throne, placed there by the king of Egypt: now here we have, I.
