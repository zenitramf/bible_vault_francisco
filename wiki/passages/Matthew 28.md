# Matthew 28

## Summary

Resurrection morning, appearances, the soldiers’ lie, and the Great Commission under all authority with promised presence.

## Core claims

- Christ rises the third day; the first day light breaks as at creation. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-28#Verses 1–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 28]]
- He meets the women with unexpected grace; they worship and are sent as messengers. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-28#Verses 9, 10|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 28]]
- Soldiers are bribed to lie; money advances falsehood while truth is grudged support. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-28#Verses 11–15|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 28]]
- All authority is given to the risen Lord; disciples make disciples of all nations, baptizing and teaching, with his presence to the end of the age. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-28#Verses 16–20|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 28]]
- In the foregoing chapters, we saw the Captain of our salvation engaged with the powers of darkness, attacked by them, and vigorously attacking them; victory seemed to hover between the combatants; nay, at length, it… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-5/matthew/chapter-28|Matthew Henry Complete on Matthew 28]]
