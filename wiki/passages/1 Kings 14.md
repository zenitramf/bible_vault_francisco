# 1 Kings 14

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Kings 14: Abijah being sick, his mother consults Ahijah. (1–6). The destruction of Jeroboam's house. (7–20). Rehoboam's wicked reign. (21–31).

## Core claims

- “At that time,” when Jeroboam did evil, his child sickened. When sickness comes into our families, we should inquire whether there may not be some particular sin harboured in our houses, which the affliction is sent to convince us of, and reclaim us from. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-kings/chapter-14#Verses 1–6|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Kings 14]]
- Whether we keep an account of God's mercies to us or not, he does; and he will set them in order before us, if we are ungrateful, to our greater confusion. Ahijah foretells the speedy death of the child then sick, in mercy to him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-kings/chapter-14#Verses 7–20|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Kings 14]]
- Here is no good said of Rehoboam, and much said to the disadvantage of his subjects.
