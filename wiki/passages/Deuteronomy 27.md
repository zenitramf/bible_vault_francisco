# Deuteronomy 27

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Deuteronomy 27: The law to be written on stones in the promised land. (1–10). The curses to be pronounced on mount Ebal. (11–26).

## Core claims

- As soon as they were come into Canaan, they must set up a monument, on which they must write the words of this law. They must set up an altar. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/deuteronomy/chapter-27#Verses 1–10|Matthew Henry Concise on Deuteronomy 27]]
- The six tribes appointed for blessing, were all children of the free women, for to such the promise belongs, Ga 4:31. Levi is here among the rest. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/deuteronomy/chapter-27#Verses 11–26|Matthew Henry Concise on Deuteronomy 27]]
- Moses having very largely and fully set before the people their duty, both to God and one another, in general and in particular instances,—having shown them plainly what is good, and what the law requires of them,—and… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/deuteronomy/chapter-27|Matthew Henry Complete on Deuteronomy 27]]
