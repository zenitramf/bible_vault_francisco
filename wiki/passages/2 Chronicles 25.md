# 2 Chronicles 25

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 25: Amaziah, king of Judah. (1–13). Amaziah worships the idols of Edom. (14–16). Amaziah's rash challenge. (17–28).

## Core claims

- Amaziah was no enemy to religion, but cool and indifferent friend. Many do what is good, but not with a perfect heart. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-25#Verses 1–13|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 25]]
- To worship the gods of those whom Amaziah had conquered, who could not help their own worshippers, was the greatest absurdity. If men would consider how unable all those things are to help them, to which they look whenever they forsake God, they would not be such enemies to themselves. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-25#Verses 14–16|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 25]]
- Never was a proud prince more thoroughly mortified than Amaziah by Joash king of Israel. A man's pride will bring him low, Pr 29:23; it goes before his destruction, and deservedly brings it on. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-25#Verses 17–28|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 25]]
- Amaziah's reign, recorded in this chapter, was not one of the worse and yet for from good. Most of the passages in this chapter we had before more briefly related, 2Ki 14:1-22 Here we find Amaziah, I. A just revenger… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/2-chronicles/chapter-25|Matthew Henry Complete on 2 Chronicles 25]]
