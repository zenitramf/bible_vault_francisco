# Hosea 6

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 6: An exhortation to repentance. (1-3). Israel's instability and breach of the covenant. (4-11).

## Core claims

- Those who have gone from God by consent, and in a body, drawing one another to sin, should, by consent and in a body, return to him, which will be for his glory, and their good. It will be of great use for support under afflictions, and to encourage our repentance, to keep up good thoughts of God, and of his purposes… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/hosea/chapter-6#Verses 1-3|Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 6]]
- Sometimes Israel and Judah seemed disposed to repent under their sufferings, but their goodness vanished like the empty morning cloud, and the early dew, and they were as vile as ever. Therefore the Lord sent awful messages by the prophets. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/hosea/chapter-6#Verses 4-11|Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 6]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
