## Core claims

- We cannot forget how often, while their Master was with them there were strifes among the disciples which should be the greatest; but now all these strifes were at an end. They had prayed more together of late. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/acts/chapter-2#Verses 1–4|Matthew Henry Concise on Acts 2]]
- The difference in languages which arose at Babel, has much hindered the spread of knowledge and religion. The instruments whom the Lord first employed in spreading the Christian religion, could have made no progress without this gift, which proved that their authority was from God.
