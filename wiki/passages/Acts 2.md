# Acts 2

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Acts 2: The descent of the Holy Spirit at the day of Pentecost. (1–4). The apostles speak in divers languages. (5–13). Peter's address to the Jews. (14–36). Three thousand souls converted. (37–41). The piety and affection of the primitive church. McGee thickens Pentecost as church origin (“Bethlehem of the Holy Spirit”), Spirit baptism into the body, filling vs baptism order, known-language tongues, partial Joel “this is that,” and Christ/resurrection as sermon center.

## Agreements and tensions

Complete commentary deepens the Concise atlas. McGee and Henry agree tongues aided gospel spread in many languages and that Peter centers Jesus; McGee’s hard church-birthday line, Spirit-ministry order, and non-fulfillment of Joel are outline distinctives to compare with broader fulfillment readings.
