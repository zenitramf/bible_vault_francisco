# Joshua 22

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 22: Reuben and Gad, with the half tribe of Manasseh, dismissed to their homes. (1–9). They build an altar of testimony, The congregation offended thereat. (10–20). The answer of the Reubenites. (21–29). The children of Israel satisfied. (30–34).

## Core claims

- Joshua dismisses the tribes with good counsel. Those who have the commandment have it in vain, unless they do the commandment; and it will not be done aright unless we take diligent heed.
