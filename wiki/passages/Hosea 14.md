# Hosea 14

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 14: An exhortation to repentance. (1-3). Blessings promised, showing the rich comforts of the gospel. (4-8). The just and the wicked. (9).

## Core claims

- Israel is exhorted to return unto Jehovah, from their sins and idols, by faith in his mercy, and grace through the promised Redeemer, and by diligently attending on his worship and service. Take away iniquity; lift it off as a burden we are ready to sink under, or as the stumbling-block we have often fallen over. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/hosea/chapter-14#Verses 1-3|Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 14]]
- Israel seeks God's face, and they shall not seek it in vain. His anger is turned from them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/hosea/chapter-14#Verses 4-8|Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 14]]
- Who profit by the truths the prophet delivered? Such as set themselves to understand and know these things. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/hosea/chapter-14#Verse 9|Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 14]]
- The strain of this chapter differs from that of the foregoing chapters.
