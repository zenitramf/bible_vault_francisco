# Job 39

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Job 39: God inquires of Job concerning several animals. (1-30).

## Core claims

- —In these questions the Lord continued to humble Job. In this chapter several animals are spoken of, whose nature or situation particularly show the power, wisdom, and manifold works of God. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/job/chapter-39#Verses 1-30|Matthew Henry Concise on Job 39]]
- God proceeds here to show Job what little reason he had to charge him with unkindness who was so compassionate to the inferior creatures and took such a tender care of them, or to boast of himself, and his own good… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-3/job/chapter-39|Matthew Henry Complete on Job 39]]
