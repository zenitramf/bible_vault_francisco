# Psalm 29

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 29: Exhortation to give glory to God. (1-11).

## Core claims

- The mighty and honourable of the earth are especially bound to honour and worship him; but, alas, few attempt to worship him in the beauty of holiness. When we come before him as the Redeemer of sinners, in repentance faith, and love, he will accept our defective services, pardon the sin that cleaves to them, and… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-29#Verses 1-11|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 29]]
- It is the probable conjecture of some very good interpreters that David penned this psalm upon occasion, and just at the time, of a great storm of thunder, lightning, and rain, as the eighth psalm was his meditation in… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-3/psalms/psalm-29|Matthew Henry Complete on Psalm 29]]
- Title — A Psalm of David. The title affords us no information beyond the fact that David is the author of this sublime song. [[sources/commentaries_english/chspurgeon-tod/volume-2/psalm-29|Treasury of David, Psalm 29]]
