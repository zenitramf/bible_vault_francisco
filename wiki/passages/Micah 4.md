# Micah 4

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Micah 4: The peace of the kingdom of Christ. (1–8). The judgments to come upon Jerusalem, but the final triumph of Israel. (9–13).

## Core claims

- The nations have not yet so submitted to the Prince of Peace, as to beat their swords into ploughshares, nor has war ceased. But very precious promises these are, relating to the gospel church, which will be more and more fulfilled, for He is faithful that has promised. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/micah/chapter-4#Verses 1–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Micah 4]]
- Many nations would assemble against Zion to rejoice in her calamities. They would not understand that the Lord had collected them as sheaves are gathered to be threshed; and that Zion would be strengthened to beat them to pieces. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/micah/chapter-4#Verses 9–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Micah 4]]
- Comparing this chapter with the close of the foregoing chapter, the comfortable promises here with the terrible threatenings there, we may, with the apostle, "behold the goodness and severity of God," (Ro 11:22),… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/micah/chapter-4|Matthew Henry Complete on Micah 4]]
