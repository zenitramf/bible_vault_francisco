* [Matthew 1](Matthew%201.md) - Matthew Henry Concise presents Matthew 1 as establishing Jesus’ messianic lineage and the humble incarnation of God with us through Joseph’s obedience to the angelic word.
* [Matthew 10](Matthew%2010.md) - The Twelve are sent with instructions for mission, suffering, fearless confession, and the reward of receiving Christ’s messengers.
* [Matthew 11](Matthew%2011.md) - Evidence for John and the Messiah, woes on unrepentant cities, and the gentle invitation to the weary frame the chapter’s appeal.
* [Matthew 12](Matthew%2012.md) - Sabbath mercy, the Spirit’s work, warnings about blasphemy and empty reform, and true kinship in doing the Father’s will.
* [Matthew 13](Matthew%2013.md) - Kingdom parables explain varied reception of the word, mixed growth until harvest, costly treasure, and Nazareth’s unbelief.
* [Matthew 14](Matthew%2014.md) - John’s death, the feeding of five thousand, walking on water, and healings at Gennesaret show both human cruelty and divine compassion.
* [Matthew 15](Matthew%2015.md) - Heart defilement vs tradition, Gentile faith, and another feeding of multitudes extend grace beyond Israel’s boundaries of custom.
* [Matthew 16](Matthew%2016.md) - Confession of Christ, the church’s foundation, the necessity of the cross, and the call to self-denying discipleship.
* [Matthew 17](Matthew%2017.md) - Transfiguration glory, faith for the demonized boy, passion prediction, and temple-tax freedom under the Son.
* [Matthew 18](Matthew%2018.md) - Childlike greatness, care for little ones, church discipline, and limitless forgiveness among brethren.
* [Matthew 19](Matthew%2019.md) - Marriage design, blessing children, the rich young ruler, and the impossibility of self-salvation versus God’s power.
* [Matthew 2](Matthew%202.md) - Matthew 2 traces Gentile seekers who worship the Child, providential flight to Egypt, Herod’s cruelty, and return to Nazareth under divine guidance.
* [Matthew 20](Matthew%2020.md) - Grace in the vineyard, servant leadership after the Son of Man, and mercy that opens blind eyes.
* [Matthew 21](Matthew%2021.md) - Triumphal entry, temple cleansing, fruitless profession judged, and the rejected Son of the vineyard owner.
* [Matthew 22](Matthew%2022.md) - Marriage feast, civil and divine dues, resurrection hope, the double love command, and David’s Lord.
* [Matthew 23](Matthew%2023.md) - Woes on scribes and Pharisees for hypocrisy, and Jesus’ lament over Jerusalem that would not be gathered.
* [Matthew 24](Matthew%2024.md) - Temple destruction foreshadowed, signs and tribulations, the certain coming of the Son of Man, and the call to watch.
* [Matthew 25](Matthew%2025.md) - Virgins, talents, and the sheep-and-goats judgment press readiness, faithful work, and merciful love.
* [Matthew 26](Matthew%2026.md) - Plot, anointing, betrayal, Passover supper, Gethsemane, arrest, trial, and Peter’s denial unfold the passion’s beginning.
* [Matthew 27](Matthew%2027.md) - Judas’s end, Pilate’s compromise, crucifixion, atoning darkness, riven veil, burial, and sealed tomb.
* [Matthew 28](Matthew%2028.md) - Resurrection morning, appearances, the soldiers’ lie, and the Great Commission under all authority with promised presence.
* [Matthew 3](Matthew%203.md) - John the Baptist prepares the way with repentance preaching; Jesus’ baptism displays Trinitarian witness and his commitment to fulfill all righteousness.
