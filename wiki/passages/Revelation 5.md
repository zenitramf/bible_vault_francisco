# Revelation 5

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 5: A book sealed with seven seals, which could be opened by none but Christ, who took the book to open it. (1–7). Upon which all honour is ascribed to him, as worthy to open it. (8–14).

## Core claims

- The apostle saw in the hand of Him that sat upon the throne, a roll of parchments in the form usual in those times, and sealed with seven seals. This represented the secret purposes of God about to be revealed. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-5#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 5]]
- It is matter of joy to all the world, to see that God deals with men in grace and mercy through the Redeemer. He governs the world, not merely as a Creator, but as our Saviour. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-5#Verses 8–14|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 5]]
- In the foregoing chapter the prophetical scene was opened, in the sight and hearing of the apostle, and he had a sight of God the Creator and ruler of the world, and the great King of the church.
