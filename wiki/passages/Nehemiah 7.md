# Nehemiah 7

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Nehemiah 7: The city committed to Hananiah. (1–4). Register of those that first returned. (5–73).

## Core claims

- Nehemiah, having finished the wall, returned to the Persian court, and came to Jerusalem again with a new commission. The public safety depends on every one's care to guard himself and his family against sin. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/nehemiah/chapter-7#Verses 1–4|Matthew Henry Concise on Nehemiah 7]]
- Nehemiah knew that the safety of a city, under God, depends more upon the inhabitants than upon its walls. Every good gift and every good work are from above. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/nehemiah/chapter-7#Verses 5–73|Matthew Henry Concise on Nehemiah 7]]
- The success of one good design for God and our generation should encourage us to proceed and form some other; Nehemiah did so, having fortified Jerusalem with gates and walls, his next care is, I. To see the city well… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/nehemiah/chapter-7|Matthew Henry Complete on Nehemiah 7]]
