# 2 Kings 19

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 19: Hezekiah receives an answer of peace. (1–7). Sennacherib's letter. (8–19). His fall is prophesied. (20–34). The Assyrian army destroyed, Sennacherib slain. (35–37).

## Core claims

- Hezekiah discovered deep concern at the dishonour done to God by Rabshakeh's blasphemy. Those who speak from God to us, we should in a particular manner desire to speak to God for us. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-kings/chapter-19#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 19]]
- All Sennacherib's motions were under the Divine cognizance. God himself undertakes to defend the city; and that person, that place, cannot but be safe, which he undertakes to protect. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-kings/chapter-19#Verses 20–34|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 19]]
- That night which followed the sending of this message to Hezekiah, the main body of their army was slain. See how weak the mightiest men are before Almighty God. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-kings/chapter-19#Verses 35–37|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 19]]
- Jerusalem's great distress we read of in the foregoing chapter, and left it besieged, insulted, threatened, terrified, and just ready to be swallowed up by the Assyrian army.
