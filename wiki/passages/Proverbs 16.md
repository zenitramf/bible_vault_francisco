## Core claims

- The renewing grace of God alone prepares the heart for every good work. This teaches us that we are not sufficient of ourselves to think or speak any thing wise and good. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-16#Verse 1|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 16]]
- A sincerely religious man keeps at a distance from every appearance of evil. Happy is the man that walks in Christ, and is led by the Spirit of Christ. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-16#Verse 17|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 16]]
- All the disposal of Providence concerning our affairs, we must look upon to be the determining what we referred to God; and we must be reconciled to them accordingly. Blessed are those that give themselves up to the will of God; for he knows what is good for them.
