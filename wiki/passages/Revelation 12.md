## Core claims

- The church, under the emblem of a woman, the mother of believers, was seen by the apostle in vision, in heaven. She was clothed with the sun, justified, sanctified, and shining by union with Christ, the Sun of Righteousness. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-12#Verses 1–6|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 12]]
- The attempts of the dragon proved unsuccessful against the church, and fatal to his own interests. The seat of this war was in heaven; in the church of Christ, the kingdom of heaven on earth. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-12#Verses 7–11|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 12]]
- The church and all her friends might well be called to praise God for deliverance from pagan persecution, though other troubles awaited her. The wilderness is a desolate place, and full of serpents and scorpions, uncomfortable and destitute of provisions; yet a place of safety, as well as where one might be alone. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-12#Verses 12–17|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 12]]
