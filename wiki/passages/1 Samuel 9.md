# 1 Samuel 9

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Samuel 9: Saul is brought to Samuel. (1–10). Samuel told concerning Saul. (11–17). Samuel's treatment of Saul. (18–27).

## Core claims

- Saul readily went to seek his father's asses. His obedience to his father was praise-worthy. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-samuel/chapter-9#Verses 1–10|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Samuel 9]]
- The very maid-servants of the city could direct to the prophet. They had heard of the sacrifice, and could tell of the necessity for Samuel's presence. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-samuel/chapter-9#Verses 11–17|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Samuel 9]]
- Samuel, that good prophet, was so far from envying Saul, or bearing him any ill-will, that he was the first and most forward to do him honour. Both that evening and early the next morning, Samuel communed with Saul upon the flat roof of the house. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-samuel/chapter-9#Verses 18–27|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Samuel 9]]
- Samuel had promised Israel, from God, that they should have a king; it is strange that the next news is not of candidates setting up for the government, making an interest in the people, or recommending themselves to… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/1-samuel/chapter-9|Matthew Henry Complete on 1 Samuel 9]]
