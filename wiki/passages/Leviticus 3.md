# Leviticus 3

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 3: The peace-offering of the herd. (1–5). The peace-offering of the flock. (6–17).

## Core claims

- The peace-offerings had regard to God as the giver of all good things. These were divided between the altar, the priest, and the owner. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/leviticus/chapter-3#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 3]]
- Here is a law that they should eat neither fat nor blood. As for the fat, it means the fat of the inwards, the suet. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/leviticus/chapter-3#Verses 6–17|Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 3]]
- In this chapter we have the law concerning the peace-offerings, whether they were, I. Of the heard, a bullock or a heifer, Le 3:1-5. Or, II. Of the flock, either a lamb (Le 3:6-11) or a goat, Le 3:12-17. The ordinances… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/leviticus/chapter-3|Matthew Henry Complete on Leviticus 3]]
