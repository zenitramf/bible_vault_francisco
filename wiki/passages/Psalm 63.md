# Psalm 63

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 63: David's desire toward God. (1, 2). His satisfaction in God. (3–6). His dependence upon God, and assurance of safety. (7–11).

## Core claims

- Early will I seek thee. The true Christian devotes to God the morning hour. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-63#Verses 1, 2|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 63]]
- Even in affliction we need not want matter for praise. When this is the regular frame of a believer's mind, he values the loving-kindness of God more than life. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-63#Verses 3–6|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 63]]
- True Christians can, in some measure, and at some times, make use of the strong language of David, but too commonly our souls cleave to the dust. Having committed ourselves to God, we must be easy and pleased, and quiet from the fear of evil. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-63#Verses 7–11|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 63]]
