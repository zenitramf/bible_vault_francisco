# Jeremiah 1

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 1: Jeremiah's call to the prophetic office. (1-10). A vision of an almond-tree and of a seething-pot, Divine protection is promised. (11-19).

## Core claims

- Jeremiah's early call to the work and office of a prophet is stated. He was to be a prophet, not to the Jews only, but to the neighbouring nations. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-1#Verses 1-10|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 1]]
- God gave Jeremiah a view of the destruction of Judah and Jerusalem by the Chaldeans. The almond-tree, which is more forward in the spring than any other, represented the speedy approach of judgments. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-1#Verses 11-19|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 1]]
