# Esther 8

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Esther 8: Mordecai is advanced. (1, 2). Esther makes suit for the Jews. (3–14). Mordecai honoured, The joy of the Jews. (15–17).

## Core claims

- What Haman would have done mischief with, Esther will do good with. All the trust the king had reposed in Haman, he now placed in Mordecai: a happy change. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/esther/chapter-8#Verses 1, 2|Matthew Henry Concise on Esther 8]]
- It was time to be earnest, when the church of God was at stake. Esther, though safe herself, fell down and begged for the deliverance of her people. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/esther/chapter-8#Verses 3–14|Matthew Henry Concise on Esther 8]]
- Mordecai's robes now were rich. These things are not worth notice, but as marks of the king's favour, and the fruit of God's favour to his church. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/esther/chapter-8#Verses 15–17|Matthew Henry Concise on Esther 8]]
- We left the plotter hanging, and are now to see what becomes of his plot. I. His plot was to raise an estate for himself; and all his estate, being confiscated for treason, is given to Esther and Mordecai, Es 8:1,2.… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/esther/chapter-8|Matthew Henry Complete on Esther 8]]
