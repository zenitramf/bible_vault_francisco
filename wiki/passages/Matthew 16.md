# Matthew 16

## Summary

Confession of Christ, the church’s foundation, the necessity of the cross, and the call to self-denying discipleship.

## Core claims

- Beware the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees—corrupt doctrine. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-16#Verses 1–4|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 16]]
- Peter confesses Jesus as the Christ, the Son of the living God; the church is built on this rock of confession. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-16#Verses 13–20|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 16]]
- Christ must suffer, be killed, and rise; rebuking that path is satanic thinking of men, not God. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-16#Verses 21–23|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 16]]
- Discipleship means self-denial, taking the cross, and following him; the soul’s value outweighs the world. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-16#Verses 24–28|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 16]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later deepening should add Matthew Henry Complete on the same chapter and Spurgeon where he treats the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
