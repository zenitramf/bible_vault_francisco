# Amos 4

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Amos 4: Israel is reproved. (1–5). Their impenitence shown. (6–13).

## Core claims

- What is got by extortion is commonly used to provide for the flesh, and to fulfil the lusts thereof. What is got by oppression cannot be enjoyed with satisfaction. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/amos/chapter-4#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on Amos 4]]
- See the folly of carnal hearts; they wander from one creature to another, seeking for something to satisfy, and labour for that which satisfies not; yet, after all, they will not incline their ear to Him in whom they might find all they can want. Preaching the gospel is as rain, and every thing withers where this… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/amos/chapter-4#Verses 6–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Amos 4]]
