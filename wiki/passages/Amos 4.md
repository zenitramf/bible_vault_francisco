# Amos 4

## Summary

Israel is reproved for oppression and empty calf-worship; past chastisements (famine, insects, pestilence, war) did not turn them. Henry stresses extortion and carnal wandering; McGee hears biting sarcasm inviting them to transgress what they called worship of the golden calf, and notes none of the past judgments deterred them from sin.
