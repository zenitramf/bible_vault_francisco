# John 1

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on John 1: The Divinity of Christ. (1–5). His Divine and human nature. (6–14). John the Baptist's testimony to Christ. (15–18). John's public testimony concerning Christ. (19–28). Other testimonies of John concerning Christ. (29….

## Core claims

- The plainest reason why the Son of God is called the Word, seems to be, that as our words explain our minds to others, so was the Son of God sent in order to reveal his Father's mind to the world. What the evangelist says of Christ proves that he is God. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/john/chapter-1#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on John 1]]
- John the Baptist came to bear witness concerning Jesus. Nothing more fully shows the darkness of men's minds, than that when the Light had appeared, there needed a witness to call attention to it. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/john/chapter-1#Verses 6–14|Matthew Henry Concise on John 1]]
- As to the order of time and entrance on his work, Christ came after John, but in every other way he was before him. The expression clearly shows that Jesus had existence before he appeared on earth as man. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/john/chapter-1#Verses 15–18|Matthew Henry Concise on John 1]]
- John disowns himself to be the Christ, who was now expected and waited for. He came in the spirit and power of Elias, but he was not the person of Elias. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/john/chapter-1#Verses 19–28|Matthew Henry Concise on John 1]]
- See the nature of true Christianity, it is following Jesus; devoting ourselves to him, and treading in his steps. Observe the objection Nathanael made. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/john/chapter-1#Verses 43–51|Matthew Henry Concise on John 1]]
