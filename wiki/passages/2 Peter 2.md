## Core claims

- Though the way of error is a hurtful way, many are always ready to walk therein. Let us take care we give no occasion to the enemy to blaspheme the holy name whereby we are called, or to speak evil of the way of salvation by Jesus Christ, who is the Way, the Truth, and the Life. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-peter/chapter-2#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Peter 2]]
- Impure seducers and their abandoned followers, give themselves up to their own fleshly minds. Refusing to bring every thought to the obedience of Christ, they act against God's righteous precepts. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-peter/chapter-2#Verses 10–16|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Peter 2]]
- The word of truth is the water of life, which refreshes the souls that receive it; but deceivers spread and promote error, and are set forth as empty, because there is no truth in them. As clouds hinder the light of the sun, so do these darken counsel by words wherein there is no truth. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-peter/chapter-2#Verses 17–22|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Peter 2]]
