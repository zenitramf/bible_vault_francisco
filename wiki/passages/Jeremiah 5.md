# Jeremiah 5

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 5: The Jews' profession of religion was hypocritical. (1-9). The cruel proceedings of their enemies. (10-18). Their apostacy and idolatry. (19-31).

## Core claims

- None could be found who behaved as upright and godly men. But the Lord saw the true character of the people through all their disguises. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-5#Verses 1-9|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 5]]
- Multitudes are ruined by believing that God will not be so strict as his word says he will; by this artifice Satan undid mankind. Sinners are not willing to own any thing to be God's word, that tends to part them from, or to disquiet them in, their sins. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-5#Verses 10-18|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 5]]
- Unhumbled hearts are ready to charge God with being unjust in their afflictions. But they may read their sin in their punishment. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-5#Verses 19-31|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 5]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
