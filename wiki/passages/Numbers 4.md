# Numbers 4

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 4: The Levites' service. (1–3). The duties of the Kohathites. (4–20). The duties of the Gershonites and Merarites. (21–33). The numbers of the serviceable Levites. (34–49).

## Core claims

- The middle-aged men of the tribe of Levi, all from thirty years old to fifty, were to be employed in the service of the tabernacle. The service of God requires the best of our strength, and the prime portion of our time, which cannot be better spent than to the honour of Him who is the First and Best. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-4#Verses 1–3|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 4]]
- We have here the charge of the other two families of the Levites, which, though not so honourable as the first, yet was necessary, and to be done regularly. All the things were delivered them by name. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-4#Verses 21–33|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 4]]
- God so ordered it, that though the Merarites were the fewest in number, yet they should have most able men among them; for whatever service God calls men to, he will furnish them for it, give strength in proportion to the work, and grace sufficient.
