# 2 Chronicles 7

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 7: God's answer to Solomon's prayer. (1-22). McGee stresses fire accepting the sacrifices, temple activity, God’s appearance with conditions of blessing in the land, and 7:14 as having direct reference to Israel.

## Core claims

- God gave a gracious answer to Solomon's prayer. The mercies of God to sinners are made known in a manner well suited to impress all who receive them, with his majesty and holiness. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-7#Verses 1-22|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 7]]
- In this chapter we have God's answer to Solomon's prayer. I. His public answer by fire from heaven, which consumed the sacrifices (2Ch 7:1), with which the priests and people were much affected, 2Ch 7:2,3. By that… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/2-chronicles/chapter-7|Matthew Henry Complete on 2 Chronicles 7]]
- McGee: God accepts the sacrifices (v. 1) and the temple becomes a beehive of activity (v. 6); God appears to Solomon and gives the condition of blessing upon Israel in the land; v. 14 has direct reference to Israel. [[sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/1-2_Chronicles.pdf.extract.md|McGee Notes & Outlines on 1–2 Chronicles]] [[wiki/source-notes/McGee Notes and Outlines on 1 and 2 Chronicles|McGee Chronicles source-note]]

## Agreements and tensions

Complete commentary on this chapter deepens the Concise atlas with fuller pastoral and doctrinal exposition (Phase 4.1).

McGee and Henry agree on gracious public answer by fire and the gravity of God’s conditions after dedication. McGee’s insistence that 7:14 applies directly to Israel is a dispensational outline claim—record as authorial application, not the vault’s sole hermeneutic of the verse.

Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
