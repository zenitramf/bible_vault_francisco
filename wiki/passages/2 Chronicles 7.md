# 2 Chronicles 7

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 7: God's answer to Solomon's prayer. (1-22).

## Core claims

- God gave a gracious answer to Solomon's prayer. The mercies of God to sinners are made known in a manner well suited to impress all who receive them, with his majesty and holiness.
