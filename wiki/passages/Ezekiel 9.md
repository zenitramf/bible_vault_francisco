# Ezekiel 9

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 9: A vision denoting the destruction of the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and the departure of the symbol of the Divine presence. (1-11).

## Core claims

- It is a great comfort to believers, that in the midst of destroyers and destructions, there is a Mediator, a great High Priest, who has an interest in heaven, and in whom saints on earth have an interest. The representation of the Divine glory from above the ark, removed to the threshold, denoted that the Lord was… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-9#Verses 1-4|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 9]]
- The slaughter must begin at the sanctuary, that all may see and know that the Lord hates sin most in those nearest to him. He who was appointed to protect, reported the matter. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-9#Verses 5-11|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 9]]
