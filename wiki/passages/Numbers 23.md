# Numbers 23

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 23: Balak's sacrifice, Balaam pronounces a blessing instead of a curse. (1–10). Balak's disappointment, and second sacrifice, Balaam again blesses Israel. (11–30).

## Core claims

- With the camps of Israel full in view, Balaam ordered seven altars to be built, and a bullock and a ram to be offered on each. Oh the sottishness of superstition, to imagine that God will be at man's beck! [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-23#Verses 1–10|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 23]]
- Balak was angry with Balaam. Thus a confession of God's overruling power is extorted from a wicked prophet, to the confusion of a wicked prince. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-23#Verses 11–30|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 23]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
