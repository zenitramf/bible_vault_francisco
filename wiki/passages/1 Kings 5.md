# 1 Kings 5

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Kings 5: Solomon's agreement with Hiram. (1–9). Solomon's workmen for the temple. (10–18).

## Core claims

- Here is Solomon's design to build a temple. There is no adversary, no Satan, so the word is; no instrument of Satan to oppose it, or to divert from it. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-kings/chapter-5#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Kings 5]]
- The temple was chiefly built by the riches and labour of Gentiles, which typified their being called into the church. Solomon commanded, and they brought costly stones for the foundation. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-kings/chapter-5#Verses 10–18|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Kings 5]]
- The great work which Solomon was raised up to do was the building of the temple; his wealth and wisdom were given him to qualify him for that.
