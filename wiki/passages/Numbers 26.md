# Numbers 26

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 26: Numbering of Israel in the plains of Moab. (1–51). The division of the land. (52–56). Number of the Levites. (57–62). None remaining of the first numbering. (63–65).

## Core claims

- Moses did not number the people but when God commanded him. We have here the families registered, as well as the tribes. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-26#Verses 1–51|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 26]]
- Levi was God's tribe; therefore it was not numbered with the rest, but alone. It came not under the sentence, that none of them should enter Canaan excepting Caleb and Joshua. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-26#Verses 57–62|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 26]]
- The execution of the sentence passed on the murmurers, chap. 14:29, is observable. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-26#Verses 63–65|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 26]]
- This book is called Numbers, from the numberings of the children of Israel, of which it gives an account. Once they were numbered at Mount Sinai, in the first year after they came out of Egypt, which we had an account… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/numbers/chapter-26|Matthew Henry Complete on Numbers 26]]
