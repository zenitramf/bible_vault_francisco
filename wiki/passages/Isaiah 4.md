# Isaiah 4

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 4: The havoc occasioned by war. (1). The times of the Messiah. (2-6).

## Core claims

- This first verse belongs to the third chapter. When the troubles should come upon the land, as the unmarried state was deemed reproachful among the Jews, these women would act contrary to common usage, and seek husbands for themselves. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-4#Verses 1|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 4]]
- Not only the setting forth Christ's kingdom in the times of the apostles, but its enlargement by gathering the dispersed Jews into the church, is foretold. Christ is called the Branch of the Lord, being planted by his power, and flourishing to his praise. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-4#Verses 2-6|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 4]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
