# Jeremiah 2

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 2: God expostulates with his people. (1-8). Their revolt beyond example. (9-13). Guilt the cause of sufferings. (14-19). The sins of Judah. (20-28). Their false confidence. (29-37).

## Core claims

- Those who begin well, but do not persevere, will justly be upbraided with their hopeful and promising beginnings. Those who desert religion, commonly oppose it more than those who never knew it. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-2#Verses 1-8|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 2]]
- Is Israel a servant? No, they are the seed of Abraham. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-2#Verses 14-19|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 2]]
- The nation had not been wrought upon by the judgements of God, but sought to justify themselves. The world is, to those who make it their home and their portion, a wilderness and a land of darkness; but those who dwell in God, have the lines fallen to them in pleasant places.
