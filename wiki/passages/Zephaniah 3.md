# Zephaniah 3

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Zephaniah 3: Further reproofs for sin. (1–7). Encouragement to look for mercy. (8–13). Promises of future favour and prosperity. (14–20).

## Core claims

- The holy God hates sin most in those nearest to him. A sinful state is, and will be, a woful state. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/zephaniah/chapter-3#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Zephaniah 3]]
- The preaching of the gospel is predicted, when vengeance would be executed on the Jewish nation. The purifying doctrines of the gospel, or the pure language of the grace of the Lord, would teach men to use the language of humility, repentance, and faith. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/zephaniah/chapter-3#Verses 8–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Zephaniah 3]]
- After the promises of taking away sin, follow promises of taking away trouble. When the cause is removed, the effect will cease. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/zephaniah/chapter-3#Verses 14–20|Matthew Henry Concise on Zephaniah 3]]
- We now return to Jerusalem, and must again hear what God has to say to her, I.
