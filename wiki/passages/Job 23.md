# Job 23

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Job 23: Job complains that God has withdrawn. (1–7). He asserts his own integrity. (8–12). The Divine terrors. (13–17).

## Core claims

- Job appeals from his friends to the just judgement of God. He wants to have his cause tried quickly. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/job/chapter-23#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Job 23]]
- Job knew that the Lord was every where present; but his mind was in such confusion, that he could get no fixed view of God's merciful presence, so as to find comfort by spreading his case before him. His views were all gloomy. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/job/chapter-23#Verses 8–12|Matthew Henry Concise on Job 23]]
- As Job does not once question but that his trials are from the hand of God, and that there is no such thing as chance, how does he account for them?
