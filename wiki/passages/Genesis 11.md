# Genesis 11

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 11: One language in the world, The building of Babel. (1–4). The confusion of tongues, The builders of Babel dispersed. (5–9). The descendants of Shem. (10–26). Terah, father of Abram, grandfather of Lot, they remove to Haran. (27–32).

## Core claims

- How soon men forget the most tremendous judgments, and go back to their former crimes! Though the desolations of the deluge were before their eyes, though they sprang from the stock of righteous Noah, yet even during his life-time, wickedness increases exceedingly. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-11#Verses 1–4|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 11]]
- Here is a genealogy, or list of names, ending in Abram, the friend of God, and thus leading towards Christ, the promised Seed, who was the son of Abram. Nothing is left upon record but their names and ages; the Holy Ghost seeming to hasten through them to the history of Abram. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-11#Verses 10–26|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 11]]
- Here begins the story of Abram, whose name is famous in both Testaments. Even the children of Eber had become worshippers of false gods. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-11#Verses 27–32|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 11]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
