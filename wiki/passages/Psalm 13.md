# Psalm 13

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 13: The psalmist complains that God had long withdrawn. He earnestly prays for comfort. He assures himself of an answer of peace. (1-6).

## Core claims

- God sometimes hides his face, and leaves his own children in the dark concerning their interest in him: and this they lay to heart more than any outward trouble whatever. But anxious cares are heavy burdens with which believers often load themselves more than they need.
