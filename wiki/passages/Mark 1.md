## Core claims

- Isaiah and Malachi each spake concerning the beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, in the ministry of John. From these prophets we may observe, that Christ, in his gospel, comes among us, bringing with him a treasure of grace, and a sceptre of government. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/mark/chapter-1#Verses 1–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Mark 1]]
- Christ's baptism was his first public appearance, after he had long lived unknown. How much hidden worth is there, which in this world is not known! [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/mark/chapter-1#Verses 9–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Mark 1]]
- Jesus began to preach in Galilee, after that John was put in prison. If some be laid aside, others shall be raised up, to carry on the same work. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/mark/chapter-1#Verses 14–22|Matthew Henry Concise on Mark 1]]
- The devil is an unclean spirit, because he has lost all the purity of his nature, because he acts in direct opposition to the Holy Spirit of God, and by his suggestions defiles the spirits of men.
