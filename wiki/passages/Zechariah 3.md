# Zechariah 3

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Zechariah 3: The restoration of the church. (1–5). A promise concerning the Messiah. (6–10).

## Core claims

- The angel showed Joshua, the high priest, to Zechariah, in a vision. Guilt and corruption are great discouragements when we stand before God. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/zechariah/chapter-3#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on Zechariah 3]]
- All whom God calls to any office he finds fit, or makes so. The Lord will cause the sins of the believer to pass away by his sanctifying grace, and will enable him to walk in newness of life. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/zechariah/chapter-3#Verses 6–10|Matthew Henry Concise on Zechariah 3]]
