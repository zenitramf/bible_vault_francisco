# Joshua 21

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 21: Cities for the Levites. (1–8). The cities allotted to the Levites. (9–42). God gave the land and rest to the Israelites, according to his promise. (43–45).

## Core claims

- The Levites waited till the other tribes were provided for, before they preferred their claim to Joshua. They build their claim upon a very good foundation; not their own merits or services, but the Divine precept. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-21#Verses 1–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 21]]
- By mixing the Levites with the other tribes, they were made to see that the eyes of all Israel were upon them, and therefore it was their concern to walk so that their ministry might not be blamed. Every tribe had its share of Levites' cities. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-21#Verses 9–42|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 21]]
- God promised to give to the seed of Abraham the land of Canaan for a possession, and now they possessed it, and dwelt therein. And the promise of the heavenly Canaan is as sure to all God's spiritual Israel; for it is the promise of Him that cannot lie. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-21#Verses 43–45|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 21]]
