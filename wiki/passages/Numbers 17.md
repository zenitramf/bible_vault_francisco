# Numbers 17

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 17: Twelve rods laid up before the Lord. (1–7). Aaron's rod buds, and is kept for a memorial. (8–13).

## Core claims

- It is an instance of the grace of God, that, having wrought divers miracles to punish sin, he would work one more to prevent it. Twelve rods or staves were to be brought in. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-17#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 17]]
- While all the other rods remained as they were. Aaron's rod became a living branch. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-17#Verses 8–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 17]]
- Enough had been done in the chapter before to quash all pretensions of the families of the tribe of Levi that would set up in competition with Aaron, and to make it appear that Aaron was the head of the tribe; but it… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/numbers/chapter-17|Matthew Henry Complete on Numbers 17]]
