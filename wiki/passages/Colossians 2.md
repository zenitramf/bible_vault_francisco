# Colossians 2

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise frames Colossians 2 as love and joy in believers (1–7); caution against heathen philosophy and Jewish rites fulfilled in Christ (8–17); and warning against angel worship and will-worship (18–23). McGee’s Notes & Outlines structure the chapter as Christ the answer to philosophy (for the head, 2:1–15) and to ritual (for the heart, 2:16–23), with clear *pleroma* deity, baptismal identification, and rejection of ascetic fads.

## Agreements and tensions

- Complete commentary deepens Concise pastoral exposition (Phase 4.1).
- McGee and Henry agree against vain philosophy, creature-mediated access, and will-worship; McGee adds Head/heart outline split, *pleroma* bodily deity slogan, baptism-as-identification with Romans 6, and strong shadow-to-substance reading of ritual.
- “Monkey business” and “pride that apes humility” (Juvenal) are McGee homiletic color—hold as radio outline notes beside Henry’s soberer pastoral warnings.
