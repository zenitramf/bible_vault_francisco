## Core claims

- The soul prospers when we have clear knowledge of the truth as it is in Jesus. When we not only believe with the heart, but are ready, when called, to make confession with the mouth. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/colossians/chapter-2#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Colossians 2]]
- There is a philosophy which rightly exercises our reasonable faculties; a study of the works of God, which leads us to the knowledge of God, and confirms our faith in him.
