# Lamentations 2

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Lamentations 2: Lamentation for the misery of Jerusalem. (1-22).

## Core claims

- A sad representation is here made of the state of God's church, of Jacob and Israel; but the notice seems mostly to refer to the hand of the Lord in their calamities. Yet God is not an enemy to his people, when he is angry with them and corrects them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/lamentations/chapter-2#Verses 1-9|Matthew Henry Concise on Lamentations 2]]
- Causes for lamentation are described. Multitudes perished by famine. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/lamentations/chapter-2#Verses 10-22|Matthew Henry Concise on Lamentations 2]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
