# Psalm 97

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 97: The Lord Jesus reigns in power that cannot be resisted. (1–7). His care of his people, and his provision for them. (8–12).

## Core claims

- Though many have been made happy in Christ, still there is room. And all have reason to rejoice in Christ's government. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-97#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 97]]
- The faithful servants of God may well rejoice and be glad, because he is glorified; and whatever tends to his honour, is his people's pleasure. Care is taken for their safety. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-97#Verses 8–12|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 97]]
- This psalm dwells upon the same subject, and is set to the same tune, with the foregoing psalm.
