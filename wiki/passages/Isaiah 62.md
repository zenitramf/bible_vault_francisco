# Isaiah 62

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 62: God's care of his church and people. (1-5). The office of ministers in preaching the gospel. (6-9). Every hinderance shall be removed from the way of salvation. (10-12).

## Core claims

- The Son of God here assures his church of his unfailing love, and his pleading for her under all trails and difficulties. She shall be called by a new name, a pleasant name, such as she was never called by before. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-62#Verses 1-5|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 62]]
- God's professing people must be a praying people. He is not displeased with us for being earnest, as men commonly are; he bids us to cry after him, and give him no rest, Luke 11:5,6. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-62#Verses 6-9|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 62]]
- Way shall be made for Christ's salvation; all difficulties shall be removed. He brings a reward of comfort and peace with him; but a work of humiliation and reformation before him; and they shall be called, The holy people, and, The redeemed of the Lord. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-62#Verses 10-12|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 62]]
- The business of prophets was both to preach and pray. In this chapter, I. The prophet determines to apply closely and constantly to this business, Isa 62:1. II. God appoints him and others of his prophets to continue… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/isaiah/chapter-62|Matthew Henry Complete on Isaiah 62]]
