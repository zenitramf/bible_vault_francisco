# 1 Chronicles 25

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Chronicles 25: The singers and musicians. (1-31).

## Core claims

- David put those in order who were appointed to be singers and musicians in the temple. To prophesy, in this place, means praising God with great earnestness and devout affections, under the influences of the Holy Spirit.
