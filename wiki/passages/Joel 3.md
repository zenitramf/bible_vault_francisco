# Joel 3

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Joel 3: God's judgments in the latter days. (1-8) . The extent of these judgments. (9-17) . The blessings the church shall enjoy. (18-21).

## Core claims

- The restoration of the Jews, and the final victory of true religion over all opposers, appear to be here foretold. The contempt and scorn with which the Jews have often been treated as a people, and the little value set upon them, are noticed. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joel/chapter-3#Verses 1-8|Matthew Henry Concise on Joel 3]]
- Here is a challenge to all the enemies of God's people. There is no escaping God's judgments; hardened sinners, in that day of wrath, shall be cut off from all comfort and joy. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joel/chapter-3#Verses 9-17|Matthew Henry Concise on Joel 3]]
- There shall be abundant Divine influences, and the gospel will spread speedily into the remotest corners of the earth. These events are predicted under significant emblems; there is a day coming, when every thing amiss shall be amended. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joel/chapter-3#Verses 18-21|Matthew Henry Concise on Joel 3]]
- In the close of the foregoing chapter we had a gracious promise of deliverance in Mount Zion and Jerusalem; now this whole chapter is a comment upon that promise, showing what that deliverance shall be, how it shall be… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/joel/chapter-3|Matthew Henry Complete on Joel 3]]
