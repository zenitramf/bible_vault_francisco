# Ezekiel 37

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 37: God restores dried bones to life. (1-14). The whole house of Israel is represented as enjoying the blessings of Christ's kingdom. (15-28).

## Core claims

- No created power could restore human bones to life. God alone could cause them to live. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-37#Verses 1-14|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 37]]
- This emblem was to show the people, that the Lord would unite Judah and Israel. Christ is the true David, Israel's King of old; and those whom he makes willing in the day of his power, he makes to walk in his judgments, and to keep his statutes. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-37#Verses 15-28|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 37]]
- The threatenings of the destruction of Judah and Jerusalem for their sins, which we had in the former part of this book, were not so terrible, but the promises of their restoration and deliverance for the glory of God,… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/ezekiel/chapter-37|Matthew Henry Complete on Ezekiel 37]]
