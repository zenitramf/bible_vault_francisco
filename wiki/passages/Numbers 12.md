# Numbers 12

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 12: God rebukes the murmuring of Aaron and Miriam. (1–9). Miriam struck with leprosy, and healed at the prayer of Moses. (10–16).

## Core claims

- The patience of Moses was tried in his own family, as well as by the people. The pretence was, that he had married a foreign wife; but probably their pride was hurt, and their envy stirred up, by his superior authority. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-12#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 12]]
- The cloud departed, and Miriam became leprous. When God goes, evil comes: expect no good when God departs. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-12#Verses 10–16|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 12]]
- In the foregoing chapter we had the vexation which the people gave to Moses; in this we have his patience tried by his own relations. I. Miriam and Aaron, his own brother and sister, affronted him, Nu 12:1-3. II. God… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/numbers/chapter-12|Matthew Henry Complete on Numbers 12]]
