# Isaiah 23

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 23: The overthrow of Tyre. (1-14). It is established again. (15-18).

## Core claims

- Tyre was the mart of the nations. She was noted for mirth and diversions; and this made her loth to consider the warnings God gave by his servants. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-23#Verses 1-14|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 23]]
- The desolations of Tyre were not to be for ever. The Lord will visit Tyre in mercy. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-23#Verses 15-18|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 23]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
