# 2 Kings 10

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 10: Ahab's sons and Ahaziah's brethren put to death. (1–14). Jehu destroys the worshippers of Baal. (15–28). Jehu follows Jeroboam's sins. (29–36).

## Core claims

- In the most awful events, though attended by the basest crimes of man, the truth and justice of God are to be noticed; and he never did nor can command any thing unjust or unreasonable. Jehu destroyed all that remained of the house of Ahab; all who had been partners in his wickedness. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-kings/chapter-10#Verses 1–14|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 10]]
- Is thine heart right? This is a question we should often put to ourselves. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-kings/chapter-10#Verses 15–28|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 10]]
- It is justly questionable whether Jehu acted from a good principle, and whether he did not take some false steps in doing it; yet no services done for God shall go unrewarded. But true conversion is not only from gross sin, but from all sin; not only from false gods, but from false worships. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-kings/chapter-10#Verses 29–36|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 10]]
