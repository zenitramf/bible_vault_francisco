# Genesis 41

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 41: Pharaoh's dreams. (1–8). Joseph interprets Pharaoh's dreams. (9–32). Joseph's counsel, He is highly advanced. (33–45). Joseph's children, The beginning of the famine. (46–57).

## Core claims

- The means of Joseph's being freed from prison were Pharaoh's dreams, as here related. Now that God no longer speaks to us in that way, it is no matter how little we either heed dreams, or tell them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-41#Verses 1–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 41]]
- Joseph gave good advice to Pharaoh. Fair warning should always be followed by good counsel. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-41#Verses 33–45|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 41]]
- In the names of his two sons, Manasseh and Ephraim, Joseph owned the Divine providence. 1. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-41#Verses 46–57|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 41]]
