# Numbers 32

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 32: The tribes of Reuben and Gad request an inheritance on the east of Jordan. (1–5). Moses reproves the Reubenites and Gadites. (6–15). They explain their views, Moses consents. (16–27). They take possession of the land to the east of Jordan. (28–42).

## Core claims

- Here is a proposal made by the Reubenites and Gadites, that the land lately conquered might be allotted to them. Two things common in the world might lead these tribes to make this choice; the lust of the eye, and the pride of life. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-32#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 32]]
- Here is the good effect of plain dealing. Moses, by showing their sin, and the danger of it, brought them to their duty, without murmuring or disputing. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-32#Verses 16–27|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 32]]
- Concerning the settlement of these tribes, observe, that they built the cities, that is, repaired them. They changed the names of them; probably they were idolatrous, therefore they should be forgotten. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-32#Verses 28–42|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 32]]
