# Psalm 27

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 27: The psalmist's faith. (1–6). His desire toward God, and expectation from him. (7–14).

## Core claims

- The Lord, who is the believer's light, is the strength of his life; not only by whom, but in whom he lives and moves. In God let us strengthen ourselves. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-27#Verses 1–6|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 27]]
- Wherever the believer is, he can find a way to the throne of grace by prayer. God calls us by his Spirit, by his word, by his worship, and by special providences, merciful and afflicting. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-27#Verses 7–14|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 27]]
- Some think David penned this psalm before his coming to the throne, when he was in the midst of his troubles, and perhaps upon occasion of the death of his parents; but the Jews think he penned it when he was old, upon… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-3/psalms/psalm-27|Matthew Henry Complete on Psalm 27]]
