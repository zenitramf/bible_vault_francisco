# Jeremiah 52

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 52: The fate of Zedekiah. (1-11). The destruction of Jerusalem. (12-23). The captivities. (24-30). The advancement of Jehoiachin. (31-34).

## Core claims

- This fruit of sin we should pray against above any thing; Cast me not away from thy presence, Ps. 51:11. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-52#Verses 1-11|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 52]]
- The leaders of the Jews caused them to err; but now they are, in particular, made monuments of Divine justice. Here is an account of two earlier captivities. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-52#Verses 24-30|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 52]]
- See this history of king Jehoiachin in II Kin. 25:27-30. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-52#Verses 31-34|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 52]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
