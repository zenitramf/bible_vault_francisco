# Psalm 82

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 82: An exhortation to judges. (1–5). The doom of evil rulers. (6–8).

## Core claims

- Magistrates are the mighty in authority for the public good. Magistrates are the ministers of God's providence, for keeping up order and peace, and particularly in punishing evil-doers, and protecting those that do well. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-82#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 82]]
- It is hard for men to have honour put upon them, and not to be proud of it. But all the rulers of the earth shall die, and all their honour shall be laid in the dust. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-82#Verses 6–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 82]]
- This psalm is calculated for the meridian of princes' courts and courts of justice, not in Israel only, but in other nations; yet it was probably penned primarily for the use of the magistrates of Israel, the great… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-3/psalms/psalm-82|Matthew Henry Complete on Psalm 82]]
