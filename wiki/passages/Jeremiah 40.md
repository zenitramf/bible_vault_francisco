# Jeremiah 40

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 40: Jeremiah is directed to go to Gedaliah. (1-6). A conspiracy against Gedaliah. (7-16).

## Core claims

- The captain of the guard seems to glory that he had been God's instrument to fulfil, what Jeremiah had been God's messenger to foretell. Many can see God's justice and truth with regard to others, who are heedless and blind as to themselves and their own sins. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-40#Verses 1-6|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 40]]
- Jeremiah had never in his prophecies spoken of any good days for the Jews, to come immediately after the captivity; yet Providence seemed to encourage such an expectation. But how soon is this hopeful prospect blighted! [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-40#Verses 7-16|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 40]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
