# Ezekiel 36

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 36: The land shall be delivered from heathen oppressors. (1-15). The people are reminded of former sins, and promised deliverance. (16-24). Also holiness, and gospel blessings. (25-38).

## Core claims

- Those who put contempt and reproach on God's people, will have them turned on themselves. God promises favour to his Israel. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-36#Verses 1-15|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 36]]
- The restoration of that people, being typical of our redemption by Christ, shows that the end aimed at in our salvation is the glory of God. The sin of a people defiles their land; renders it abominable to God, and uncomfortable to themselves. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-36#Verses 16-24|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 36]]
- Water is an emblem of the cleansing our polluted souls from sin. But no water can do more than take away the filth of the flesh. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-36#Verses 25-38|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 36]]
- We have done with Mount Seir, and left it desolate, and likely to continue so, and must now turn ourselves, with the prophet, to the mountains of Israel, which we find desolate too, but hope before we have done with… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/ezekiel/chapter-36|Matthew Henry Complete on Ezekiel 36]]
