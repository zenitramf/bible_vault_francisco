# Psalm 43

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 43: David endeavours to still his spirit, with hope and confidence in God. (1-5).

## Core claims

- As to the quarrel God had with David for sin, he prays, Enter not into judgment with me, if Thou doest so I shall be condemned; but as to the quarrel his enemies had with him, he prays, Lord, judge me, and in thy providence appear on my behalf.
