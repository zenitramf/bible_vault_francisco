## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezra 5: The leaders forward the building of the temple. (1, 2). letter against the Jews. (3–17).

## Core claims

- The building of the temple was stopped about fifteen years. Then they had two good ministers, who urged them to go on with the work. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezra/chapter-5#Verses 1, 2|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezra 5]]
- While employed in God's work, we are under his special protection; his eye is upon us for good. This should keep us to our duty, and encourage us therein, when difficulties are ever so discouraging.
