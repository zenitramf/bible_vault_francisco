# Leviticus 21

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 21: Laws concerning the priests. (1-24).

## Core claims

- As these priests were types of Christ, so all ministers must be followers of him, that their example may teach others to imitate the Saviour. Without blemish, and separate from sinners, He executed his priestly office on earth.
