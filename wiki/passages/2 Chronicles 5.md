# 2 Chronicles 5

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 5: The ark placed in the temple. (1–10). The temple filled with glory. (11–14).

## Core claims

- The ark was a type of Christ, and, as such, a token of the presence of God. That gracious promise, Lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world, does, in effect, bring the ark into our religious assemblies, if we by faith and prayer plead that promise; and this we should be most earnest for. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-5#Verses 1–10|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 5]]
- God took possession of the temple; he filled it with a cloud. Thus he signified his acceptance of this temple, to be the same to him that the tabernacle of Moses was, and assured his people that he would be the same in it. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-5#Verses 11–14|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 5]]
- The temple being built and furnished for God, we have here, I. Possession given to him, by bringing in the dedicated things (2Ch 5:1), but especially the ark, the token of his presence, 2Ch 5:2-10.
