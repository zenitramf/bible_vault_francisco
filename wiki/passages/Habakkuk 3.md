# Habakkuk 3

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Habakkuk 3: The prophet beseeches God for his people. (1, 2). He calls to mind former deliverances. (3–15). His firm trust in the Divine mercy. (16–19).

## Core claims

- The word prayer seems used here for an act of devotion. The Lord would revive his work among the people in the midst of the years of adversity. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/habakkuk/chapter-3#Verses 1, 2|Matthew Henry Concise on Habakkuk 3]]
- God's people, when in distress, and ready to despair, seek help by considering the days of old, and the years of ancient times, and by pleading them with God in prayer. - When we see a day of trouble approach, it concerns us to prepare. A good hope through grace is founded in holy fear. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/habakkuk/chapter-3#Verses 16–19|Matthew Henry Concise on Habakkuk 3]]
- Still the correspondence is kept up between God and his prophet.
