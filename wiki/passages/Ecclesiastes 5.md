# Ecclesiastes 5

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ecclesiastes 5: What renders devotion vain. (1-3). Of vows, and oppression. (4-8). the vanity of riches shown. (9-7). The right use of riches. (18-20).

## Core claims

- Address thyself to the worship of God, and take time to compose thyself for it. Keep thy thoughts from roving and wandering: keep thy affections from running out toward wrong objects. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ecclesiastes/chapter-5#Verses 1-3|Matthew Henry Concise on Ecclesiastes 5]]
- The goodness of Providence is more equally distributed than appears to a careless observer. The king needs the common things of life, and the poor share them; they relish their morsel better than he does his luxuries. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ecclesiastes/chapter-5#Verses 9-17|Matthew Henry Concise on Ecclesiastes 5]]
- Life is God's gift. We must not view our calling as a drudgery, but take pleasure in the calling where God puts us. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ecclesiastes/chapter-5#Verses 18-20|Matthew Henry Concise on Ecclesiastes 5]]
- Solomon, in this chapter, discourses, I. Concerning the worship of God, prescribing that as a remedy against all those vanities which he had already observed to be in wisdom, learning, pleasure, honour, power, and… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-3/ecclesiastes/chapter-5|Matthew Henry Complete on Ecclesiastes 5]]
