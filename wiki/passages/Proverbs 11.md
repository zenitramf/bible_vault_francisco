## Core claims

- However men may make light of giving short weight or measure, and however common such crimes may be, they are an abomination to the Lord. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-11#Verse 1|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 11]]
- A cruel, froward, ill-natured man, is vexatious to those that are, and should be to him as his own flesh, and punishes himself. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-11#Verse 17|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 11]]
- Even the righteous, when they offend on earth, shall meet with sharp corrections; much more will the wicked meet the due reward of their sins. Let us then seek those blessings which our Surety purchased by his sufferings and death; let us seek to copy his example, and to keep his commandments.
