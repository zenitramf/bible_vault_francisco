# Jeremiah 43

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 43: The leaders carry the people to Egypt. (1-7). Jeremiah foretells the conquest of Egypt. (8-13).

## Core claims

- Only by pride comes contention, both with God and man. They preferred their own wisdom to the revealed will of God. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-43#Verses 1-7|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 43]]
- God can find his people wherever they are. The Spirit of prophecy was not confined to the land of Israel. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-43#Verses 8-13|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 43]]
- Jeremiah had faithfully delivered his message from God in the foregoing chapter, and the case was made so very plain by it that one would have thought there needed no more words about it; but we find it quite… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/jeremiah/chapter-43|Matthew Henry Complete on Jeremiah 43]]
