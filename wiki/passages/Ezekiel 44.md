# Ezekiel 44

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 44: The Vision of the Temple. (1-31).

## Core claims

- This chapter contains ordinances relative to the true priests. The prince evidently means Christ, and the words in 2, may remind us that no other can enter heaven, the true sanctuary, as Christ did; namely, by virtue of his own excellency, and his personal holiness, righteousness, and strength. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-44#Verses 1-31|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 44]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
