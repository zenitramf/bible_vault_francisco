# 2 Chronicles 23

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 23: Joash crowned, and Athaliah slain. (1-21).

## Core claims

- To look upon ourselves and each other as the Lord's people, should make us earnest in the discharge of our duty both to God and man. Thus was this happy revolution brought about, and the people rejoiced. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-23#Verses 1-21|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 23]]
