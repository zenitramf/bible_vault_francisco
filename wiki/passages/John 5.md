# John 5

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on John 5: The cure at the pool of Bethesda. (1–9). The Jews' displeasure. (10–16). Christ reproves the Jews. (17–23). Christ's discourse. (24–47).

## Core claims

- We are all by nature impotent folk in spiritual things, blind, halt, and withered; but full provision is made for our cure, if we attend to it. An angel went down, and troubled the water; and what disease soever it was, this water cured it, but only he that first stepped in had benefit. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/john/chapter-5#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on John 5]]
- Those eased of the punishment of sin, are in danger of returning to sin, when the terror and restraint are over, unless Divine grace dries up the fountain. The misery believers are made whole from, warns us to sin no more, having felt the smart of sin. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/john/chapter-5#Verses 10–16|Matthew Henry Concise on John 5]]
- The Divine power of the miracle proved Jesus to be the Son of God, and he declared that he worked with, and like unto his Father, as he saw good.
