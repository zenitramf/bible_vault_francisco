# Exodus 8

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 8: The plague of frogs. (1–15). The plague of lice. (16–19). The plague of flies. (20–32).

## Core claims

- Pharaoh is plagued with frogs; their vast numbers made them sore plagues to the Egyptians. God could have plagued Egypt with lions, or bears, or wolves, or with birds of prey, but he chose to do it by these despicable creatures. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-8#Verses 1–15|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 8]]
- These lice were produced out of the dust of the earth; out of any part of the creation God can fetch a scourge, with which to correct those who rebel against him. Even the dust of the earth obeys him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-8#Verses 16–19|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 8]]
- Pharaoh was early at his false devotions to the river; and shall we be for more sleep and more slumber, when any service to the Lord is to be done? The Egyptians and the Hebrews were to be marked in the plague of flies. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-8#Verses 20–32|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 8]]
- Three more of the plagues of Egypt are related in this chapter, I. That of the frogs, which is, 1. Threatened, Ex 8:1-4. 2. Inflicted, Ex 8:5,6. 3. Mimicked by the magicians, Ex 8:7. 4. Removed, at the humble request… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/exodus/chapter-8|Matthew Henry Complete on Exodus 8]]
