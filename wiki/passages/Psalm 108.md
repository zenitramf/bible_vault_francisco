# Psalm 108

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 108: Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 108.

## Core claims

- We may usefully select passages from different psalms, as here, Ps 57; 60, to help our devotions, and enliven our gratitude. When the heart is firm in faith and love, the tongue, being employed in grateful praises, is our glory.
