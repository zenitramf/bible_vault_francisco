# Isaiah 41

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 41: God's care of his people. (1-9). they are encouraged not to fear. (10-20). The vanity and folly of idolatry. (21-29).

## Core claims

- Can any heathen god raise up one in righteousness, make what use of him he pleases, and make him victorious over the nations? The Lord did so with Abraham, or rather, he would do so with Cyrus. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-41#Verses 1-9|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 41]]
- God speaks with tenderness; Fear thou not, for I am with thee: not only within call, but present with thee. Art thou weak? [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-41#Verses 10-20|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 41]]
- There needs no more to show the folly of sin, than to bring to notice the reasons given in defence of it. There is nothing in idols worthy of regard. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-41#Verses 21-29|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 41]]
- This chapter, as the former, in intended both for the conviction of idolaters and for the consolation of all God's faithful worshippers; for the Spirit is sent, and ministers are employed by him, both to convince and… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/isaiah/chapter-41|Matthew Henry Complete on Isaiah 41]]
