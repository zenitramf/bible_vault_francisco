# Job 4

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Job 4: Eliphaz reproves Job. (1–6). And maintains that God's judgments are for the wicked. (7–11). The vision of Eliphaz. (12–21).

## Core claims

- Satan undertook to prove Job a hypocrite by afflicting him; and his friends concluded him to be one because he was so afflicted, and showed impatience. This we must keep in mind if we would understand what passed. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/job/chapter-4#Verses 1–6|Matthew Henry Concise on Job 4]]
- Eliphaz argues, 1. That good men were never thus ruined. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/job/chapter-4#Verses 7–11|Matthew Henry Concise on Job 4]]
- Eliphaz relates a vision. When we are communing with our own hearts, and are still, Ps 4:4, then is a time for the Holy Spirit to commune with us. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/job/chapter-4#Verses 12–21|Matthew Henry Concise on Job 4]]
- Job having warmly given vent to his passion, and so broken the ice, his friends here come gravely to give vent to their judgment upon his case, which perhaps they had communicated to one another apart, compared notes… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-3/job/chapter-4|Matthew Henry Complete on Job 4]]
