# Exodus 17

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 17: The Israelites murmur for water at Rephidim, God sendeth it out of the rock. (1–7). Amalek overcome, The prayers of Moses. (8–16).

## Core claims

- The children of Israel journeyed according to the commandment of the Lord, led by the pillar of cloud and fire, yet they came to a place where there was no water for them to drink. We may be in the way of duty, yet may meet with troubles, which Providence brings us into, for the trial of our faith, and that God may… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-17#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 17]]
- Israel engaged with Amalek in their own necessary defence. God makes his people able, and calls them to various services for the good of his church. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-17#Verses 8–16|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 17]]
