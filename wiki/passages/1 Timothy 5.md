## Core claims

- Respect must be paid to the dignity of years and place. The younger, if faulty, must be rebuked, not as desirous to find fault with them, but as willing to make the best of them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-timothy/chapter-5#Verses 1, 2|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Timothy 5]]
- Honour widows that are widows indeed, relieve them, and maintain them. It is the duty of children, if their parents are in need, and they are able to relieve them, to do it to the utmost of their power. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-timothy/chapter-5#Verses 3–8|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Timothy 5]]
- Every one brought into any office in the church, should be free from just censure; and many are proper objects of charity, yet ought not to be employed in public services.
