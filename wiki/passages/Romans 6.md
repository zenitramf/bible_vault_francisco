# Romans 6

## Summary

Henry Concise presses that free grace does not license sin: justification and holiness are inseparable. Baptism teaches dying to sin and rising to newness of life; believers must reckon themselves dead to sin and alive to God, yield members as instruments of righteousness, and remember that sin’s wages is death while God’s gift is eternal life through Jesus Christ.

## Core claims

- The necessity of holiness is pressed without explaining away free grace; justification and holiness are inseparable. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-6#Verses 1, 2|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 6]]
- Baptism teaches dying to sin, burial from ungodly pursuits, and rising to walk with God in newness of life; outward sign without inward reality is empty. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-6#Verses 3–10|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 6]]
- Believers made free from sin’s reign and alive unto God must not let sin reign in mortal bodies but yield themselves to God. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-6#Verses 11–15|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 6]]
- Everyone is servant of the master to whom he yields; obedience from the heart to gospel teaching marks the new service. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-6#Verses 16–20|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 6]]
- Sin’s end is death; holiness issues in everlasting life. The gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-6#Verses 21–23|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 6]]
