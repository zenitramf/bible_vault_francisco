# Numbers 21

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 21: The Canaanites of Arad destroyed. (1–3). The people murmuring, are plagued with fiery serpents, They repenting, are healed through the brazen serpent. (4–9). Further journeys of the Israelites. (10–20). Sihon and Og overcome, Their land possessed. (21–35).

## Core claims

- Before the people began their march round the country of Edom, the king of Arad, a Canaanite, who inhabited the southern part of the country, attacked them in the wilderness, and took some prisoners. This was to lead the Israelites to look more thoroughly to the Lord. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-21#Verses 1–3|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 21]]
- We have here the removes of the children of Israel, till they came to the plains of Moab, from whence they passed over Jordan into Canaan. The end of their pilgrimage was near. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-21#Verses 10–20|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 21]]
- Sihon went with his forces against Israel, out of his own borders, without provocation, and so ran upon his own ruin. The enemies of God's church often perish by the counsels they think most wisely taken. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-21#Verses 21–35|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 21]]
- The armies of Israel now begin to emerge out of the wilderness, and to come into a land inhabited, to enter upon action, and take possession of the frontiers of the land of promise. A glorious campaign this chapter… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/numbers/chapter-21|Matthew Henry Complete on Numbers 21]]
