# Ruth 2

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ruth 2: Ruth gleans in the field of Boaz. (1–3). The kindness of Boaz to Ruth. (4–16). Ruth returns to her mother-in-law. (17–23).

## Core claims

- Observe Ruth's humility. When Providence had made her poor, she cheerfully stoops to her lot. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ruth/chapter-2#Verses 1–3|Matthew Henry Concise on Ruth 2]]
- The pious and kind language between Boaz and his reapers shows that there were godly persons in Israel. Such language as this is seldom heard in our field; too often, on the contrary, what is immoral and corrupt. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ruth/chapter-2#Verses 4–16|Matthew Henry Concise on Ruth 2]]
- It encourages industry, that in all labour, even that of gleaning, there is profit. Ruth was pleased with what she gained by her own industry, and was careful to secure it. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ruth/chapter-2#Verses 17–23|Matthew Henry Concise on Ruth 2]]
- There is scarcely any chapter in all the sacred history that stoops so low as this to take cognizance of so mean a person as Ruth, a poor Moabitish widow, so mean an action as her gleaning corn in a neighbour's field,… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/ruth/chapter-2|Matthew Henry Complete on Ruth 2]]
