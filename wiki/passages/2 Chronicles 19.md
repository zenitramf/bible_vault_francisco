## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 19: Jehoshaphat visits his kingdom. (1-11).

## Core claims

- Whenever we return in peace to our houses, we ought to acknowledge God's providence in preserving our going out and coming in. And if we have been kept through more than common dangers, we are, in a special manner, bound to be thankful. The reproof given him for his league with Ahab, and his acting in conjunction with him, 2Ch 19:2,3.… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/2-chronicles/chapter-19|Matthew Henry Complete on 2 Chronicles 19]]
