# Ezra 7

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezra 7: Ezra goes up to Jerusalem. (1–10). The commission to Ezra. (11–26). Ezra blesses God for his favour. (27, 28).

## Core claims

- Ezra went from Babylon to Jerusalem, for the good of his country. The king was kind to him; he granted all his requests, whatever Ezra desired to enable him to serve his country. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezra/chapter-7#Verses 1–10|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezra 7]]
- The liberality of heathen kings to support the worship of God, reproached the conduct of many kings of Judah, and will rise up in judgment against the covetousness of wealthy professed Christians, who will not promote the cause of God. But the weapons of Christian ministers are not carnal. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezra/chapter-7#Verses 11–26|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezra 7]]
- Two things Ezra blessed God for: 1. For his commission. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezra/chapter-7#Verses 27, 28|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezra 7]]
- Ezra's precious name saluted us, at first, in the title of the book, but in the history we have not met with it till this chapter introduces him into public action in another reign, that of Artaxerxes.
