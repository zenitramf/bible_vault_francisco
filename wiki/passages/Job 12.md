# Job 12

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Job 12: Job reproves his friends. (1–5). The wicked often prosper. (6–11). Job speaks of the wisdom and power of God. (12–25).

## Core claims

- Job upbraids his friends with the good opinion they had of their own wisdom compared with his. We are apt to call reproofs reproaches, and to think ourselves mocked when advised and admonished; this is our folly; yet here was colour for this charge. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/job/chapter-12#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on Job 12]]
- Job appeals to facts. The most audacious robbers, oppressors, and impious wretches, often prosper. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/job/chapter-12#Verses 6–11|Matthew Henry Concise on Job 12]]
- This is a noble discourse of Job concerning the wisdom, power, and sovereignty of God, in ordering all the affairs of the children of men, according to the counsel of His own will, which none can resist.
