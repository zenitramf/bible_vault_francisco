# Isaiah 22

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 22: The siege and taking of Jerusalem. (1-7). The wicked conduct of its inhabitants. (8-14). The displacing of Shebna, and the promotion of Eliakim, applied to the Messiah. (15-25).

## Core claims

- Why is Jerusalem in such terror? Her slain men are not slain with the sword, but with famine; or, slain with fear, disheartened. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-22#Verses 1-7|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 22]]
- The weakness of Judah now appeared more than ever. Now also they discovered their carnal confidence and their carnal security. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-22#Verses 8-14|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 22]]
- This message to Shebna is a reproof of his pride, vanity, and security; what vanity is all earthly grandeur, which death will so soon end! What will it avail, whether we are laid in a magnificent tomb, or covered with the green sod? [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-22#Verses 15-25|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 22]]
- We have now come nearer home, for this chapter is "the burden of the valley of vision," Jerusalem; other places had their burden for the sake of their being concerned in some way or other with Jerusalem, and were… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/isaiah/chapter-22|Matthew Henry Complete on Isaiah 22]]
