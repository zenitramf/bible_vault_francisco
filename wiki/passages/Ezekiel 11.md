# Ezekiel 11

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 11: Divine judgments against the wicked at Jerusalem. (1-13). Divine favour towards those in captivity. (14-21). The Divine presence forsakes the city. (22-25).

## Core claims

- Where Satan cannot persuade men to look upon the judgment to come as uncertain, he gains his point by persuading them to look upon it as at a distance. - The pious captives in Babylon were insulted by the Jews who continued in Jerusalem; but God made gracious promises to them. It is promised, that God will give them one heart; a heart firmly fixed for God, and not wavering. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-11#Verses 14-21|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 11]]
- Here is the departure of God's presence from the city and temple. It was from the Mount of Olives that the vision went up, typifying the ascension of Christ to heaven from that very mountain. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-11#Verses 22-25|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 11]]
