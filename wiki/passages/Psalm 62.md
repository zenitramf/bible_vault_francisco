# Psalm 62

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 62: David's confidence in God. (1–7). No trust to be put in worldly things. (8–12).

## Core claims

- We are in the way both of duty and comfort, when our souls wait upon God; when we cheerfully give up ourselves, and all our affairs, to his will and wisdom; when we leave ourselves to all the ways of his providence, and patiently expect the event, with full satisfaction in his goodness.
