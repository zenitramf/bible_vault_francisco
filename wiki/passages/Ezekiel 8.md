# Ezekiel 8

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 8: The idolatries committed by the Jewish rulers. (1-6). The superstitions to which the Jews were then devoted, the Egyptian. (7-12). The Phoenician. (13,14). The Persian. (15,16) . The heinousness of their sin. (17,18).

## Core claims
