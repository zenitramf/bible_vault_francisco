# Leviticus 5

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 5: Concerning various trespasses. (1–13). Concerning trespasses against the Lord. (14–19).

## Core claims

- The offences here noticed are, 1. A man's concealing the truth, when he was sworn as a witness to speak the truth, the whole truth, and nothing but the truth. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/leviticus/chapter-5#Verses 1–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 5]]
- Here are offerings to atone for trespasses against a neighbour. If a man put to his own use unwittingly, any thing dedicated to God, he was to bring this sacrifice. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/leviticus/chapter-5#Verses 14–19|Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 5]]
