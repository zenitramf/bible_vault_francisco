# Acts 27

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Acts 27: Paul's voyage towards Rome. (1–11). Paul and his companions endangered by a tempest. (12–20). He receives a Divine assurance of safety. (21–29). Paul encourages those with him. (30–38). They are shipwrecked. (39–44).

## Core claims

- It was determined by the counsel of God, before it was determined by the counsel of Festus, that Paul should go to Rome; for God had work for him to do there. The course they steered, and the places they touched at, are here set down. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/acts/chapter-27#Verses 1–11|Matthew Henry Concise on Acts 27]]
- Those who launch forth on the ocean of this world, with a fair gale, know not what storms they may meet with; and therefore must not easily take it for granted that they have obtained their purpose.
