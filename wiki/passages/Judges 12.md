# Judges 12

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Judges 12: Ephraimites quarrel with Jephthah. (1–7). Ibzan, Elon, and Abdon judge Israel. (8–15).

## Core claims

- The Ephraimites had the same quarrel with Jephthah as with Gideon. Pride was at the bottom of the quarrel; only by that comes contention. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/judges/chapter-12#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Judges 12]]
- We have here a short account of three more of the judges of Israel. The happiest life of individuals, and the happiest state of society, is that which affords the fewest remarkable events. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/judges/chapter-12#Verses 8–15|Matthew Henry Concise on Judges 12]]
- In this chapter we have, I. Jephthah's rencounter with the Ephraimites, and the blood shed on that unhappy occasion (Jdg 12:1-6), and the conclusion of Jephthah's life and government, Jdg 12:7. II.
