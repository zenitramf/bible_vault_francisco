# Hosea 5

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 5: The Divine judgments against Israel. (1-7). Approaching desolations threatened. (8-15).

## Core claims

- The piercing eye of God saw secret liking and disposition to sin, the love the house of Israel had to their sins, and the dominion their sins had over them. Pride makes men obstinate in other sins. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/hosea/chapter-5#Verses 1-7|Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 5]]
- The destruction of impenitent sinners is not mere talk, to frighten them, it is a sentence which will not be recalled. And it is a mercy that we have timely warning given us, that we may flee from the wrath to come. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/hosea/chapter-5#Verses 8-15|Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 5]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
