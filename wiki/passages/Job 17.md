# Job 17

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Job 17: Job appeals from man to God. (1–9). His hope is not in life, but in death. (10–16).

## Core claims

- Job reflects upon the harsh censures his friends had passed upon him, and, looking on himself as a dying man, he appeals to God. Our time is ending. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/job/chapter-17#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Job 17]]
- Job's friends had pretended to comfort him with the hope of his return to a prosperous estate; he here shows that those do not go wisely about the work of comforting the afflicted, who fetch their comforts from the possibility of recovery in this world.
