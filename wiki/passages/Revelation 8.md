## Core claims

- The seventh seal is opened. There was profound silence in heaven for a space; all was quiet in the church, for whenever the church on earth cries through oppression, that cry reaches up to heaven; or it is a silence of expectation. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-8#Verses 1–6|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 8]]
- The first angel sounded the first trumpet, and there followed hail and fire mingled with blood. A storm of heresies, a mixture of dreadful errors falling on the church, or a tempest of destruction. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-8#Verses 7–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 8]]
