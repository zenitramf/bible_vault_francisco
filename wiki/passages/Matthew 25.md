# Matthew 25

## Summary

Virgins, talents, and the sheep-and-goats judgment press readiness, faithful work, and merciful love.

## Core claims

- Wise virgins keep oil ready for the bridegroom’s delay; the foolish are shut out. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-25#Verses 1–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 25]]
- Talents are entrusted for work; idle burial of the gift is condemned. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-25#Verses 14–30|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 25]]
- The King separates sheep and goats; mercy to the least of his brethren is mercy to him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-25#Verses 31–46|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 25]]
- This chapter continues and concludes our Saviour's discourse, which began in the foregoing chapter, concerning his second coming and the end of the world. This was his farewell sermon of caution, as that, Joh 14:15,16,… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-5/matthew/chapter-25|Matthew Henry Complete on Matthew 25]]
