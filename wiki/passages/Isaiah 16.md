# Isaiah 16

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 16: Moab is exhorted to yield obedience. (1-5). The pride and the judgments of Moab. (6-14).

## Core claims

- God tells sinners what they may do to prevent ruin; so he does to Moab. Let them send the tribute they formerly engaged to pay to Judah. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-16#Verses 1-5|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 16]]
- Those who will not be counselled, cannot be helped. More souls are ruined by pride than by any other sin whatever. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-16#Verses 6-14|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 16]]
- This chapter continues and concludes the burden of Moab. In it, I. The prophet gives good counsel to the Moabites, to reform what was amiss among them, and particularly to be kind to God's people, as the likeliest way… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/isaiah/chapter-16|Matthew Henry Complete on Isaiah 16]]
