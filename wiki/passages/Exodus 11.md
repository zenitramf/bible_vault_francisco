# Exodus 11

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 11: God's last instructions to Moses respecting Pharaoh and the Egyptians. (1–3). The death of the first-born threatened. (4–10).

## Core claims

- A secret revelation was made to Moses while in the presence of Pharaoh, that he might give warning of the last dreadful judgment, before he went out. This was the last day of the servitude of Israel; they were about to go away. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-11#Verses 1–3|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 11]]
- The death of all the first-born in Egypt at once: this plague had been the first threatened, but is last executed. See how slow God is to wrath. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-11#Verses 4–10|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 11]]
- Pharaoh had told Moses to get out of his presence (Ex 10:28), and Moses had promised this should be the last time he would trouble him, yet he resolves to say out what he had to say, before he left him; accordingly, we… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/exodus/chapter-11|Matthew Henry Complete on Exodus 11]]
