# Psalm 65

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 65: God is to be praised in the kingdom of grace. (1–5). In the kingdom of providence. (6–13).

## Core claims

- All the praise the Lord receives from this earth is from Zion, being the fruit of the Spirit of Christ, and acceptable through him. Praise is silent unto thee, as wanting words to express the great goodness of God. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-65#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 65]]
- That Almighty strength which sets fast the mountains, upholds the believer. That word which stills the stormy ocean, and speaks it into a calm, can silence our enemies. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-65#Verses 6–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 65]]
- In this psalm we are directed to give to God the glory of his power and goodness, which appear, I.
