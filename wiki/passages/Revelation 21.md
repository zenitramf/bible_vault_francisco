## Core claims

- The new heaven and the new earth will not be separate from each other; the earth of the saints, their glorified, bodies, will be heavenly. The old world, with all its troubles and tumults, will have passed away. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-21#Verses 1–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 21]]
- God has various employments for his holy angels. Sometimes they sound the trumpet of Divine Providence, and warn a careless world; sometimes they discover things of a heavenly nature of the heirs of salvation. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-21#Verses 9–21|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 21]]
- Perfect and direct communion with God, will more than supply the place of gospel institutions. And what words can more full express the union and co-equality of the Son with the Father, in the Godhead? [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-21#Verses 22–27|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 21]]
