# 2 Chronicles 6

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 6: Solomon's prayer at the dedication of the temple. (1-42).

## Core claims

- The order of Solomon's prayer is to be observed. First and chiefly, he prays for repentance and forgiveness, which is the chief blessing, and the only solid foundation of other mercies: he then prays for temporal mercies; thereby teaching us what things to mind and desire most in our prayers. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-6#Verses 1-42|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 6]]
