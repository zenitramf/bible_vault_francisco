# Revelation 11

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 11: The state of the church is represented under the figure of a temple measured. (1, 2). Two witnesses prophesy is sackcloth. (3–6). They are slain, after which they arise and ascend to heaven. (7–13). Under the seventh ….

## Core claims

- This prophetical passage about measuring the temple seems to refer to Ezekiel's vision. The design of this measuring seems to be the preservation of the church in times of public danger; or for its trial, or for its reformation. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-11#Verses 1, 2|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 11]]
- In the time of treading down, God kept his faithful witnesses to attest the truth of his word and worship, and the excellence of his ways, The number of these witnesses is small, yet enough. They prophesy in sackcloth. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-11#Verses 3–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 11]]
- Before the sounding of the seventh and last trumpet, there is the usual demand of attention. The saints and angels in heaven know the right of our God and Saviour to rule over all the world. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-11#Verses 14–19|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 11]]
