# Matthew 7

## Summary

Matthew 7 closes the sermon: rash judgment forbidden, prayer encouraged, the narrow way, false prophets known by fruit, and builders who do the word.

## Core claims

- Judge yourself first; do not rashly condemn a brother without ground. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-7#Verses 1–6|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 7]]
- Prayer is the appointed means for what we need: ask, seek, knock with earnest perseverance. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-7#Verses 7–11|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 7]]
- The golden rule and the narrow way mark true discipleship; broad is the way to destruction. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-7#Verses 12–14|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 7]]
- False prophets are known by fruits; mere profession is not enough—doers of the word stand in the storm. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-7#Verses 15–20|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 7]]
- This chapter continues and concludes Christ's sermon on the mount, which is purely practical, directing us to order our conversation aright, both toward God and man; for the design of the Christian religion is to make… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-5/matthew/chapter-7|Matthew Henry Complete on Matthew 7]]
