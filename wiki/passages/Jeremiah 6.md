# Jeremiah 6

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 6: The invasion of Judea. (1-8). The justice of God's proceedings. (9-17). All methods used to amend them had been without success. (18-30).

## Core claims

- Whatever methods are used, it is vain to contend with God's judgments. The more we indulge in the pleasures of this life, the more we unfit ourselves for the troubles of this life. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-6#Verses 1-8|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 6]]
- When the Lord arises to take vengeance, no sinners of any age or rank, or of either sex escape. They were set upon the world, and wholly carried away by the love of it. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-6#Verses 9-17|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 6]]
- God rejects their outward services, as worthless to atone for their sins. Sacrifice and incense were to direct them to a Mediator; but when offered to purchase a license to go on in sin, they provoke God. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-6#Verses 18-30|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 6]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
