# Ezekiel 18

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 18: God has no respect of persons. (1-20). The Divine providence is vindicated. (21-29). A gracious invitation to repentance. (30-32).

## Core claims

- The soul that sinneth it shall die. As to eternity, every man was, is, and will be dealt with, as his conduct shows him to have been under the old covenant of works, or the new covenant of grace. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-18#Verses 1-20|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 18]]
- The wicked man would be saved, if he turned from his evil ways. The true penitent is a true believer. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-18#Verses 21-29|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 18]]
- The Lord will judge each of the Israelites according to his ways. On this is grounded an exhortation to repent, and to make them a new heart and a new spirit. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-18#Verses 30-32|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 18]]
- Perhaps, in reading some of the foregoing chapters, we may have been tempted to think ourselves not much concerned in them (though they also were written for our learning); but this chapter, at first view, appears… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/ezekiel/chapter-18|Matthew Henry Complete on Ezekiel 18]]
