# Job 26

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Job 26: Job reproves Bildad. (1–4). Job acknowledges the power of God. (5–14).

## Core claims

- Job derided Bildad's answer; his words were a mixture of peevishness and self-preference. Bildad ought to have laid before Job the consolations, rather than the terrors of the Almighty. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/job/chapter-26#Verses 1–4|Matthew Henry Concise on Job 26]]
- Many striking instances are here given of the wisdom and power of God, in the creation and preservation of the world. If we look about us, to the earth and waters here below, we see his almighty power. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/job/chapter-26#Verses 5–14|Matthew Henry Concise on Job 26]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
