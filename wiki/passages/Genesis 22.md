# Genesis 22

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 22: God commands Abraham to offer up Isaac. (1, 2). Abraham's faith and obedience to the Divine command. (3–10). Another sacrifice is provided instead of Isaac. (11–14). The covenant with Abraham renewed. (15–19). The family of Nahor. (20–24).

## Core claims

- We never are secure from trials In Hebrew, to tempt, and to try, or to prove, are expressed by the same word. Every trial is indeed a temptation, and tends to show the dispositions of the heart, whether holy or unholy. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-22#Verses 1, 2|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 22]]
- It was not God's intention that Isaac should actually be sacrificed, yet nobler blood than that of animals, in due time, was to be shed for sin, even the blood of the only begotten Son of God. But in the mean while God would not in any case have human sacrifices used, another sacrifice is provided. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-22#Verses 11–14|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 22]]
- This chapter ends with some account of Nahor's family, who had settled at Haran. This seems to be given for the connexion which it had with the church of God. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-22#Verses 20–24|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 22]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
