# Genesis 26

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 26: Isaac, because of famine, goes to Gerar. (1–5). He denies his wife and is reproved by Abimelech. (6–11). Isaac grows rich, The Philistines' envy. (12–17). Isaac digs wells God blesses him. (18–25). Abimelech makes a covenant with Isaac. (26–33). Esau's wives. (34, 35).

## Core claims

- Isaac had been trained up in a believing dependence upon the Divine grant of the land of Canaan to him and his heirs; and now that there is a famine in the land, Isaac still cleaves to the covenant. The real worth of God's promises cannot be lessened to a believer by any cross providences that may befall him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-26#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 26]]
- Isaac met with much opposition in digging wells. Two were called Contention and Hatred. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-26#Verses 18–25|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 26]]
- Esau was foolish in marrying two wives together, and still more in marrying Canaanites, strangers to the blessing of Abraham, and subject to the curse of Noah. It grieved his parents that he married without their advice and consent. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-26#Verses 34, 35|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 26]]
- In this chapter we have, I. Isaac in adversity, by reason of a famine in the land, which, 1. Obliges him to change his quarters, Ge 26:1. But, 2.
