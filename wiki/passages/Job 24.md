# Job 24

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Job 24: Wickedness often unpunished. (1–12). The wicked shun the light. (13–17). Judgements for the wicked. (18–25).

## Core claims

- Job discourses further about the prosperity of the wicked. That many live at ease who are ungodly and profane, he had showed, ch. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/job/chapter-24#Verses 1–12|Matthew Henry Concise on Job 24]]
- See what care and pains wicked men take to compass their wicked designs; let it shame our negligence and slothfulness in doing good. See what pains those take, who make provision for the flesh to fulfil the lusts of it: pains to compass, and then to hide that which will end in death and hell at last. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/job/chapter-24#Verses 13–17|Matthew Henry Concise on Job 24]]
- Sometimes how gradual is the decay, how quiet the departure of a wicked person, how is he honoured, and how soon are all his cruelties and oppressions forgotten! They are taken off with other men, as the harvestman gathers the ears of corn as they come to hand. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/job/chapter-24#Verses 18–25|Matthew Henry Concise on Job 24]]
- Job having by his complaints in the foregoing chapter given vent to his passion, and thereby gained some ease, breaks them off abruptly, and now applies himself to a further discussion of the doctrinal controversy… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-3/job/chapter-24|Matthew Henry Complete on Job 24]]
