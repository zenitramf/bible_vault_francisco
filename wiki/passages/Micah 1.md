# Micah 1

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Micah 1: The wrath of God against Israel. (1–7). Also against Jerusalem and other cities, Their precautions vain. (8–16).

## Core claims

- The earth is called upon, with all that are therein, to hear the prophet. God's holy temple will not protect false professors. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/micah/chapter-1#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Micah 1]]
- The prophet laments that Israel's case is desperate; but declare it not in Gath. Gratify not those that make merry with the sins or with the sorrows of God's Israel. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/micah/chapter-1#Verses 8–16|Matthew Henry Concise on Micah 1]]
- In this chapter we have, I. The title of the book (Mic 1:1) and a preface demanding attention, Mic 1:2. II. Warning given of desolating judgments hastening upon the kingdoms of Israel and Judah (Mic 1:3,4), and all for… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/micah/chapter-1|Matthew Henry Complete on Micah 1]]
