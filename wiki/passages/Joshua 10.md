# Joshua 10

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 10: Five kings war against Gibeon. (1–6). Joshua succours Gibeon, The sun and moon stand still. (7–14). The kings are taken, their armies defeated, and they are put to death. (15–27). Seven other kings defeated and slain. (28–43).

## Core claims

- When sinners leave the service of Satan and the friendship of the world, that they make peace with God and join Israel, they must not marvel if the world hate them, if their former friends become foes. - None moved his tongue against any of the children of Israel. This shows their perfect safety. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-10#Verses 15–27|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 10]]
- Joshua made speed in taking these cities. See what a great deal of work may be done in a little time, if we will be diligent, and improve our opportunities. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-10#Verses 28–43|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 10]]
