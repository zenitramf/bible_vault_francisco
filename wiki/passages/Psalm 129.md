# Psalm 129

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 129: Thankfulness for former deliverances. (1–4). A believing prospect of the destruction of the enemies of Zion. (5–8).

## Core claims

- The enemies of God's people have very barbarously endeavoured to wear out the saints of the Most High. But the church has been always graciously delivered. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-129#Verses 1–4|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 129]]
- While God's people shall flourish as the loaded palm-tree, or the green and fruitful olive, their enemies shall wither as the grass upon the house-tops, which in eastern countries are flat, and what grows there never ripens; so it is with the designs of God's enemies. No wise man will pray the Lord to bless these… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-129#Verses 5–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 129]]
