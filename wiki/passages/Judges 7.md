# Judges 7

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Judges 7: Gideon's army reduced. (1–8). Gideon is encouraged. (9–15). The defeat of the Midianites. (16–22). The Ephraimites take Oreb and Zeeb. (23–25).

## Core claims

- God provides that the praise of victory may be wholly to himself, by appointing only three hundred men to be employed.
