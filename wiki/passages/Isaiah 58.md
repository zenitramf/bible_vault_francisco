# Isaiah 58

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 58: Hypocrisy reproved. (1,2) . A counterfeit and a true fast, with promises to real godliness, and, (3-12).

## Core claims

- The Holy Spirit had hypocrites of every age in view. Self-love and timid Christians may say, Spare thyself; dislike to the cross and other motives will say, "Spare the rich and powerful;" but God says, "Spare not:" and we must obey God, not men. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-58#Verses 1,2|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 58]]
- A fast is a day to afflict the soul; if it does not express true sorrow for sin, and does not promote the putting away of sin, it is not a fast. These professors had shown sorrow on stated or occasioned fasts. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-58#Verses 3-12|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 58]]
- The prophet, in this chapter, has his commission and charge renewed to reprove the sinners in Zion, particularly the hypocrites, to show them their transgressions, Isa 58:1. It is intended for admonition and warning to… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/isaiah/chapter-58|Matthew Henry Complete on Isaiah 58]]
