## Core claims

- Let believers be of one mind, and ready to help each other. As the apostle had found the benefit of their assistance, he knew how comfortable it would be to his fellow-labourers to have the help of others. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/philippians/chapter-4#Verses 2–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Philippians 4]]
- It is a good work to succour and help a good minister in trouble. The nature of true Christian sympathy, is not only to feel concern for our friends in their troubles, but to do what we can to help them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/philippians/chapter-4#Verses 10–19|Matthew Henry Concise on Philippians 4]]
- The apostle ends with praises to God. We should look upon God, under all our weakness and fears, not as an enemy, but as a Father, disposed to pity us and help us. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/philippians/chapter-4#Verses 20–23|Matthew Henry Concise on Philippians 4]]
- Exhortations to several Christian duties, as stedfastness, unanimity, joy, etc., Php 4:1-9.
