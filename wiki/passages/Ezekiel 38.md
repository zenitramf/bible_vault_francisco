# Ezekiel 38

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 38: The army and malice of Gog. (1-13). God's judgments. (14-23).

## Core claims

- These events will be in the latter days. It is supposed these enemies will come together to invade the land of Judea, and God will defeat them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-38#Verses 1-13|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 38]]
- The enemy should make a formidable descent upon the land of Israel. When Israel dwell safely under the Divine protection, shalt not thou be made to know it by finding that endeavours to destroy them are made in vain? [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-38#Verses 14-23|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 38]]
