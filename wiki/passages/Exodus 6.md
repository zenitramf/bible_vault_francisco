# Exodus 6

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 6: God renews his promise. (1–9). Moses and Aaron again sent to Pharaoh. (10–13). The parentage of Moses and Aaron. (14–30).

## Core claims

- We are most likely to prosper in attempts to glorify God, and to be useful to men, when we learn by experience that we can do nothing of ourselves; when our whole dependence is placed on him, and our only expectation is from him. Moses had been expecting what God would do; but now he shall see what he will do. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-6#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 6]]
- The faith of Moses was so feeble that he could scarcely be kept to his work. Ready obedience is always according to the strength of our faith. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-6#Verses 10–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 6]]
- Moses and Aaron were Israelites; raised up unto them of their brethren, as Christ also should be, who was to be the Prophet and Priest, the Redeemer and Lawgiver of the people of Israel. Moses returns to his narrative, and repeats the charge God had given him to deliver his message to Pharaoh, and his objection… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-6#Verses 14–30|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 6]]
- Much ado there was to bring Moses to his work, and when the ice was broken, some difficulty having occurred in carrying it on, there was no less ado to put him forward in it. Witness this chapter, in which, I. God… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/exodus/chapter-6|Matthew Henry Complete on Exodus 6]]
