# Matthew 10

## Summary

The Twelve are sent with instructions for mission, suffering, fearless confession, and the reward of receiving Christ’s messengers.

## Core claims

- The Twelve are sent with authority; they go first to the lost sheep of Israel with the kingdom message. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-10#Verses 1–4|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 10]]
- Disciples are sheep among wolves; they must be wise and harmless, expecting persecution. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-10#Verses 16–42|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 10]]
- Fear God rather than men; confess Christ before men; love him above family. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-10#Verses 16–42|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 10]]
- Receiving Christ’s messengers is receiving Christ; even a cup of cold water is not forgotten. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-10#Verses 16–42|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 10]]
- This chapter is an ordination sermon, which our Lord Jesus preached, when he advanced his twelve disciples to the degree and dignity of apostles. In the close of the foregoing chapter, he had stirred up them and others… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-5/matthew/chapter-10|Matthew Henry Complete on Matthew 10]]
