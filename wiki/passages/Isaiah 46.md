# Isaiah 46

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 46: The idols could not save themselves, but God saves his people. (1-4). The folly of worshipping idols. (5-13).

## Core claims

- The heathen insulted the Jews, as if their idols Bel and Nebo were too hard for Jehovah. But their worshippers cannot help them; both the idols and the idolaters are gone into captivity. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-46#Verses 1-4|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 46]]
- Here the folly of those who made idols, and then prayed to them, is exposed. How does the profuseness of idolaters shame the niggardliness of many who call themselves God's servants, but are for a religion which costs them nothing! [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-46#Verses 5-13|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 46]]
- God, by the prophet here, designing shortly to deliver them out of their captivity, prepared them for that deliverance by possessing them with a detestation of idols and with a believing confidence in God, even their… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/isaiah/chapter-46|Matthew Henry Complete on Isaiah 46]]
