# Numbers 15

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 15: The law of the meat-offering and the drink-offering, The stranger under the same law. (1–21). The sacrifice for the sin of ignorance. (22–29). The punishment of presumption, The sabbath-breaker stoned. (30–36). The law for fringes on garment. (37–41).

## Core claims

- Full instructions are given about the meat-offerings and drink-offerings. The beginning of this law is very encouraging, When ye come into the land of your habitation which I give unto you. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-15#Verses 1–21|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 15]]
- Those are to be reckoned presumptuous sinners, who sin designedly against God's will and glory. Sins thus committed are exceedingly sinful. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-15#Verses 30–36|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 15]]
- The people are ordered by the Lord to make fringes on the borders of their garments. The Jews were distinguished from their neighbours in their dress, as well as in their diet, and thus taught not to be conformed to the way of the heathen in other things. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-15#Verses 37–41|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 15]]
- This chapter, which is mostly concerning sacrifice and offering, comes in between the story of two rebellions (one Nu 14:1-45 the other Nu 16:1-50), to signify that these legal institutions were typical of the gifts… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/numbers/chapter-15|Matthew Henry Complete on Numbers 15]]
