# Matthew 24

## Summary

Temple destruction foreshadowed, signs and tribulations, the certain coming of the Son of Man, and the call to watch.

## Core claims

- The temple’s glory will be defaced; do not overvalue worldly splendor. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-24#Verses 1–3|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 24]]
- Deception, wars, and tribulation mark the approach of judgment; endurance and gospel witness matter. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-24#Verses 4–28|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 24]]
- The Son of Man’s coming is certain; none knows the day or hour. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-24#Verses 29–41|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 24]]
- Watch and be ready as faithful servants; the evil servant is surprised and judged. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-24#Verses 42–51|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 24]]
- Christ's preaching was mostly practical; but, in this chapter, we have a prophetical discourse, a prediction of things to come; such however as had a practical tendency, and was intended, not to gratify the curiosity… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-5/matthew/chapter-24|Matthew Henry Complete on Matthew 24]]
