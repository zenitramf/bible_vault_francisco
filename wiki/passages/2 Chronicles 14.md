# 2 Chronicles 14

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 14: Asa's piety, He strengthens his kingdom. (1-15).

## Core claims

- Asa aimed at pleasing God, and studied to approve himself to him. Happy those that walk by this rule, not to do that which is right in their own eyes, or in the eye of the world, but which is so in God's sight.
