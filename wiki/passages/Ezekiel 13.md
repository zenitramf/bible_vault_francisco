# Ezekiel 13

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 13: Heavy judgments against lying prophets. (1-9). The insufficiency of their work. (10-16). Woes against false prophetesses. (17-23).

## Core claims

- Where God gives a warrant to do any thing, he gives wisdom. What they delivered was not what they had seen or heard, as that is which the ministers of Christ deliver. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-13#Verses 1-9|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 13]]
- One false prophet built the wall, set up the notion that Jerusalem should be victorious, and made himself acceptable by it. Others made the matter yet more plausible and promising; they daubed the wall which the first had built; but they would, ere long, be undeceived when their work was beaten down by the storm of… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-13#Verses 10-16|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 13]]
- It is ill with those who had rather hear pleasing lies than unpleasing truths. The false prophetesses tried to make people secure, signified by laying them at ease, and to make them proud, signified by the finery laid on their heads. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-13#Verses 17-23|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 13]]
