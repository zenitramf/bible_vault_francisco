# Ezekiel 13

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 13: Heavy judgments against lying prophets. (1-9). The insufficiency of their work. (10-16). Woes against false prophetesses. (17-23).

## Core claims

- Where God gives a warrant to do any thing, he gives wisdom. What they delivered was not what they had seen or heard, as that is which the ministers of Christ deliver. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-13#Verses 1-9|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 13]]
- One false prophet built the wall, set up the notion that Jerusalem should be victorious, and made himself acceptable by it. - It is ill with those who had rather hear pleasing lies than unpleasing truths.
