# 2 Chronicles 32

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 32: The invasion of Sennacherib, His defeat. (1–23). Hezekiah's sickness, His prosperous reign, and death. (24–33).

## Core claims

- Those who trust God with their safety, must use proper means, else they tempt him. God will provide, but so must we also. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-32#Verses 1–23|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 32]]
- God left Hezekiah to himself, that, by this trial and his weakness in it, what was in his heart might be known; that he was not so perfect in grace as he thought he was.
