# Leviticus 19

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 19: Laws. (1-37).

## Core claims

- There are some ceremonial precepts in this chapter, but most of these precepts are binding on us, for they are explanations of the ten commandments. It is required that Israel be a holy people, because the God of Israel is a holy God, verse 2. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/leviticus/chapter-19#Verses 1-37|Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 19]]
