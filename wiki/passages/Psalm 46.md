# Psalm 46

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 46: Confidence in God. (1–5). An exhortation to behold it. (6–11).

## Core claims

- This psalm encourages to hope and trust in God; in his power and providence, and his gracious presence with his church in the worst of times. We may apply it to spiritual enemies, and the encouragement we have that, through Christ, we shall be conquerors over them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-46#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 46]]
- Come and see the effects of desolating judgments, and stand in awe of God. This shows the perfect security of the church, and is an assurance of lasting peace. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-46#Verses 6–11|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 46]]
