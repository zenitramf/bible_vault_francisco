## Core claims

- The apostle desired to preserve the Corinthians from being corrupted by the false apostles. There is but one Jesus, one Spirit, and one gospel, to be preached to them, and received by them; and why should any be prejudiced, by the devices of an adversary, against him who first taught them in faith? ## Agreements and tensions

Henry and McGee agree false apostles corrupt and self-support protects gospel integrity. McGee’s “attractive and winsome” false-minister portrait thickens spiritual-warfare and pastoral-discernment layers.
