# Matthew 23

## Summary

Woes on scribes and Pharisees for hypocrisy, and Jesus’ lament over Jerusalem that would not be gathered.

## Core claims

- Do as the scribes teach from Moses’ seat, but not as they do; they love titles and burden others. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-23#Verses 1–12|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 23]]
- Woes fall on hypocrisy that shuts the kingdom, swears falsely, tithes mint while neglecting justice and mercy. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-23#Verses 13–33|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 23]]
- Jerusalem’s guilt fills up; Christ would have gathered her as a hen her chicks, yet she would not. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-23#Verses 34–39|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 23]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later deepening should add Matthew Henry Complete on the same chapter and Spurgeon where he treats the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
