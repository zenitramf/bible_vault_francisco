# Habakkuk 2

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Habakkuk 2: Habakkuk must wait in faith. (1–4). Judgments upon the Chaldeans. (5–14). Also upon drunkenness and idolatry. (15–20).

## Core claims

- When tossed and perplexed with doubts about the methods of Providence, we must watch against temptations to be impatient. When we have poured out complaints and requests before God, we must observe the answers God gives by his word, his Spirit, and providences; what the Lord will say to our case. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/habakkuk/chapter-2#Verses 1–4|Matthew Henry Concise on Habakkuk 2]]
- The prophet reads the doom of all proud and oppressive powers that bear hard upon God's people. The lusts of the flesh, the lust of the eye, and the pride of life, are the entangling snares of men; and we find him that led Israel captive, himself led captive by each of these. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/habakkuk/chapter-2#Verses 5–14|Matthew Henry Concise on Habakkuk 2]]
- A severe woe is pronounced against drunkenness; it is very fearful against all who are guilty of drunkenness at any time, and in any place, from the stately palace to the paltry ale-house.
