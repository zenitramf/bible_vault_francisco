# Jeremiah 39

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 39: The taking of Jerusalem. (1-10). Jeremiah used well. (11-14). Promises of safety to Ebed-melech. (15-18).

## Core claims

- Jerusalem was so strong, that the inhabitants believed the enemy could never enter it. But sin provoked God to withdraw his protection, and then it was as weak as other cities. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-39#Verses 1-10|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 39]]
- The servants of God alone are prepared for all events; and they are delivered and comforted, while the wicked suffer. They often meet with more kindness from the profane, than from hypocritical professors of godliness. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-39#Verses 11-14|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 39]]
- Here is a message to assure Ebed-melech of a recompence for his great kindness to Jeremiah. Because thou hast put thy trust in me, saith the Lord. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-39#Verses 15-18|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 39]]
- As the prophet Isaiah, after he had largely foretold the deliverance of Jerusalem out of the hands of the king of Assyria, gave a particular narrative of the story, that it might appear how exactly the event answered… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/jeremiah/chapter-39|Matthew Henry Complete on Jeremiah 39]]
