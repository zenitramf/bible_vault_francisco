# Jeremiah 27

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 27: The neighbouring nations to be subdued. (1-11). Zedekiah is warned to yield. (12-18). The vessels of the temple to be carried to Babylon, but afterwards to be restored. (19-22).

## Core claims

- Jeremiah is to prepare a sign that all the neighbouring countries would be made subject to the king of Babylon. God asserts his right to dispose of kingdoms as he pleases. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-27#Verses 1-11|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 27]]
- Jeremiah persuades the king of Judah to surrender to the king of Babylon. - Jeremiah assures them that the brazen vessels should go after the golden ones. All shall be carried to Babylon. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-27#Verses 19-22|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 27]]
- Jeremiah the prophet, since he cannot persuade people to submit to God's precept, and so to prevent the destruction of their country by the king of Babylon, is here persuading them to submit to God's providence, by… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/jeremiah/chapter-27|Matthew Henry Complete on Jeremiah 27]]
