# 2 Chronicles 30

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 30: Hezekiah's passover. (1–12). The passover celebrated. (13–20). The feast of unleavened bread. (21–27).

## Core claims

- Hezekiah made Israel as welcome to the passover, as any of his own subjects. Let us yield ourselves unto the Lord. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-30#Verses 1–12|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 30]]
- The great thing needful in attendance upon God in solemn ordinances, is, that we make heart-work of it; all is nothing without this. Where this sincerity and fixedness of heart are, there may yet be many things short of the purification of the sanctuary. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-30#Verses 13–20|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 30]]
- Many prayers were put up to God with the peace-offerings. In these Israel looked to God as the God of their fathers, a God in covenant with them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-30#Verses 21–27|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 30]]
- In this chapter we have an account of the solemn passover which Hezekiah kept in the first year of his reign. I.
