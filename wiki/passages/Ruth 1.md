# Ruth 1

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ruth 1: Elimelech and his sons die in the land of Moab. (1–5). Naomi returns home. (6–14). Orpah stays behind, but Ruth goes with Naomi. (15–18). They come to Bethlehem. (19–22).

## Core claims

- Elimelech's care to provide for his family, was not to be blamed; but his removal into the country of Moab could not be justified. And the removal ended in the wasting of his family. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ruth/chapter-1#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on Ruth 1]]
- See Ruth's resolution, and her good affection to Naomi. Orpah was loth to part from her; yet she did not love her well enough to leave Moab for her sake. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ruth/chapter-1#Verses 15–18|Matthew Henry Concise on Ruth 1]]
- Naomi and Ruth came to Bethlehem. Afflictions will make great and surprising changes in a little time. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ruth/chapter-1#Verses 19–22|Matthew Henry Concise on Ruth 1]]
- In this chapter we have Naomi's afflictions. I. As a distressed housekeeper, forced by famine to remove into the land of Moab, Ru 1:1,2. II. As a mournful widow and mother, bewailing the death of her husband and her… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/ruth/chapter-1|Matthew Henry Complete on Ruth 1]]
