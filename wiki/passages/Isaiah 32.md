# Isaiah 32

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 32: Times of peace and happiness. (1-8). An interval of trouble, yet comfort and blessings in the end. (9-20).

## Core claims

- Christ our righteous King, and his true disciples, are evidently here intended. The consolations and graces of his Spirit are as rivers of water in this dry land; and as the overhanging rock affords refreshing shade and shelter to the weary traveller in the desert, so his power, truth, and love, yield the believer… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-32#Verses 1-8|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 32]]
- When there was so much provocation given to the holy God, bad times might be expected. Alas! [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-32#Verses 9-20|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 32]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
