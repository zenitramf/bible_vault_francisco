# Malachi 4

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Malachi 4: The judgements on the wicked, and the happiness of the righteous. (1–3). Regard to be had to the law; John the Baptist promised as the forerunner of Messiah. (4–6).

## Core claims

- Here is a reference to the first and to the second coming of Christ: God has fixed the day of both. Those who do wickedly, who do not fear God's anger, shall feel it. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/malachi/chapter-4#Verses 1–3|Matthew Henry Concise on Malachi 4]]
- Here is a solemn conclusion, not only of this prophecy, but of the Old Testament. Conscience bids us remember the law. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/malachi/chapter-4#Verses 4–6|Matthew Henry Concise on Malachi 4]]
- We have here proper instructions given us (very proper to close the canon of the Old Testament with), I. Concerning the state of recompence and retribution that is before us, the misery of the wicked and the happiness… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/malachi/chapter-4|Matthew Henry Complete on Malachi 4]]
