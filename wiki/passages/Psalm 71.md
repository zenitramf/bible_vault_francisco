# Psalm 71

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 71: Prayers that God would deliver and save. (1–13). Believing praises. (14–24).

## Core claims

- David prays that he might never be made ashamed of dependence upon God. With this petition every true believer may come boldly to the throne of grace. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-71#Verses 1–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 71]]
- The psalmist declares that the righteousness of Christ, and the great salvation obtained thereby, shall be the chosen subject of his discourse. Not on a sabbath only, but on every day of the week, of the year, of his life. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-71#Verses 14–24|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 71]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
