# Hosea 13

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 13: The abuse of God's favour leads to punishment. (1-8). A promise of God's mercy. (9-16).

## Core claims

- While Ephraim kept up a holy fear of God, and worshipped Him in that fear, so long he was very considerable. When Ephraim forsook God, and followed idolatry, he sunk. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/hosea/chapter-13#Verses 1-8|Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 13]]
- Israel had destroyed himself by his rebellion; but he could not save himself, his help was from the Lord only. This may well be applied to the case of spiritual redemption, from that lost state into which all have fallen by wilful sins. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/hosea/chapter-13#Verses 9-16|Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 13]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
