# 1 Chronicles 22

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Chronicles 22: David's preparations for the temple. (1–5). David's instructions to Solomon. (6–16). The prices commanded to assist. (17–19).

## Core claims

- On occasion of the terrible judgment inflicted on Israel for the sin of David, God pointed out the place where he would have the temple built; upon which, David was excited to make preparations for the great work. David must not build, but he would do all he could; he prepared abundantly before his death. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-chronicles/chapter-22#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Chronicles 22]]
- David gives Solomon the reason why he should build the temple. Because God named him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-chronicles/chapter-22#Verses 6–16|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Chronicles 22]]
- Whatever is done towards rendering the word of God generally known and attended to, is like bringing a stone, or an ingot of gold, towards erecting the temple. This should encourage us when we grieve that we do not see more fruit of our labours; much good may appear after our death, which we never thought of. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-chronicles/chapter-22#Verses 17–19|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Chronicles 22]]
- "Out of the eater comes forth meat."
