# Jeremiah 3

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 3: Exhortations to repentance. (1-5). Judah more guilty than Israel. (6-11). But pardon is promised. (12-20). The children of Israel express their sorrow and repentance. (21-25).

## Core claims

- In repentance, it is good to think upon the sins of which we have been guilty, and the places and companies where they have been committed. How gently the Lord had corrected them! [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-3#Verses 1-5|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 3]]
- See God's readiness to pardon sin, and the blessings reserved for gospel times. These words were proclaimed toward the north; to Israel, the ten tribes, captive in Assyria. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-3#Verses 12-20|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 3]]
- Sin is turning aside to crooked ways. And forgetting the Lord our God is at the bottom of all sin. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-3#Verses 21-25|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 3]]
- The foregoing chapter was wholly taken up with reproofs and threatenings against the people of God, for their apostasies from him; but in this chapter gracious invitations and encouragements are given them to return… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/jeremiah/chapter-3|Matthew Henry Complete on Jeremiah 3]]
