## Core claims

- To dissuade the Corinthians from communion with idolaters, and security in any sinful course, the apostle sets before them the example of the Jewish nation of old. They were, by a miracle, led through the Red Sea, where the pursuing Egyptians were drowned. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-corinthians/chapter-10#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Corinthians 10]]
- Carnal desires gain strength by indulgence, therefore should be checked in their first rise. Let us fear the sins of Israel, if we would shun their plagues. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-corinthians/chapter-10#Verses 6–14|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Corinthians 10]]
