# Matthew 3

## Summary

John the Baptist prepares the way with repentance preaching; Jesus’ baptism displays Trinitarian witness and his commitment to fulfill all righteousness.

## Core claims

- John appears after long prophetic silence, calling for repentance and baptizing those who confess sins. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-3#Verses 1–6|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 3]]
- True preaching applies truth to hearers’ souls; outward religion without spiritual fruit is worthless; the axe is laid to the root. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-3#Verses 7–12|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 3]]
- Jesus is baptized to fulfill all righteousness; the Father’s voice and the Spirit’s descent declare the beloved Son. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-3#Verses 13–17|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 3]]

## Agreements and tensions
