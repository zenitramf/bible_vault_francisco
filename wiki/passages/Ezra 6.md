# Ezra 6

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezra 6: The decree for completing the temple. (1–12). The temple is finished. (13–22).

## Core claims

- When God's time is come for fulfilling his gracious purposes concerning his church, he will raise up instruments to do it, from whom such good service was not expected. While our thoughts are directed to this event, we are led by Zechariah to fix our regard on a nobler, a spiritual building. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezra/chapter-6#Verses 1–12|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezra 6]]
- The gospel church, that spiritual temple, is long in the building, but it will be finished at last, when the mystical body is completed. Every believer is a living temple, building up himself in his most holy faith: much opposition is given to this work by Satan and our own corruptions. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezra/chapter-6#Verses 13–22|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezra 6]]
- How solemnly the foundation of the temple was laid we read in Ezr 3:1-13 How slowly the building went on, and with how much difficulty, we found in Ezr 4:1-5:17 But how gloriously the topstone was at length brought… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/ezra/chapter-6|Matthew Henry Complete on Ezra 6]]
