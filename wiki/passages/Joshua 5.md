# Joshua 5

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 5: The Canaanites are afraid, Circumcision renewed. (1–9). The passover at Gilgal, The manna ceases. (10–12). The Captain of the Lord's host appears to Joshua. (13–15).

## Core claims

- How dreadful is their case, who see the wrath of God advancing towards them, without being able to turn it aside, or escape it! Such will be the horrible situation of the wicked; nor can words express the anguish of their feelings, or the greatness of their terror. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-5#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 5]]
- A solemn passover was kept, at the time appointed by the law, in the plains of Jericho, in defiance of the Canaanites round about them. It was a performance of the promise, that when they went up to keep the feasts, their land should be under the special protection of the Divine providence, Ex 34:24. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-5#Verses 10–12|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 5]]
- We read not of any appearance of God's glory to Joshua till now. There appeared to him one as a man to be noticed. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-5#Verses 13–15|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 5]]
- Israel have now got over Jordan, and the waters which had opened before them, to favour their march forward, are closed again behind them, to forbid their retreat backward.
