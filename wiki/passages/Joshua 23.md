# Joshua 23

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 23: Joshua's exhortation before his death. (1–10). Joshua warns the people of idolatry. (11–16).

## Core claims

- Joshua was old and dying, let them observe what he said now. He put them in mind of the great things God had done for them in his days. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-23#Verses 1–10|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 23]]
- Would we cleave to the Lord, we must always stand upon our guard, for many a soul is lost through carelessness. Love the Lord your God, and you will not leave him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-23#Verses 11–16|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 23]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
