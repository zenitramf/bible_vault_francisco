# Psalm 58

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 58: Wicked judges described and reproved. (1–5). A prayer that they may be disabled, and their ruin predicted. (6–11).

## Core claims

- When wrong is done under the form of law, it is worse than any other; especially it is grievous to behold those who profess to be children of God, joining together against any of his people. We should thank the Lord for merciful restraints; we should be more earnest in seeking renewing grace, more watchful over… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-58#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 58]]
- David prayed that the enemies of God's church and people might be disabled to do further mischief. We may, in faith, pray against the designs of the enemies of the church. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-58#Verses 6–11|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 58]]
