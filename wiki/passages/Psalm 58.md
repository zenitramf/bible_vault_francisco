# Psalm 58

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 58: Wicked judges described and reproved. (1–5). A prayer that they may be disabled, and their ruin predicted. (6–11).

## Core claims

- When wrong is done under the form of law, it is worse than any other; especially it is grievous to behold those who profess to be children of God, joining together against any of his people.
