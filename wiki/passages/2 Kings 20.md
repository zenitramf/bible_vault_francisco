# 2 Kings 20

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 20: Hezekiah's sickness, His recovery in answer to prayer. (1–11). Hezekiah shows his treasures to the ambassadors from Babylon, His death. (12–21).

## Core claims

- Hezekiah was sick unto death, in the same year in which the king of Assyria besieged Jerusalem. A warning to prepare for death was brought to Hezekiah by Isaiah. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-kings/chapter-20#Verses 1–11|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 20]]
- The king of Babylon was at this time independent of the king of Assyria, though shortly after subdued by him. Hezekiah showed his treasures and armour, and other proofs of his wealth and power. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-kings/chapter-20#Verses 12–21|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 20]]
