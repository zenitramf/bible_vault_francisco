# Ezekiel 15

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 15: Jerusalem like an unfruitful vine. (1-8).

## Core claims

- If a vine be fruitful, it is valuable. But if not fruitful, it is worthless and useless, it is cast into the fire. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-15#Verses 1-8|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 15]]
- Ezekiel has again and again, in God's name, foretold the utter ruin of Jerusalem; but, it should seem, he finds it hard to reconcile himself to it, and to acquiesce in the will of God in this severe dispensation; and… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/ezekiel/chapter-15|Matthew Henry Complete on Ezekiel 15]]
