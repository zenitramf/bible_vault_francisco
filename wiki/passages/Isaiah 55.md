# Isaiah 55

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 55: An invitation to receive freely the blessings of the Saviour. (1-5). Gracious offers of pardon and peace. (6-13).

## Core claims

- All are welcome to the blessings of salvation, to whom those blessings are welcome. In Christ there is enough for all, and enough for each. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-55#Verses 1-5|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 55]]
- Here is a gracious offer of pardon, and peace, and of all happiness. It shall not be in vain to seek God, now his word is calling to us, and his Spirit is striving with us. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-55#Verses 6-13|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 55]]
- As we had much of Christ in the Isa 53:1-12, and much of the church of Christ in the Isa 54:1-17, so in this chapter we have much of the covenant of grace made with us in Christ.
