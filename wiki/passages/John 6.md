# John 6

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on John 6: Five thousand miraculously fed. (1–14). Jesus walks on the sea. (15–21). He directs to spiritual food. (22–27). His discourse with the multitude. (28–65). Many of disciples go back. (66–71).

## Core claims

- John relates the miracle of feeding the multitude, for its reference to the following discourse. Observe the effect this miracle had upon the people. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/john/chapter-6#Verses 1–14|Matthew Henry Concise on John 6]]
- Here were Christ's disciples in the way of duty, and Christ was praying for them; yet they were in distress. There may be perils and afflictions of this present time, where there is an interest in Christ. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/john/chapter-6#Verses 15–21|Matthew Henry Concise on John 6]]
- Instead of answering the inquiry how he came there, Jesus blamed their asking. The utmost earnestness should be employed in seeking salvation, in the use of appointed means; yet it is to be sought only as the gift of the Son of man. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/john/chapter-6#Verses 22–27|Matthew Henry Concise on John 6]]
- Constant exercise of faith in Christ, is the most important and difficult part of the obedience required from us, as sinners seeking salvation. When by his grace we are enabled to live a life of faith in the Son of God, holy tempers follow, and acceptable services may be done. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/john/chapter-6#Verses 28–35|Matthew Henry Concise on John 6]]
- When we admit into our minds hard thoughts of the words and works of Jesus, we enter into temptation, which, if the Lord in mercy prevent not, will end in drawing back. The corrupt and wicked heart of man often makes that an occasion for offence, which is matter of the greatest comfort. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/john/chapter-6#Verses 66–71|Matthew Henry Concise on John 6]]
- In this chapter we have, I. The miracle of the loaves, Joh 6:1-14. II. Christ's walking upon the water, Joh 6:15-21. III. The people's flocking after him to Capernaum, Joh 6:22-25. IV.
