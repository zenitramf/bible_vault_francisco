# Revelation 6

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 6: The opening of the seals, The first, second, third, and fourth. (1–8). The fifth. (9–11). The sixth. (12–17).

## Core claims

- Christ, the Lamb, opens the first seal: observe what appeared. A rider on a white horse. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-6#Verses 1–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 6]]
- The sight the apostle beheld at the opening the fifth seal was very affecting. He saw the souls of the martyrs under the altar; at the foot of the altar in heaven, at the feet of Christ. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-6#Verses 9–11|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 6]]
- When the sixth seal was opened, there was a great earthquake. The foundations of churches and states would be terribly shaken. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/revelation/chapter-6#Verses 12–17|Matthew Henry Concise on Revelation 6]]
- The book of the divine counsels being thus lodged in the hand of Christ, he loses no time, but immediately enters upon the work of opening the seals and publishing the contents; but this is done in such a manner as… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-6/revelation/chapter-6|Matthew Henry Complete on Revelation 6]]
