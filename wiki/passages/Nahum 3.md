# Nahum 3

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Nahum 3: The sins and judgments of Nineveh. (1–7). Its utter destruction. (8–19).

## Core claims

- When proud sinners are brought down, others should learn not to lift themselves up. The fall of this great city should be a lesson to private persons, who increase wealth by fraud and oppression. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/nahum/chapter-3#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Nahum 3]]
- Strong-holds, even the strongest, are no defence against the judgments of God. They shall be unable to do any thing for themselves. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/nahum/chapter-3#Verses 8–19|Matthew Henry Concise on Nahum 3]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
