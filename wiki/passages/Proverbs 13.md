## Core claims

- There is great hope of those that reverence their parents. There is little hope of any who will not hear those that deal faithfully with them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-13#Verse 1|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 13]]
- He that stands in awe of God, and reverences his word, shall escape destruction, and be rewarded for his godly fear. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-13#Verse 13|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 13]]
- It is the misery of the wicked, that even their sensual appetites are always craving. The righteous feeds on the word and ordinances, to the satisfying of his soul with the promises of the gospel, and the Lord Jesus Christ, who is the Bread of life. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-13#Verse 25|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 13]]
