# 1 Kings 4

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Kings 4: Solomon's court. (1–19). Solomon's dominions, His daily provision. (20–28). The wisdom of Solomon. (29–34).

## Core claims

- In the choice of the great officers of Solomon's court, no doubt, his wisdom appeared. Several are the same that were in his father's time. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-kings/chapter-4#Verses 1–19|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Kings 4]]
- Never did the crown of Israel shine so bright, as when Solomon wore it. He had peace on all sides. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-kings/chapter-4#Verses 20–28|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Kings 4]]
- Solomon's wisdom was more his glory than his wealth. He had what is here called largeness of heart, for the heart is often put for the powers of the mind. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-kings/chapter-4#Verses 29–34|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Kings 4]]
- An instance of the wisdom God granted to Solomon we had in the close of the foregoing chapter. In this we have an account of his wealth and prosperity, the other branch of the promise there made him.
