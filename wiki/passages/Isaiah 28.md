# Isaiah 28

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 28: The desolations of Samaria. (1-4). The prosperity of Judah; with reproofs for sinfulness and unbelief. (5-15). Christ is pointed out as the sure Foundation for all believers. (16-22). God's dealings with his people. (23-29).

## Core claims

- What men are proud of, be it ever so mean, is to them as a crown; but pride is the forerunner of destruction. How foolishly drunkards act! [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-28#Verses 1-4|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 28]]
- Here is a promise of Christ, as the only foundation of hope for escaping the wrath to come. This foundation was laid in Zion, in the eternal counsels of God. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-28#Verses 16-22|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 28]]
- The husbandman applies to his calling with pains and prudence, in all the works of it according to their nature. Thus the Lord, who has given men this wisdom, is wonderful in counsel, and excellent in his working.
