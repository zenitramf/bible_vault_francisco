# Proverbs 8

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 8: Christ, as Wisdom, calls to the sons of men. (1–11). The nature and riches of Wisdom. (12–21). Christ one with the Father, in the creation of the world, and rejoicing in his work for the salvation of man. (22–31). Exhortations to hear Christ's word. (32–36).

## Core claims

- The will of God is made known by the works of creation, and by the consciences of men, but more clearly by Moses and the prophets. The chief difficulty is to get men to attend to instruction. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-8#Verses 1–11|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 8]]
- The Son of God declares himself to have been engaged in the creation of the world. How able, how fit is the Son of God to be the Saviour of the world, who was the Creator of it! [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-8#Verses 22–31|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 8]]
- Surely we should hearken to Christ's voice with the readiness of children. Let us all be wise, and not refuse such mercy. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-8#Verses 32–36|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 8]]
