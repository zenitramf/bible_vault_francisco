# Ezra 1

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezra 1: The proclamation of Cyrus for the rebuilding of the temple. (1–4). The people provide for their return. (5–11).

## Core claims

- The Lord stirred up the spirit of Cyrus. The hearts of kings are in the hand of the Lord. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezra/chapter-1#Verses 1–4|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezra 1]]
- The same God that raised up the spirit of Cyrus to proclaim liberty to the Jews, raised up their spirits to take the benefit. The temptation was to some to stay in Babylon; but some feared not to return, and they were those whose spirits God raised, by his Spirit and grace. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezra/chapter-1#Verses 5–11|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezra 1]]
