# Nehemiah 4

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Nehemiah 4: Opposition of Sanballat and others. (1–6). The designs of the adversaries. (7–15). Nehemiah's precautions. (16–23).

## Core claims

- Many a good work has been looked upon with contempt by proud and haughty scorners. Those who disagree in almost every thing, will unite in persecution. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/nehemiah/chapter-4#Verses 1–6|Matthew Henry Concise on Nehemiah 4]]
- The hindering good work is what bad men aim at, and promise themselves success in; but good work is God's work, and it shall prosper. God has many ways of bringing to light, and so of bringing to nought, the devices and designs of his church's enemies. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/nehemiah/chapter-4#Verses 7–15|Matthew Henry Concise on Nehemiah 4]]
- We must watch always against spiritual enemies, and not expect that our warfare will be over till our work is ended. The word of God is the sword of the Spirit, which we ought to have always at hand, and never to have to seek for it, either in our labours, or in our conflicts, as Christians. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/nehemiah/chapter-4#Verses 16–23|Matthew Henry Concise on Nehemiah 4]]
