# 2 Chronicles 15

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 15: The people make a solemn covenant with God. (1-19).

## Core claims

- The work of complete reformation appeared so difficult, that Asa had not courage to attempt it, till assured of Divine assistance and acceptance. He and his people offered sacrifices to God; thanksgiving for the favours they had received, and supplication for further favours.
