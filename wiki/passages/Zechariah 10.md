# Zechariah 10

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Zechariah 10: Blessings to be sought from the Lord. (1–5). God will restore his people. (6–12).

## Core claims

- Spiritual blessings had been promised under figurative allusions to earthly plenty. Seasonable rain is a great mercy, which we may ask of God when there is most need of it, and we may look for it to come. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/zechariah/chapter-10#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on Zechariah 10]]
- Here are precious promises to the people of God, which look to the state of the Jews, and even to the latter days of the church. Preaching the gospel is God's call for souls to come to Jesus Christ. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/zechariah/chapter-10#Verses 6–12|Matthew Henry Concise on Zechariah 10]]
