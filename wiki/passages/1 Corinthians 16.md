# 1 Corinthians 16

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Corinthians 16: A collection for the poor at Jerusalem. (1–9). Timothy and Apollos commended. (10–12). Exhortation to watchfulness in faith and love. (13–18). Christian salutations. (19–24).

## Core claims

- The good examples of other Christians and churches should rouse us. It is good to lay up in store for good uses. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-corinthians/chapter-16#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Corinthians 16]]
- Timothy came to do the work of the Lord. Therefore to vex his spirit, would be to grieve the Holy Spirit; to despise him, would be to despise Him that sent him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-corinthians/chapter-16#Verses 10–12|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Corinthians 16]]
- A Christian is always in danger, therefore should ever be on the watch. He should be fixed in the faith of the gospel, and never desert or give it up. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-corinthians/chapter-16#Verses 13–18|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Corinthians 16]]
- Christianity by no means destroys civility. Religion should promote a courteous and obliging temper towards all. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-corinthians/chapter-16#Verses 19–24|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Corinthians 16]]
