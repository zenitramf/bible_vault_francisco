# Luke 3

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Luke 3: John the Baptist's ministry. (1–14). John the Baptist testifies concerning Christ. (15–20). The baptism of Christ. (21, 22). The genealogy of Christ. (23–38).

## Core claims

- The scope and design of John's ministry were, to bring the people from their sins, and to their Saviour. He came preaching, not a sect, or party, but a profession; the sign or ceremony was washing with water. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/luke/chapter-3#Verses 1–14|Matthew Henry Concise on Luke 3]]
- John the Baptist disowned being himself the Christ, but confirmed the people in their expectations of the long-promised Messiah. He could only exhort them to repent, and assure them of forgiveness upon repentance; but he could not work repentance in them, nor confer remission on them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/luke/chapter-3#Verses 15–20|Matthew Henry Concise on Luke 3]]
- Christ did not confess sin, as others did, for he had none to confess; but he prayed, as others did, and kept up communion with his Father. Observe, all the three voices from heaven, by which the Father bare witness to the Son, were pronounced while he was praying, or soon after, Lu 9:35; Joh 12:28. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/luke/chapter-3#Verses 21, 22|Matthew Henry Concise on Luke 3]]
