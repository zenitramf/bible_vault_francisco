# Genesis 28

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 28: Isaac sends Jacob to Padan-aram. (1–5). Esau marries the daughter of Ishmael. (6–9). Jacob's vision. (10–15). The stone of Beth-el. (16–19). Jacob's vow. (20–22).

## Core claims

- Jacob had blessings promised both as to this world and that which is to come; yet goes out to a hard service. This corrected him for the fraud on his father. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-28#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 28]]
- Jacob's conduct hitherto, as recorded, was not that of one who simply feared and trusted in God. But now in trouble, obliged to flee, he looked only to God to make him to dwell in safety, and he could lie down and sleep in the open air with his head upon a stone. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-28#Verses 10–15|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 28]]
- Jacob made a solemn vow on this occasion. In this observe, 1. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-28#Verses 20–22|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 28]]
- We have here, I. Jacob parting with his parents, to go to Padan-aram; the charge his father gave him (Ge 28:1,2), the blessing he sent him away with (Ge 28:3,4), his obedience to the orders given him (Ge 28:5,10), and… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/genesis/chapter-28|Matthew Henry Complete on Genesis 28]]
