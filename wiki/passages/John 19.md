# John 19

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on John 19: Christ condemned and crucified. (1–18). Christ on the cross. (19–30). His side pierced. (31–37). The burial of Jesus. (38–42).

## Core claims

- Little did Pilate think with what holy regard these sufferings of Christ would, in after-ages, be thought upon and spoken of by the best and greatest of men. Our Lord Jesus came forth, willing to be exposed to their scorn. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/john/chapter-19#Verses 1–18|Matthew Henry Concise on John 19]]
- Here are some remarkable circumstances of Jesus' death, more fully related than before. Pilate would not gratify the chief priests by allowing the writing to be altered; which was doubtless owing to a secret power of God upon his heart, that this statement of our Lord's character and authority might continue. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/john/chapter-19#Verses 19–30|Matthew Henry Concise on John 19]]
- A trial was made whether Jesus was dead. He died in less time than persons crucified commonly did. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/john/chapter-19#Verses 31–37|Matthew Henry Concise on John 19]]
- Joseph of Arimathea was a disciple of Christ in secret. Disciples should openly own themselves; yet some, who in lesser trials have been fearful, in greater have been courageous. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/john/chapter-19#Verses 38–42|Matthew Henry Concise on John 19]]
- Though in the history hitherto this evangelist seems industriously to have declined the recording of such passages as had been related by the other evangelists, yet, when he comes to the sufferings and death of Christ,… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-5/john/chapter-19|Matthew Henry Complete on John 19]]
