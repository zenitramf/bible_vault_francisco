# Jeremiah 34

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 34: Zedekiah's death at Babylon foretold. (1-7). The Jews reproved for compelling their poor brethren to return to unlawful bondage. (8-22).

## Core claims

- Zedekiah is told that the city shall be taken, and that he shall die a captive, but he shall die a natural death. It is better to live and die penitent in a prison, than to live and die impenitent in a palace. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-34#Verses 1-7|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 34]]
- A Jew should not be held in servitude above seven years. This law they and their fathers had broken. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-34#Verses 8-22|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 34]]
- In this chapter we have two messages which God sent by Jeremiah. I. One to foretel the fate of Zedekiah king of Judah, that he should fall into the hands of the king of Babylon, that he should live a captive, but… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/jeremiah/chapter-34|Matthew Henry Complete on Jeremiah 34]]
