# Genesis 36

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 36: Esau and his descendants. (1-43).

## Core claims

- The registers in this chapter show the faithfulness of God to his promise to Abraham. Esau is here called Edom, that name which kept up the remembrance of his selling his birth-right for a mess of pottage. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-36#Verses 1-43|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 36]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
