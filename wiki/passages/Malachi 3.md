# Malachi 3

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Malachi 3: The coming of Christ. (1–6). The Jews reproved for their corruptions. (7–12). God's care of his people; The distinction between the righteous and the wicked. (13–18).

## Core claims

- The first words of this chapter seem an answer to the scoffers of those days. Here is a prophecy of the appearing of John the Baptist. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/malachi/chapter-3#Verses 1–6|Matthew Henry Concise on Malachi 3]]
- The men of that generation turned away from God, they had not kept his ordinances. God gives them a gracious call. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/malachi/chapter-3#Verses 7–12|Matthew Henry Concise on Malachi 3]]
- Among the Jews at this time, some plainly discovered themselves to be children of the wicked one. The yoke of Christ is easy. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/malachi/chapter-3#Verses 13–18|Matthew Henry Concise on Malachi 3]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
