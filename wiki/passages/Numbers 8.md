# Numbers 8

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 8: The lamps of the sanctuary. (1–4). Consecration of the Levites, and their service. (5–26).

## Core claims

- Aaron himself lighted the lamps, thus representing his Divine Master. The Scripture is a light shining in a dark place, 2Pe 1:19. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-8#Verses 1–4|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 8]]
- Here we have directions for the solemn ordination of the Levites. All Israel must know that they took not this honour to themselves, but were called of God to it; nor was it enough that they were distinguished from others. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-8#Verses 5–26|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 8]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
