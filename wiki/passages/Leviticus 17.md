# Leviticus 17

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 17: All sacrifices to be offered at the tabernacle. (1–9). Eating of blood, or of animals which died a natural death, forbidden. (10–16).

## Core claims

- All the cattle killed by the Israelites, while in the wilderness, were to be presented before the door of the tabernacle, and the flesh to be returned to the offerer, to be eaten as a peace-offering, according to the law. When they entered Canaan, this only continued in respect of sacrifices. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/leviticus/chapter-17#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 17]]
- Here is a confirmation of the law against eating blood. They must eat no blood. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/leviticus/chapter-17#Verses 10–16|Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 17]]
