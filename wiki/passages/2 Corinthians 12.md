## Agreements and tensions

Henry leaves thorn and mode of rapture open; McGee’s Lystra-stoning identification and three-heavens cosmology are outline inferences—hold beside Henry’s deliberate agnosticism on the mode. Both stress humility under revelation and strength perfected in weakness.
