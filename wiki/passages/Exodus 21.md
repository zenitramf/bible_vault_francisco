# Exodus 21

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 21: Laws respecting servants. (1–11). Judicial laws. (12–21). Judicial laws. (22–36).

## Core claims

- The laws in this chapter relate to the fifth and sixth commandments; and though they differ from our times and customs, nor are they binding on us, yet they explain the moral law, and the rules of natural justice. - God, who by his providence gives and maintains life, by his law protects it. A wilful murderer shall be taken even from God's altar. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-21#Verses 12–21|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 21]]
- The cases here mentioned give rules of justice then, and still in use, for deciding similar matters. We are taught by these laws, that we must be very careful to do no wrong, either directly or indirectly.
