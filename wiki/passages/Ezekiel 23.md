# Ezekiel 23

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 23: Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 23.

## Core claims

- In this parable, Samaria and Israel bear the name Aholah, "her own tabernacle;" because the places of worship those kingdoms had, were of their own devising. Jerusalem and Judah bear the name of Aholibah, "my tabernacle is in her," because their temple was the place which God himself had chosen, to put his name there.
