# Jeremiah 28

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 28: A false prophet opposes Jeremiah. (1-9). The false prophet warned of his approaching death. (10-17).

## Core claims

- Hananiah spoke a false prophecy. Here is not a word of good counsel urging the Jews to repent and return to God. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-28#Verses 1-9|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 28]]
- Hananiah is sentenced to die, and Jeremiah, when he has received direction from God, boldly tells him so; but not before he received that commission. Those have much to answer for, who tell sinners that they shall have peace, though they harden their hearts in contempt of God's word. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-28#Verses 10-17|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 28]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
