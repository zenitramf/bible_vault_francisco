## Core claims

- The word resurrection, usually points out our existence beyond the grave. Of the apostle's doctrine not a trace can be found in all the teaching of philosophers. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-corinthians/chapter-15#Verses 1–11|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Corinthians 15]]
- Having shown that Christ was risen, the apostle answers those who said there would be no resurrection. There had been no justification, or salvation, if Christ had not risen. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-corinthians/chapter-15#Verses 12–19|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Corinthians 15]]
