# Hosea 7

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 7: The manifold sins of Israel. (1-7). Their senselessness and hypocrisy. (8-16).

## Core claims

- A practical disbelief of God's government was at the bottom of all israel's wickedness; as if God could not see it or did not heed it. Their sins appear on every side of them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/hosea/chapter-7#Verses 1-7|Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 7]]
- Israel was as a cake not turned, half burnt and half dough, none of it fit for use; a mixture of idolatry and of the worship of Jehovah. There were tokens of approaching ruin, as grey hairs are of old age, but they noticed them not.
