# Numbers 18

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 18: The charge of the priests and Levites. (1–7). The priests' portion. (8–19). The Levites' portion. (20–32).

## Core claims

- The people complained of their difficulty and peril in drawing near to God. God here gives them to understand, that the priests should come near for them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-18#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 18]]
- All believers are spiritual priests, and God has promised to take care of them. Godliness has the promise of the life that now is. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-18#Verses 8–19|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 18]]
- As Israel was a people not to be numbered among the nations, so Levi was a tribe to be distinguished from the rest. Those who have God for their Inheritance and their Portion for ever, ought to look with holy contempt and indifference upon the possessions of this world. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-18#Verses 20–32|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 18]]
