# Genesis 44

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 44: Joseph's policy to stay his brethren, and try their affection for Benjamin. (1–17). Judah's supplication to Joseph. (18–34).

## Core claims

- Joseph tried how his brethren felt towards Benjamin. Had they envied and hated the other son of Rachel as they had hated him, and if they had the same want of feeling towards their father Jacob as heretofore, they would now have shown it. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-44#Verses 1–17|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 44]]
- Had Joseph been, as Judah supposed him, an utter stranger to the family, he could not but be wrought upon by his powerful reasonings. But neither Jacob nor Benjamin need an intercessor with Joseph; for he himself loved them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-44#Verses 18–34|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 44]]
