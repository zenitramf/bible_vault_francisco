# 2 Kings 3

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 3: Jehoram, king of Israel. (1–5). War with Moab, The intercession of Elisha. (6–19). Water supplied, Moab overcome. (20–27).

## Core claims

- Jehoram took warning by God's judgment, and put away the image of Baal, yet he maintained the worship of the calves. Those do not truly repent or reform, who only part with the sins they lose by, but continue to love the sins that they think to gain by. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-kings/chapter-3#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 3]]
- The king of Israel laments their distress, and the danger they were in. He called these kings together, yet he charges it upon Providence. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-kings/chapter-3#Verses 6–19|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 3]]
- It is a blessing to be favoured with the company of those who have power with God, and can prevail by their prayers. A kingdom may be upheld and prosper, in consequence of the fervent prayers of those who are dear to God. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-kings/chapter-3#Verses 20–27|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 3]]
