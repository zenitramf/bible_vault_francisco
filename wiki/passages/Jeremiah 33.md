# Jeremiah 33

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 33: The restoration of the Jews. (1-13). The Messiah promised; happiness of his times. (14-26).

## Core claims

- Those who expect to receive comforts from God, must call upon him. Promises are given, not to do away, but to quicken and encourage prayer. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-33#Verses 1-13|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 33]]
- To crown the blessings God has in store, here is a promise of the Messiah. He imparts righteousness to his church, for he is made of God to us righteousness; and believers are made the righteousness of God in him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-33#Verses 14-26|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 33]]
- The scope of this chapter is much the same with that of the foregoing chapter—to confirm the promise of the restoration of the Jews, notwithstanding the present desolations of their country and dispersions of their… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/jeremiah/chapter-33|Matthew Henry Complete on Jeremiah 33]]
