# Judges 6

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Judges 6: Israel oppressed by Midianites. (1–6). Israel rebuked by a prophet. (7–10). Gideon set to deliver Israel. (11–24). Gideon destroys Baal's altar. (25–32). Signs given him. (33–40).

## Core claims

- Israel's sin was renewed, and Israel's troubles were repeated. Let all that sin expect to suffer. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/judges/chapter-6#Verses 1–6|Matthew Henry Concise on Judges 6]]
- Gideon was a man of a brave, active spirit, yet in obscurity through the times: he is here stirred up to undertake something great. It was very sure that the Lord was with him, when his Angel was with him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/judges/chapter-6#Verses 11–24|Matthew Henry Concise on Judges 6]]
- These signs are truly miraculous, and very significant. Gideon and his men were going to fight the Midianites; could God distinguish between a small fleece of Israel, and the vast floor of Midian? [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/judges/chapter-6#Verses 33–40|Matthew Henry Concise on Judges 6]]
- Nothing that occurred in the quiet and peaceable times of Israel is recorded; the forty years' rest after the conquest of Jabin is passed over in silence; and here begins the story of another distress and another… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/judges/chapter-6|Matthew Henry Complete on Judges 6]]
