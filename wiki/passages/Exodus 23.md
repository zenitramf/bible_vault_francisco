# Exodus 23

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 23: Laws against falsehood and injustice. (1–9). The year of rest, The sabbath, The three festivals. (10–19). God promises to conduct the Israelites to Canaan. (20–33).

## Core claims

- In the law of Moses are very plain marks of sound moral feeling, and of true political wisdom. Every thing in it is suited to the desired and avowed object, the worship of one only God, and the separation of Israel from the pagan world. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-23#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 23]]
- Every seventh year the land was to rest. They must not plough or sow it; what the earth produced of itself, should be eaten, and not laid up. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-23#Verses 10–19|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 23]]
- It is here promised that they should be guided and kept in their way through the wilderness to the land of promise, Behold, I send an angel before thee, mine angel. The precept joined with this promise is, that they be obedient to this angel whom God would send before them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-23#Verses 20–33|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 23]]
- This chapter continues and concludes the acts that passed in the first session (if I may so call it) upon Mount Sinai. Here are, I. Some laws of universal obligation, relating especially to the ninth commandment,… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/exodus/chapter-23|Matthew Henry Complete on Exodus 23]]
