# Ezekiel 34

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 34: The rulers reproved. (1-6). The people are to be restored to their own land. (7-16). The kingdom of Christ. (17-31).

## Core claims

- The people became as sheep without a shepherd, were given up as a prey to their enemies, and the land was utterly desolated. No rank or office can exempt from the reproofs of God's word, men who neglect their duty, and abuse the trust reposed in them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-34#Verses 1-6|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 34]]
- The Lord declared that he intended mercy towards the scattered flock. Doubtless this, in the first place, had reference to the restoration of the Jews. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-34#Verses 7-16|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 34]]
- The whole nation seemed to be the Lord's flock, yet they were very different characters; but he knew how to distinguish between them. By good pastures and deep waters, are meant the pure word of God and the dispensing of justice. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-34#Verses 17-31|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 34]]
- The iniquities and calamities of God's Israel had been largely and pathetically lamented before, in this book. Now in this chapter the shepherds of Israel, their rulers both in church and state, are called to an… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/ezekiel/chapter-34|Matthew Henry Complete on Ezekiel 34]]
