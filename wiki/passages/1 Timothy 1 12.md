# 1 Timothy 1:12

## Summary

Paul's thanksgiving directs attention to Christ Jesus as the one who strengthens, regards as faithful, and appoints to service. The verse supports a view of ministry in which gratitude, divine enablement, entrusted responsibility, and faithfulness belong together.

## Core claims

- Ministry begins with gratitude to Christ rather than confidence in the minister. [[wiki/source-notes/Devotional on 1 Timothy 1 12#Summary|Source note]]
- Christ's enablement supplies the minister's sufficiency. [[wiki/source-notes/Devotional on 1 Timothy 1 12#Core claims|Source note]]
- Being placed into ministry entails accountable stewardship and expected faithfulness. [[wiki/source-notes/Devotional on 1 Timothy 1 12#Core claims|Source note]]

## Agreements and tensions

The present source applies the verse devotionally. Further exegetical work is needed on the immediate context, the meaning of Paul's being regarded as faithful, and how this apostolic testimony applies to other forms of ministry.
