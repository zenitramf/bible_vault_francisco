# 1 Samuel 7

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Samuel 7: The ark removed to Kirjath-jearim. (1–4). The Israelites solemnly repent. (5, 6). The Lord discomfits the Philistines. (7–12). They are subdued, Samuel judges Israel. (13–17).

## Core claims

- God will find a resting-place for his ark; if some thrust it from them, the hearts of others shall be inclined to receive it. It is no new thing for God's ark to be in a private house. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-samuel/chapter-7#Verses 1–4|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Samuel 7]]
- The Philistines invaded Israel. When sinners begin to repent and reform, they must expect that Satan will muster all his force against them, and set his instruments at work to the utmost, to oppose and discourage them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-samuel/chapter-7#Verses 7–12|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Samuel 7]]
- In this great revival of true religion, the ark was neither removed to Shiloh, nor placed with the tabernacle any where else.
