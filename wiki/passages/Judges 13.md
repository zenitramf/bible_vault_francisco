# Judges 13

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Judges 13: The Philistines, Samson announced. (1–7). The angel appears to Manoah. (8–14). Manoah's sacrifice. (15–23). Birth of Samson. (24, 25).

## Core claims

- Israel did evil: then God delivered them again into the hands of the Philistines. When Israel was in this distress, Samson was born. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/judges/chapter-13#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Judges 13]]
- What Manoah asked for instruction in his duty, he was readily told; but what he asked to gratify his curiosity, was denied. God has in his word given full directions concerning our duty, but never designed to answer other questionings. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/judges/chapter-13#Verses 15–23|Matthew Henry Concise on Judges 13]]
- The Spirit of the Lord began to move Samson when a youth. This was evidence that the Lord blessed him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/judges/chapter-13#Verses 24, 25|Matthew Henry Concise on Judges 13]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
