# Malachi 1

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Malachi 1: The ingratitude of Israel. (1–5). They are careless in God's institutions. (6–14).

## Core claims

- All advantages, either as to outward circumstances, or spiritual privileges, come from the free love of God, who makes one to differ from another. All the evils sinners feel and fear, are the just recompence of their crimes, while all their hopes and comforts are from the unmerited mercy of the Lord. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/malachi/chapter-1#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on Malachi 1]]
- We may each charge upon ourselves what is here charged upon the priests. Our relation to God, as our Father and Master, strongly obliges us to fear and honour him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/malachi/chapter-1#Verses 6–14|Matthew Henry Concise on Malachi 1]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
