# Ezekiel 35

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 35: A prophecy against Edom. (1-15).

## Core claims

- All who have God against them, have the word of God against them. Those that have a constant hatred to God and his people, as the carnal mind has, can only expect to be made desolate for ever. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-35#Verses 1-9|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 35]]
- When we see the vanity of the world in the disappointments, losses, and crosses, which others meet with, instead of showing ourselves greedy of worldly things, we should sit more loose to them. In the multitude of words, not one is unknown to God; not the most idle word; and the most daring is not above his rebuke.
