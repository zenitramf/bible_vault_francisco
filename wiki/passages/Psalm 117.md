# Psalm 117

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 117: All people called upon to praise God. (1-2).

## Core claims

- Here is a solemn call to all nations to praise the Lord, and proper matter for that praise is suggested. We are soon weary of well-doing, if we keep not up the pious and devout affections with which the spiritual sacrifice of praise ought to be kindled and kept burning. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-117#Verses 1-2|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 117]]
- This psalm is short and sweet; I doubt the reason why we sing it so often as we do is for the shortness of it; but, if we rightly understood and considered it, we should sing it oftener for the sweetness of it,… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-3/psalms/psalm-117|Matthew Henry Complete on Psalm 117]]
