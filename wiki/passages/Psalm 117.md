# Psalm 117

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 117: All people called upon to praise God. (1-2).

## Core claims

- Here is a solemn call to all nations to praise the Lord, and proper matter for that praise is suggested. We are soon weary of well-doing, if we keep not up the pious and devout affections with which the spiritual sacrifice of praise ought to be kindled and kept burning.
