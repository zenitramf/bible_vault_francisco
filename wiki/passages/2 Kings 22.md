# 2 Kings 22

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 22: Josiah's good reign, His care for repairing the temple, The book of the law found. (1–10). Josiah consults Huldah the prophetess. (11–20).

## Core claims

- The different event of Josiah's early succession from that of Manasseh, must be ascribed to the distinguishing grace of God; yet probably the persons that trained him up were instruments in producing this difference. His character was most excellent. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-kings/chapter-22#Verses 1–10|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 22]]
- The book of the law is read before the king. Those best honour their Bibles, who study them; daily feed on that bread, and walk by that light. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-kings/chapter-22#Verses 11–20|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 22]]
