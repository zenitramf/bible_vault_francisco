# Joshua 7

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 7: The Israelites smitten at Ai. (1–5). Joshua's humiliation and prayer. (6–9). God instructs Joshua what to do. (10–5). Achan is detected, He is destroyed. (16–26).

## Core claims

- Achan took some of the spoil of Jericho. The love of the world is that root of bitterness, which of all others is most hardly rooted up. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-7#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 7]]
- God awakens Joshua to inquiry, by telling him that when this accursed thing was put away, all would be well. Times of danger and trouble should be times of reformation. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-7#Verses 10–15|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 7]]
- See the folly of those that promise themselves secrecy in sin. The righteous God has many ways of bringing to light the hidden works of darkness. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-7#Verses 16–26|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 7]]
- More than once we have found the affairs of Israel, even when they were in the happiest posture and gave the most hopeful prospects, perplexed and embarrassed by sin, and a stop thereby put to the most promising… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/joshua/chapter-7|Matthew Henry Complete on Joshua 7]]
