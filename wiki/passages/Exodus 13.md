# Exodus 13

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 13: The first-born sanctified to God, The remembrance of the passover commanded. (1–10). The firstlings of beasts set apart. (11–16). Joseph's bones carried with the Israelites, They come to Etham. (17–20). God guideth the Israelites by a pillar of cloud fire. (21, 22).
