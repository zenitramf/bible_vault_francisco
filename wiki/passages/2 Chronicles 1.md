# 2 Chronicles 1

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 1: Solomon's choice of wisdom, His strength and wealth. (1-17).

## Core claims

- Solomon began his reign with a pious, public visit to God's altar. Those that pursue present things most eagerly, are likely to be disappointed; while those that refer themselves to the providence of God, if they have not the most, have the most comfort. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-1#Verses 1-17|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 1]]
