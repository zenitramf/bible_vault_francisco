# 1 Kings 10

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Kings 10: The queen of Sheba's visit to Solomon. (1–13). Solomon's wealth. (14–29).

## Core claims

- The queen of Sheba came to Solomon to hear his wisdom, thereby to improve her own. Our Saviour mentions her inquiries after God, by Solomon, as showing the stupidity of those who inquire not after God, by our Lord Jesus Christ. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-kings/chapter-10#Verses 1–13|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Kings 10]]
- Solomon increased his wealth. Silver was nothing accounted of. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-kings/chapter-10#Verses 14–29|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Kings 10]]
- Still Solomon looks great, and every thing in this chapter adds to his magnificence. We read nothing indeed of his charity, of no hospitals he built, or alms-houses; he made his kingdom so rich that it did not need… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/1-kings/chapter-10|Matthew Henry Complete on 1 Kings 10]]
