# Jeremiah 37

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 37: The Chaldean army will return. (1-10). Jeremiah is imprisoned. (11-21).

## Core claims

- Numbers witness the fatal effects of other men's sins, yet heedlessly step into their places, and follow the same destructive course. When in distress, we ought to desire the prayers of ministers and Christian friends. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-37#Verses 1-10|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 37]]
- There are times when it is the wisdom of good men to retire, to enter into their chambers, and to shut the doors, 20. Jeremiah was seized as a deserter, and committed to prison. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-37#Verses 11-21|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 37]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
