# Jeremiah 37

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 37: The Chaldean army will return. (1-10). Jeremiah is imprisoned. (11-21).

## Core claims

- Numbers witness the fatal effects of other men's sins, yet heedlessly step into their places, and follow the same destructive course.
