# 1 Chronicles 17

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Chronicles 17: David's purposes; God's gracious promises. (1-27). McGee treats the chapter as David’s temple desire that delights the Lord and the covenant God makes with David (parallel to 2 Samuel 7), within Chronicles’ altar-centered emphasis on David’s line leading to Messiah.

## Core claims

- This chapter is the same as 2Sa 7. See what is there said upon it. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-chronicles/chapter-17#Verses 1-27|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Chronicles 17]]
- This excellent chapter is the same with 2Sa 7:1-29 It will be worth while to look back upon what was there said upon it. Two things in general we have in it:—I. God's gracious acceptance of David's purpose to build him… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/1-chronicles/chapter-17|Matthew Henry Complete on 1 Chronicles 17]]
- McGee: David’s desire to build God a house delighted the Lord and is repeated here; God makes a covenant with David (see notes on 2 Samuel 7); Chronicles places this within God’s emphasis on David and the messianic line rather than mere political history. [[sources/commentaries_english/mcgee-thru-the-bible/1-2_Chronicles.pdf.extract.md|McGee Notes & Outlines on 1–2 Chronicles]] [[wiki/source-notes/McGee Notes and Outlines on 1 and 2 Chronicles|McGee Chronicles source-note]]

## Agreements and tensions

Complete commentary on this chapter deepens the Concise atlas with fuller pastoral and doctrinal exposition (Phase 4.1).

McGee agrees with Henry that the chapter parallels 2 Samuel 7 and centers acceptance of David’s temple purpose plus divine covenant promise; McGee adds the broader Kings-vs-Chronicles viewpoint (altar/God’s view) without replacing Henry’s pastoral reading.

Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
