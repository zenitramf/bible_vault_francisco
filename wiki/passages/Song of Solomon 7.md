# Song of Solomon 7

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Song of Solomon 7: The graces of the church. (1-9) . The delight of the church in Christ. (10-13).

## Core claims

- The similitudes here are different from what they were before, and in the original refer to glorious and splendid clothing. Such honour have all his saints; and having put on Christ, they are distinguished by their beautiful and glorious apparel. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/song-of-solomon/chapter-7#Verses 1-9|Matthew Henry Concise on Song of Solomon 7]]
- The church, the believing soul, triumphs in its relation to Christ, and interest in him. She humbly desires communion with him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/song-of-solomon/chapter-7#Verses 10-13|Matthew Henry Concise on Song of Solomon 7]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
