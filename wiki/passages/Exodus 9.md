# Exodus 9

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 9: The murrain of beasts. (1–7). The plague of boils and blains. (8–12). The plague of hail threatened. (13–21). The plague of hail inflicted. (22–35).

## Core claims

- God will have Israel released, Pharaoh opposes it, and the trial is, whose word shall stand. The hand of the Lord at once is upon the cattle, many of which, some of all kinds, die by a sort of murrain. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-9#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 9]]
- Moses is here ordered to deliver a dreadful message to Pharaoh. Providence ordered it, that Moses should have a man of such a fierce and stubborn spirit as this Pharaoh to deal with; and every thing made it a most signal instance of the power of God has to humble and bring down the proudest of his enemies. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-9#Verses 13–21|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 9]]
- Woful havoc this hail made: it killed both men and cattle; the corn above ground was destroyed, and that only preserved which as yet was not come up. The land of Goshen was preserved. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-9#Verses 22–35|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 9]]
- In this chapter we have an account of three more of the plagues of Egypt. I. Murrain among the cattle, which was fatal to them, Ex 9:1-7. II. Boils upon man and beast, Ex 9:8-12. III. Hail, with thunder and lightning.… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/exodus/chapter-9|Matthew Henry Complete on Exodus 9]]
