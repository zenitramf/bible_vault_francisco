# Romans 16

## Summary

Romans 16 closes with concrete Christian help (Phoebe), many named co-workers, a sharp warning against those who cause divisions contrary to sound doctrine, and a doxology: God establishes souls by the plain preaching of Jesus Christ unto the obedience of faith, and all glory goes to the only wise God through Jesus Christ.

## Core claims

- Christians should help one another in practical affairs, especially strangers; Phoebe is recommended as one who has been a helper of many. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-16#Verses 1–16|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 16]]
- Whatever differs from the sound doctrine of the Scriptures opens a door to divisions and offences; seducers often target soft consciences. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-16#Verses 17–20|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 16]]
- The God of peace will bruise Satan under the saints’ feet; grace of Christ is the ground of perseverance. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-16#Verses 17–20|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 16]]
- Souls are established by plain preaching of Jesus Christ; the gospel is revealed for the obedience of faith, and glory is ascribed to God through Christ forever. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/romans/chapter-16#Verses 25–27|Matthew Henry Concise on Romans 16]]
- Paul is now concluding this long and excellent epistle, and he does it with a great deal of affection. As in the main body of the epistle he appears to have been a very knowing man, so in these appurtenances of it he… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-6/romans/chapter-16|Matthew Henry Complete on Romans 16]]
