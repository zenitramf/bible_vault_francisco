# Numbers 5

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 5: The unclean to be removed out of the camp, Restitution to be made for trespasses. (1–10). The trial of jealousy. (11–31).

## Core claims

- The camp was to be cleansed. The purity of the church must be kept as carefully as the peace and order of it. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-5#Verses 1–10|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 5]]
- This law would make the women of Israel watch against giving cause for suspicion. On the other hand, it would hinder the cruel treatment such suspicions might occasion. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/numbers/chapter-5#Verses 11–31|Matthew Henry Concise on Numbers 5]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
