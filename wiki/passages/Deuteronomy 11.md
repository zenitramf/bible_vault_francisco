# Deuteronomy 11

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Deuteronomy 11: The great work God wrought for Israel. (1–7). Promises and threatenings. (8–17). Careful study of God's word requisite. (18–25). The blessings and the curse set forth. (26–32).

## Core claims

- Observe the connexion of these two; Thou shalt love the Lord, and keep his charge. Love will work in obedience, and that only is acceptable obedience which flows from a principle of love, 1Jo 5:3. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/deuteronomy/chapter-11#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Deuteronomy 11]]
- Let all be directed by the three rules here given. 1. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/deuteronomy/chapter-11#Verses 18–25|Matthew Henry Concise on Deuteronomy 11]]
- Moses sums up all the arguments for obedience in two words, the blessing and the curse. He charged the people to choose which they would have. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/deuteronomy/chapter-11#Verses 26–32|Matthew Henry Concise on Deuteronomy 11]]
- With this chapter Moses concludes his preface to the repetition of the statutes and judgments which they must observe to do. He repeats the general charge (De 11:1), and, having in the close of the foregoing chapter… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/deuteronomy/chapter-11|Matthew Henry Complete on Deuteronomy 11]]
