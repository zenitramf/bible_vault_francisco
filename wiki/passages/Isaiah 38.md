# Isaiah 38

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 38: Hezekiah's sickness and recovery. (1-8). His thanksgiving. (9-22).

## Core claims

- When we pray in our sickness, though God send not to us such an answer as he here sent to Hezekiah, yet, if by his Spirit he bids us be of good cheer, assures us that our sins are forgiven, and that, whether we live or die, we shall be his, we do not pray in vain. See II Kin. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-38#Verses 1-8|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 38]]
- We have here Hezekiah's thanksgiving. It is well for us to remember the mercies we receive in sickness. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-38#Verses 9-22|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 38]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
