# Jeremiah 10

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 10: The absurdity of idolatry. (1-16). Destruction denounced against Jerusalem. (17-25).

## Core claims

- The prophet shows the glory of Israel's God, and exposes the folly of idolaters. Charms and other attempts to obtain supernatural help, or to pry into futurity, are copied from the wicked customs of the heathen. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-10#Verses 1-16|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 10]]
- The Jews who continued in their own land, felt secure. But, sooner or later, sinners will find all things as the word of God has declared, and that its threatenings are not empty terrors. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-10#Verses 17-25|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 10]]
- We may conjecture that the prophecy of this chapter was delivered after the first captivity, in the time of Jeconiah or Jehoiachin, when many were carried away to Babylon; for it has a double reference:—I. To those… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/jeremiah/chapter-10|Matthew Henry Complete on Jeremiah 10]]
