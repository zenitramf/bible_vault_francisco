# Psalm 31

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 31: Confidence in God. (1–8). Prayer in trouble. (9–18). Praise for God's goodness. (19–24).

## Core claims

- Faith and prayer must go together, for the prayer of faith is the prevailing prayer. David gave up his soul in a special manner to God. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-31#Verses 1–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 31]]
- David's troubles made him a man of sorrows. Herein he was a type of Christ, who was acquainted with grief. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-31#Verses 9–18|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 31]]
- Instead of yielding to impatience or despondency under our troubles, we should turn our thoughts to the goodness of the Lord towards those who fear and trust in Him. All comes to sinners through the wondrous gift of the only-begotten Son of God, to be the atonement for their sins. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-31#Verses 19–24|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 31]]
