# 1 Timothy 2

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Timothy 2: Prayer to be made for all persons, since the grace of the gospel makes no difference of ranks or stations. (1–7). How men and women ought to behave, both in their religious and common life. (8–15).

## Core claims

- The disciples of Christ must be praying people; all, without distinction of nation, sect, rank, or party. Our duty as Christians, is summed up in two words; godliness, that is, the right worshipping of God; and honesty, that is, good conduct toward all men. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-timothy/chapter-2#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Timothy 2]]
- Under the gospel, prayer is not to be confined to any one particular house of prayer, but men must pray every where. We must pray in our closets, pray in our families, pray at our meals, pray when we are on journeys, and pray in the solemn assemblies, whether more public or private. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-timothy/chapter-2#Verses 8–15|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Timothy 2]]
