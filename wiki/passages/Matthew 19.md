# Matthew 19

## Summary

Marriage design, blessing children, the rich young ruler, and the impossibility of self-salvation versus God’s power.

## Core claims

- Marriage is God’s one-flesh design from the beginning; divorce is constrained by hard hearts. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-19#Verses 1, 2|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 19]]
- Children are welcomed; the kingdom belongs to such. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-19#Verses 13–15|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 19]]
- The rich young man lacks one thing—sell, give, follow; riches hinder entering the kingdom. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-19#Verses 16–22|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 19]]
- With men salvation is impossible, with God all things are possible; the first shall be last. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-19#Verses 23–30|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 19]]
