# Jeremiah 35

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 35: The obedience of the Rechabites. (1-11). The Jews' disobedience to the Lord. (12-19).

## Core claims

- Jonadab was famous for wisdom and piety. He lived nearly 300 years before, II Kin. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-35#Verses 1-11|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 35]]
- The trial of the Rechabites' constancy was for a sign; it made the disobedience of the Jews to God the more marked. The Rechabites were obedient to one who was but a man like themselves, and Jonadab never did for his seed what God has done for his people. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-35#Verses 12-19|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 35]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
