# Jeremiah 35

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 35: The obedience of the Rechabites. (1-11). The Jews' disobedience to the Lord. (12-19).

## Core claims

- Jonadab was famous for wisdom and piety. He lived nearly 300 years before, II Kin. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jeremiah/chapter-35#Verses 1-11|Matthew Henry Concise on Jeremiah 35]]
- The trial of the Rechabites' constancy was for a sign; it made the disobedience of the Jews to God the more marked. The Rechabites were obedient to one who was but a man like themselves, and Jonadab never did for his seed what God has done for his people.
