# Psalm 137

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 137: The Jews bewail their captivity. (1–4). Their affection for Jerusalem. (5–9).

## Core claims

- Their enemies had carried the Jews captive from their own land. To complete their woes, they insulted over them; they required of them mirth and a song. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-137#Verses 1–4|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 137]]
- What we love, we love to think of. Those that rejoice in God, for his sake make Jerusalem their joy. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-137#Verses 5–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 137]]
- There are divers psalms which are thought to have been penned in the latter days of the Jewish church, when prophecy was near expiring and the canon of the Old Testament ready to be closed up, but none of them appears… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-3/psalms/psalm-137|Matthew Henry Complete on Psalm 137]]
