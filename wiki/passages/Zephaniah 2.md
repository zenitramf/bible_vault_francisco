# Zephaniah 2

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Zephaniah 2: An exhortation to repentance. (1–3). Judgments upon other nations. (4–15).

## Core claims

- The prophet calls to national repentance, as the only way to prevent national ruin. A nation not desiring, that has not desires toward God, is not desirous of his favour and grace, has no mind to repent and reform. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/zephaniah/chapter-2#Verses 1–3|Matthew Henry Concise on Zephaniah 2]]
- Those are really in a woful condition who have the word of the Lord against them, for no word of his shall fall to the ground. God will restore his people to their rights, though long kept from them. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/zephaniah/chapter-2#Verses 4–15|Matthew Henry Concise on Zephaniah 2]]
- In this chapter we have, I. An earnest exhortation to the nation of the Jews to repent and make their peace with God, and so to prevent the judgments threatened before it was too late (Zep 2:1-3), and this inferred… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/zephaniah/chapter-2|Matthew Henry Complete on Zephaniah 2]]
