# Leviticus 18

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 18: Unlawful marriages and fleshly lusts. (1-30).

## Core claims

- Here is a law against all conformity to the corrupt usages of the heathen. Also laws against incest, against brutal lusts, and barbarous idolatries; and the enforcement of these laws from the ruin of the Canaanites. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/leviticus/chapter-18#Verses 1-30|Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 18]]
