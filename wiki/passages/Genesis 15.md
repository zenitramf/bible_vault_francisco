# Genesis 15

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 15: God encourages Abram. (1). The Divine promise, Abraham is justified by faith. (2–6). God promises Canaan to Abraham for an inheritance. (7–11). The promise confirmed in a vision. (12–16). The promise confirmed by a sign. (17–21).

## Core claims

- God assured Abram of safety and happiness; that he should for ever be safe. I am thy shield; or, I am a shield to thee, present with thee, actually caring for thee. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-15#Verse 1|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 15]]
- Assurance was given to Abram of the land of Canaan for an inheritance. God never promises more than he is able to perform, as men often do. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-15#Verses 7–11|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 15]]
- The smoking furnace and the burning lamp, probably represented the Israelites' severe trials and joyful deliverance, with their gracious supports in the mean time.
