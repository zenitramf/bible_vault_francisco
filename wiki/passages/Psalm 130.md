# Psalm 130

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 130: The psalmist's hope in prayer. (1–4). His patience in hope. (5–8).

## Core claims

- The only way of relief for a sin-entangled soul, is by applying to God alone. Many things present themselves as diversions, many things offer themselves as remedies, but the soul finds that the Lord alone can heal. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-130#Verses 1–4|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 130]]
- It is for the Lord that my soul waits, for the gifts of his grace, and the working of his power. We must hope for that only which he has promised in his word. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-130#Verses 5–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 130]]
- This psalm relates not to any temporal concern, either personal or public, but it is wholly taken up with the affairs of the soul. It is reckoned one of the seven penitential psalms, which have sometimes been made use… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-3/psalms/psalm-130|Matthew Henry Complete on Psalm 130]]
