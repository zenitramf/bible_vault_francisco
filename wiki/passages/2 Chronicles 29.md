# 2 Chronicles 29

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 29: Hezekiah's good reign in Judah. (1–19). Hezekiah's sacrifice of atonement. (20–36).

## Core claims

- When Hezekiah came to the crown, he applied at once to work reform. Those who begin with God, begin at the right end of their work, and it will prosper accordingly. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-29#Verses 1–19|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 29]]
- As soon as Hezekiah heard that the temple was ready, he lost no time. Atonement must be made for the sins of the last reign. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-29#Verses 20–36|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 29]]
