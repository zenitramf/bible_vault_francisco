# Judges 19

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Judges 19: The wickedness of the men of Gibeah. (1-30).

## Core claims

- The three remaining chapters of this book contain a very sad history of the wickedness of the men of Gibeah, in Benjamin. The righteous Lord permits sinners to execute just vengeance on one another, and if the scene here described is horrible, what will the discoveries of the day of judgment be! [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/judges/chapter-19#Verses 1-30|Matthew Henry Concise on Judges 19]]
