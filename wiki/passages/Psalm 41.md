# Psalm 41

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 41: God's care for his people. (1–4). The treachery of David's enemies. (5–13).

## Core claims

- The people of God are not free from poverty, sickness, or outward affliction, but the Lord will consider their case, and send due supplies. From his Lord's example the believer learns to consider his poor and afflicted brethren. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-41#Verses 1–4|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 41]]
- We complain, and justly, of the want of sincerity, and that there is scarcely any true friendship to be found among men; but the former days were no better. One particularly, in whom David had reposed great confidence, took part with his enemies. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-41#Verses 5–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 41]]
- God's kindness and truth have often been the support and comfort of the saints when they have had most experience of man's unkindness and treachery.
