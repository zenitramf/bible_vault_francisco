# Ezekiel 22

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 22: The sins of Jerusalem. (1-16). Israel is condemned as dross. (17-22). As the corruption is general, so shall be the punishment. (23-31).

## Core claims

- The prophet is to judge the bloody city; the city of bloods. Jerusalem is so called, because of her crimes. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-22#Verses 1-16|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 22]]
- Israel, compared with other nations, had been as the gold and silver compared with baser metals. But they were now as the refuse that is consumed in the furnace, or thrown away when the silver is refined. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-22#Verses 17-22|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 22]]
- All orders and degrees of men had helped to fill the measure of the nation's guilt. The people that had any power abused it, and even the buyers and sellers find some way to oppress one another. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-22#Verses 23-31|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 22]]
- Here are three separate messages which God entrusts the prophet to deliver concerning Judah and Jerusalem, and all to the same purport, to show them their sins and the judgments that were coming upon them for those… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/ezekiel/chapter-22|Matthew Henry Complete on Ezekiel 22]]
