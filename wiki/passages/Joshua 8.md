# Joshua 8

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 8: God encourages Joshua. (1, 2). The taking of Ai. (3–22). The destruction of Ai and its king. (23–29). The law read on Ebal and Gerizim. (30–35).

## Core claims

- When we have faithfully put away sin, that accursed thing which separates between us and God, then, and not till then, we may look to hear from God to our comfort; and God's directing us how to go on in our Christian work and warfare, is a good evidence of his being reconciled to us. God encouraged Joshua to proceed. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-8#Verses 1, 2|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 8]]
- God, the righteous Judge, had sentenced the Canaanites for their wickedness; the Israelites only executed his doom. None of their conduct can be drawn into an example for others. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-8#Verses 23–29|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 8]]
- As soon as Joshua got to the mountains Ebal and Gerizim, without delay, and without caring for the unsettled state of Israel, or their enemies, he confirmed the covenant of the Lord with his people, as appointed, De 11; 27.
