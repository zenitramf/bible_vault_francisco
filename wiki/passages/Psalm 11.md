# Psalm 11

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 11: David's struggle with, and triumph over a strong temptation to distrust God, and betake himself to indirect means for his own safety, in a time of danger. (1-7).

## Core claims

- Those that truly fear God and serve him, are welcome to put their trust in him. The psalmist, before he gives an account of his temptation to distrust God, records his resolution to trust in Him, as that by which he was resolved to live and die. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-11#Verses 1-7|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 11]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
