# Jonah 2

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Jonah 2: The prayer of Jonah. (1–9). He is delivered from the fish. (10).

## Core claims

- Observe when Jonah prayed. When he was in trouble, under the tokens of God's displeasure against him for sin: when we are in affliction we must pray. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jonah/chapter-2#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Jonah 2]]
- Jonah's deliverance may be considered as an instance of God's power over all the creatures. As an instance of God's mercy to a poor penitent, who in distress prays to him: and as a type and figure of Christ's resurrection. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/jonah/chapter-2#Verse 10|Matthew Henry Concise on Jonah 2]]
