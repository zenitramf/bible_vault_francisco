# Leviticus 22

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 22: Laws concerning the priests and sacrifices. (1-33).

## Core claims

- In this chapter we have divers laws concerning the priests and sacrifices, all for preserving the honour of the sanctuary. Let us recollect with gratitude that our great High Priest cannot be hindered by any thing from the discharge of his office.
