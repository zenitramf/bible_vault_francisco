# Genesis 49

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 49: Jacob calls his sons to bless them. (1, 2). Reuben, Simeon, Levi. (3–7). Judah. (8–12). Zebulun, Issachar, Dan. (13–18). Gad, Asher, Naphtali. (19–21). Joseph and Benjamin. (22–27). Jacob's charge respecting his burial, His death. (28–33).

## Core claims

- All Jacob's sons were living. His calling them together was a precept for them to unite in love, not to mingle with the Egyptians; and foretold that they should not be separated, as Abraham's sons and Isaac's were, but should all make one people. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-49#Verses 1, 2|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 49]]
- Concerning Zebulun: if prophecy says, Zebulun shall be a haven of ships, be sure Providence will so plant him. God appoints the bounds of our habitation. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-49#Verses 13–18|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 49]]
- Jacob blessed every one according to the blessings God in after-times intended to bestow upon them. He spoke about his burial-place, from a principle of faith in the promise of God, that Canaan should be the inheritance of his seed in due time. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-49#Verses 28–33|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 49]]
- This chapter is a prophecy; the likest to it we have yet met with was that of Noah, Ge 9:25-27, etc. Jacob is here upon his death-bed, making his will.
