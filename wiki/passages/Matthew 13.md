# Matthew 13

## Summary

Kingdom parables explain varied reception of the word, mixed growth until harvest, costly treasure, and Nazareth’s unbelief.

## Core claims

- Parables both reveal and conceal; the sower shows varied soils of the heart receiving the word. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-13#Verses 1–23|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 13]]
- Wheat and tares grow together until harvest; the kingdom’s growth is like mustard seed and leaven. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-13#Verses 24–30|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 13]]
- The kingdom is a treasure and pearl worth all; the net gathers good and bad until the end. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-13#Verses 44–52|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 13]]
- Nazareth’s unbelief limits what he does there; a prophet is not without honor except in his own country. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-13#Verses 53–58|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 13]]
- In this chapter, we have, I. The favour which Christ did to his countrymen in preaching the kingdom of heaven to them, Mt 13:1-2.
