## Core claims

- A sluggish, slothful disposition makes men poor; it brings them to want. And this applies both to the present life and that which is to come. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-19#Verse 15|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 19]]
- The unbelief of man shall not make God's threatenings of no effect. Christ himself, when bearing sins not his own, was not spared. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-19#Verse 29|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 19]]
