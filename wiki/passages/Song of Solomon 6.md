# Song of Solomon 6

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Song of Solomon 6: Inquiry where Christ must be sought. (1) . Where Christ may be found. (2,3) . Christ's commendations of the church. (4-10) . The work of grace in the believer. (11-13).

## Core claims

- Those made acquainted with the excellences of Christ, and the comfort of an interest in him, desire to know where they may meet him. Those who would find Christ, must seek him early and diligently. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/song-of-solomon/chapter-6#Verse 1|Matthew Henry Concise on Song of Solomon 6]]
- All the real excellence and holiness on earth centre in the church. Christ goes forth subduing his enemies, while his followers gain victories over the world, the flesh, and the devil. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/song-of-solomon/chapter-6#Verses 4-10|Matthew Henry Concise on Song of Solomon 6]]
- In retirement and in meditation the Christian character is formed and perfected. But not in the retirement of the idle, the self-indulgent, or the trifler. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/song-of-solomon/chapter-6#Verses 11-13|Matthew Henry Concise on Song of Solomon 6]]
- In this chapter, I. The daughters of Jerusalem, moved with the description which the church had given of Christ, enquire after him, So 6:1. II. The church directs them where they may meet with him, So 6:2,3. III.… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-3/song-of-solomon/chapter-6|Matthew Henry Complete on Song of Solomon 6]]
