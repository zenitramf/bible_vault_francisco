# Exodus 40

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 40: The tabernacle is to be set up, Aaron and his sons to be sanctified. (1–15). Moses performs all as directed. (16–33). The glory of the Lord fills the tabernacle. (34–38).

## Core claims

- When a new year begins, we should seek to serve God better than the year before. In half a year the tabernacle was completed. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-40#Verses 1–15|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 40]]
- When the tabernacle and the furniture of it were prepared, they did not put off rearing it till they came to Canaan; but, in obedience to the will of God, they set it up in the midst of their camp. Those who are unsettled in the world, must not think that this will excuse want of religion; as if it were enough to… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-40#Verses 16–33|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 40]]
- The cloud covered the tabernacle even in the clearest day; it was not a cloud which the sun scatters. This cloud was a token of God's presence to be seen day and night, by all Israel, that they might never again question, Is the Lord among us, or is he not? [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/exodus/chapter-40#Verses 34–38|Matthew Henry Concise on Exodus 40]]
