# Hosea 12

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 12: Judah and Israel reminded of the Divine favours. (1-6). The provocations of Israel. (7-14).

## Core claims

- Ephraim feeds himself with vain hopes of help from man, when he is at enmity with God. The Jews vainly thought to secure the Egyptians by a present of the produce of their country. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/hosea/chapter-12#Verses 1-6|Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 12]]
- Ephraim became a merchant: the word also signifies a Canaanite. They carried on trade upon Canaanitish principles, covetously and with fraud and deceit. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/hosea/chapter-12#Verses 7-14|Matthew Henry Concise on Hosea 12]]
- In this chapter we have, I. A high charge drawn up against both Israel and Judah for their sins, which were the ground of God's controversy with them, Ho 12:1,2. Particularly the sin of fraud and injustice, which… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-4/hosea/chapter-12|Matthew Henry Complete on Hosea 12]]
