# Isaiah 1

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 1: The corruptions prevailing among the Jews. (1-9). Severe censures. (10-15). Exhortations to repentance. (16-20). The state of Judah is lamented; with gracious promises of the gospel times. (21-31).

## Core claims

- Isaiah signifies, "The salvation of the Lord;" a very suitable name for this prophet, who prophesies so much of Jesus the Saviour, and his salvation. God's professing people did not know or consider that they owed their lives and comforts to God's fatherly care and kindness. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-1#Verses 1-9|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 1]]
- Not only feel sorrow for the sin committed, but break off the practice. We must be doing, not stand idle. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-1#Verses 16-20|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 1]]
- Neither holy cities nor royal ones are faithful to their trust, if religion does not dwell in them. Dross may shine like silver, and the wine that is mixed with water may still have the colour of wine. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/isaiah/chapter-1#Verses 21-31|Matthew Henry Concise on Isaiah 1]]
