# 3 John 1

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 3 John 1: The apostle commends Gaius for piety and hospitality. (1–8). Cautions him against siding with Diotrephes, who was a turbulent spirit; but recommends Demetrius as a man of excellent character. (9–12). He hopes soon to ….

## Core claims

- Those who are beloved of Christ, will love the brethren for his sake. Soul prosperity is the greatest blessing on this side heaven. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/3-john/chapter-1#Verses 1–8|Matthew Henry Concise on 3 John 1]]
- Both the heart and mouth must be watched. The temper and spirit of Diotrephes was full of pride and ambition. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/3-john/chapter-1#Verses 9–12|Matthew Henry Concise on 3 John 1]]
- Here is the character of Demetrius. A name in the gospel, or a good report in the churches, is better than worldly honour. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/3-john/chapter-1#Verses 13, 14|Matthew Henry Concise on 3 John 1]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
