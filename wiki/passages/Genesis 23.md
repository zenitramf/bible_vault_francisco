# Genesis 23

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 23: The death of Sarah, Abraham applies for a burying-place. (1–13). Sarah's burying-place. (14–20).

## Core claims

- The longest life must shortly come to a close. Blessed be God that there is a world where sin, death, vanity, and vexation cannot enter. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-23#Verses 1–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 23]]
- Prudence, as well as justice, directs us to be fair and open in our dealings; cheating bargains will not bear the light. Abraham, without fraud or delay, pays the money. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-23#Verses 14–20|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 23]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
