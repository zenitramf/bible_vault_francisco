# Nehemiah 12

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Nehemiah 12: The priests and Levites that returned. (1–26). The dedication of the wall. (27–43). The officers of the temple settled. (44–47).

## Core claims

- It is a debt we owe to faithful ministers, to remember our guides, who have spoken to us the word of God. It is good to know what our godly predecessors were, that we may learn what we should be. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/nehemiah/chapter-12#Verses 1–26|Matthew Henry Concise on Nehemiah 12]]
- All our cities, all our houses, must have holiness to the Lord written upon them. The believer should undertake nothing which he does not dedicate to the Lord. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/nehemiah/chapter-12#Verses 27–43|Matthew Henry Concise on Nehemiah 12]]
- When the solemnities of a thanksgiving day leave such impressions on ministers and people, that both are more careful and cheerful in doing their duty, they are indeed acceptable to the Lord, and turn to good account.
