# Genesis 33

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 33: The friendly meeting of Jacob and Esau. (1–16). Jacob comes to Succoth and Shalem, He builds an altar. (17–20).

## Core claims

- Jacob, having by prayer committed his case to God, went on his way. Come what will, nothing can come amiss to him whose heart is fixed, trusting in God. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-33#Verses 1–16|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 33]]
- Jacob did not content himself with words of thanks for God's favour to him, but gave real thanks. Also he kept up religion, and the worship of God in his family. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-33#Verses 17–20|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 33]]
- We read, in the former chapter, how Jacob had power with God, and prevailed; here we find what power he had with men too, and how his brother Esau was mollified, and, on a sudden, reconciled to him; for so it is… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/genesis/chapter-33|Matthew Henry Complete on Genesis 33]]
