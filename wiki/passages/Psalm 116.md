# Psalm 116

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 116: The psalmist declares his love to the Lord. (1–9). His desire to be thankful. (10–19).

## Core claims

- We have many reasons for loving the Lord, but are most affected by his loving-kindness when relieved out of deep distress. When a poor sinner is awakened to a sense of his state, and fears that he must soon sink under the just wrath of God, then he finds trouble and sorrow. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-116#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 116]]
- When troubled, we do best to hold our peace, for we are apt to speak unadvisedly. Yet there may be true faith where there are workings of unbelief; but then faith will prevail; and being humbled for our distrust of God's word, we shall experience his faithfulness to it. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-116#Verses 10–19|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 116]]
