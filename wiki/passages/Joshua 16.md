# Joshua 16

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 16: The sons of Joseph. (1-31).

## Core claims

- This and the following chapter should not be separated. They give the lots of Ephraim and Manasseh, the children of Joseph, who, next to Judah, were to have the post of honour, and therefore had the first and best portion in the northern part of Canaan, as Judah in the southern part. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/joshua/chapter-16#Verses 1-31|Matthew Henry Concise on Joshua 16]]

## Agreements and tensions

This chapter synthesis is drawn from Matthew Henry Concise only. Later work may add Matthew Henry Complete and Spurgeon on the same pericopes. Concise notes are pastoral summaries, not full critical commentary.
