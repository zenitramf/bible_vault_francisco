# 2 Kings 17

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 17: Reign of Hoshea in Israel, The israelites carried captives by the Assyrians. (1–6). Captivity of the Israelites. (7–23). The nations placed in the land of Israel. (24–41).

## Core claims

- When the measure of sin is filled up, the Lord will forbear no longer. The inhabitants of Samaria must have endured great affliction. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-kings/chapter-17#Verses 1–6|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 17]]
- Though the destruction of the kingdom of the ten tribes was but briefly related, it is in these verses largely commented upon, and the reasons of it given. It was destruction from the Almighty: the Assyrian was but the rod of his anger, Isa 10:5. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-kings/chapter-17#Verses 7–23|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Kings 17]]
