# Psalm 139

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 139: God knows all things. (1–6). He is every where present. (7–16). The psalmist's hatred to sin, and desire to be led aright. (17–24).

## Core claims

- God has perfect knowledge of us, and all our thoughts and actions are open before him. It is more profitable to meditate on Divine truths, applying them to our own cases, and with hearts lifted to God in prayer, than with a curious or disputing frame of mind. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-139#Verses 1–6|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 139]]
- We cannot see God, but he can see us. The psalmist did not desire to go from the Lord. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-139#Verses 7–16|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 139]]
- God's counsels concerning us and our welfare are deep, such as cannot be known. We cannot think how many mercies we have received from him. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/psalms/psalm-139#Verses 17–24|Matthew Henry Concise on Psalm 139]]
