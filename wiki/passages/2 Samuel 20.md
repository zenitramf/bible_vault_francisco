# 2 Samuel 20

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Samuel 20: Sheba's rebellion. (1–3). Amasa slain by Joab. (4–13). Sheba takes refuge in Abel. (14–22). David's officers. (23–26).

## Core claims

- One trial arises after another for our good, till we reach the place where sin and sorrow are for ever done away. Angry disputers misunderstand or misconstrue one another's words; proud men will have every thing their own way, or wholly refuse their assistance. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-samuel/chapter-20#Verses 1–3|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Samuel 20]]
- Justly is that place attacked, which dares to harbour a traitor; nor will the heart fare better which indulges rebellious lusts, that will not have Christ to reign over them. A discreet woman, by her prudent management, satisfied Joab, and yet saved the city. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-samuel/chapter-20#Verses 14–22|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Samuel 20]]
- Here is the state of David's court, after his restoration. It is well when able men are appointed to discharge public duties; let all seek to perform those duties, as faithful servants to the Son of David. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-samuel/chapter-20#Verses 23–26|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Samuel 20]]
- How do the clouds return after the rain! No sooner is one of David's troubles over than another arises, as it were out of the ashes of the former, wherein the threatening is fulfilled, that the sword should never… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/2-samuel/chapter-20|Matthew Henry Complete on 2 Samuel 20]]
