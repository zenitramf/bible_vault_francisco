# Mark 2

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Mark 2: Christ heals one sick of the palsy. (1–12). Levi's call, and the entertainment given to Jesus. (13–17). Why Christ's disciples did not fast. (18–22). He justifies his disciples for plucking corn on the sabbath. (23–28).

## Core claims
