## Core claims

- A right cause will be better pleaded with meekness than with passion. Nothing stirs up anger like grievous words. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-15#Verse 1|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 15]]
- He that is slow to anger, not only prevents strife, but appeases it, if kindled. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-15#Verse 18|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 15]]
- The fear of the Lord will dispose us to search the Scriptures with reverence; and it will cause us to follow the leadings of the Holy Spirit. While we humbly place all our dependence on the grace of God, we are exalted in the righteousness of Christ.
