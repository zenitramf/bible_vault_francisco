# Proverbs 2

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 2: Promises to those who seek wisdom. (1–9). The advantages of wisdom. (10–22).

## Core claims

- Those who earnestly seek heavenly wisdom, will never complain that they have lost their labour; and the freeness of the gift does not do away the necessity of our diligence, Joh 6:27 Let them seek, and they shall find it; let them ask, and it shall be given them. Observe who are thus favoured. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/proverbs/chapter-2#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on Proverbs 2]]
- If we are truly wise, we shall be careful to avoid all evil company and evil practices. When wisdom has dominion over us, then it not only fills the head, but enters into the heart, and will preserve, both against corruptions within and temptations without.
