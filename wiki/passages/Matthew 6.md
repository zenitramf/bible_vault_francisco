# Matthew 6

## Summary

Matthew 6 opposes hypocrisy in alms, prayer, and fasting; teaches the Lord’s Prayer; and warns against worldly treasure and anxious care.

## Core claims

- Almsgiving, prayer, and fasting must spring from inward principle for God’s approval, not human praise. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-6#Verses 1–4|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 6]]
- Living disciples pray; prayer must not be hypocritical display but secret address to the Father. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-6#Verses 5–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 6]]
- Christ gives matter and method for prayer—the Lord’s Prayer—without binding to this form alone. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-6#Verses 9–15|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 6]]
- Worldly-mindedness and anxious care about this life are fatal symptoms under religious profession. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/matthew/chapter-6#Verses 19–24|Matthew Henry Concise on Matthew 6]]
- Christ having, in the former chapter, armed his disciples against the corrupt doctrines and opinions of the scribes and Pharisees, especially in their expositions of the law (that was called their leaven, Mt 16:12),… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-5/matthew/chapter-6|Matthew Henry Complete on Matthew 6]]
