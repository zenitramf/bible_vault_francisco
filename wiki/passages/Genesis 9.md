# Genesis 9

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 9: God blesses Noah, and grants flesh for food. (1–3). Blood, and murder forbidden. (4–7). God's covenant by the rainbow. (8–17). Noah plants a vineyard, is drunken and mocked by Ham. (18–23). Noah curses Canaan, blesses Shem, prays for Japheth, His death. (24–29).

## Core claims

- The blessing of God is the cause of our doing well. On him we depend, to him we should be thankful. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-9#Verses 1–3|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 9]]
- As the old world was ruined, to be a monument of justice, so this world remains to this day a monument of mercy. But sin, that drowned the old world, will burn this. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-9#Verses 8–17|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 9]]
- Noah declares a curse on Canaan, the son of Ham; perhaps this grandson of his was more guilty than the rest. A servant of servants, that is, The meanest and most despicable servant, shall he be, even to his brethren. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/genesis/chapter-9#Verses 24–29|Matthew Henry Concise on Genesis 9]]
- Both the world and the church were now again reduced to a family, the family of Noah, of the affairs of which this chapter gives us an account, of which we are the more concerned to take cognizance because from this… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-1/genesis/chapter-9|Matthew Henry Complete on Genesis 9]]
