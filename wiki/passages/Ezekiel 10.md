# Ezekiel 10

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 10: A vision of the burning of the city. (1-7). The Divine glory departing from the temple. (8-22).

## Core claims

- The fire being taken from between the wheels, under the cherubim, 13, seems to have signified the wrath of God to be executed upon Jerusalem. It intimated that the fire of Divine wrath, which kindles judgment upon a people, is just and holy; and in the great day, the earth, and all the works that are therein, will be… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-10#Verses 1-7|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 10]]
- Ezekiel sees the working of Divine providence in the government of the lower world, and the affairs of it. When God is leaving a people in displeasure, angels above, and all events below, further his departure. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/ezekiel/chapter-10#Verses 8-22|Matthew Henry Concise on Ezekiel 10]]
