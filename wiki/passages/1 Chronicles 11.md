# 1 Chronicles 11

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Chronicles 11: David raised to the throne. (1–9). A list of David's mighty men. (10–47).

## Core claims

- David was brought to possess the throne of Israel after he had reigned seven years in Hebron, over Judah only. God's counsels will be fulfilled at last, whatever difficulties lie in the way. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-chronicles/chapter-11#Verses 1–9|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Chronicles 11]]
- An account is given of David's worthies, the great men who served him. Yet David reckoned his success, not as from the mighty men that were with him, but from the mighty God, whose presence is all in all. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-chronicles/chapter-11#Verses 10–47|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Chronicles 11]]
- In this chapter is repeated, I. The elevation of David to the throne, immediately upon the death of Saul, by common consent, 1Ch 11:1-3. II. His gaining the castle of Zion out of the hands of the Jebusites, 1Ch 11:4-9.… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/1-chronicles/chapter-11|Matthew Henry Complete on 1 Chronicles 11]]
