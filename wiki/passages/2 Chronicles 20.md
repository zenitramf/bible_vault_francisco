# 2 Chronicles 20

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 20: The danger and distress of Judah. (1–13). Jahaziel's prophecy of victory. (14–19). The thanksgiving of Judah. (20–30). Jehoshaphat's alliance with Ahaziah. (31–37).

## Core claims

- In all dangers, public or personal, our first business should be to seek help from God. Hence the advantage of days for national fasting and prayer. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-20#Verses 1–13|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 20]]
- Jehoshaphat exhorted his troops to firm faith in God. Faith inspires a man with true courage; nor will any thing help more to the establishing of the heart in shaking times, than a firm belief of the power, and mercy, and promise of God. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-20#Verses 20–30|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 20]]
- Jehoshaphat kept close to the worship of God, and did what he could to keep his people close to it. But after God had done such great things for him, given him not only victory, but wealth; after this, to go and join himself with a wicked king, was very ungrateful. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/2-chronicles/chapter-20#Verses 31–37|Matthew Henry Concise on 2 Chronicles 20]]
- We have here, I. The great danger and distress that Jehoshaphat and his kingdom were in from a foreign invasion, 2Ch 20:1,2. II. The pious course he took for their safety, by fasting, and praying, and seeking God, 2Ch… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/2-chronicles/chapter-20|Matthew Henry Complete on 2 Chronicles 20]]
