# Job 10

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Job 10: Job complains of his hardships. (1–7). He pleads with God as his Maker. (8–13). He complains of God's severity. (14–22).

## Core claims

- Job, being weary of his life, resolves to complain, but he will not charge God with unrighteousness. Here is a prayer that he might be delivered from the sting of his afflictions, which is sin. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/job/chapter-10#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on Job 10]]
- Job seems to argue with God, as if he only formed and preserved him for misery. God made us, not we ourselves. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/job/chapter-10#Verses 8–13|Matthew Henry Concise on Job 10]]
- Job did not deny that as a sinner he deserved his sufferings; but he thought that justice was executed upon him with peculiar rigour.
