# 1 Samuel 27

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Samuel 27: David retires to Gath. (1–7). David deceives Achish. (8–12).

## Core claims

- Unbelief is a sin that easily besets even good men, when without are fightings, and within are fears; and it is a hard matter to get over them. Lord, increase our faith! [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-samuel/chapter-27#Verses 1–7|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Samuel 27]]
- While David was in the land of the Philistines, he attacked some remains of the devoted nations. The people whom he cut off were long before doomed to destruction. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-samuel/chapter-27#Verses 8–12|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Samuel 27]]
- David was a man after God's own heart, and yet he had his faults, which are recorded, not for our imitation, but for our admonition; witness the story of this chapter, in which, though, I. We find, to his praise, that… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-2/1-samuel/chapter-27|Matthew Henry Complete on 1 Samuel 27]]
