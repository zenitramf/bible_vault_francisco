# 1 Timothy 4

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Timothy 4: Of departures from the faith that began already to appear. (1–5). Several directions, with motives for due discharge of duties. (6–16).

## Core claims

- The Holy Spirit, both in the Old and the New Testament, spoke of a general turning from the faith of Christ, and the pure worship of God. This should come during the Christian dispensation, for those are called the latter days. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-timothy/chapter-4#Verses 1–5|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Timothy 4]]
- Outward acts of self-denial profit little. What will it avail us to mortify the body, if we do not mortify sin? [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-timothy/chapter-4#Verses 6–10|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Timothy 4]]
- Men's youth will not be despised, if they keep from vanities and follies. Those who teach by their doctrine, must teach by their life. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/1-timothy/chapter-4#Verses 11–16|Matthew Henry Concise on 1 Timothy 4]]
- Paul here foretels, I. A dreadful apostasy, 1Ti 4:1-3. II. He treats of Christian liberty, 1Ti 4:4,5. III. He gives Timothy divers directions with respect to himself, his doctrine, and the people under his care, 1Ti… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-complete/volume-6/1-timothy/chapter-4|Matthew Henry Complete on 1 Timothy 4]]
