# Judges 15

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Judges 15: Samson is denied his wife, He smites the Philistines. (1–8). Samson kills a thousand of the Philistines with a jaw-bone. (9–17). His distress from thirst. (18–20).

## Core claims

- When there are differences between relations, let those be reckoned the wisest and best, who are most forward to forgive or forget, and most willing to stoop and yield for the sake of peace. In the means which Samson employed, we must look at the power of God supplying them, and making them successful, to mortify the… [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/judges/chapter-15#Verses 1–8|Matthew Henry Concise on Judges 15]]
- Sin dispirits men, it hides from their eyes the things that belong to their peace. The Israelites blamed Samson for what he had done against the Philistines, as if he had done them a great injury. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/judges/chapter-15#Verses 9–17|Matthew Henry Concise on Judges 15]]
- So little notice did the men of Judah take of their deliverer, that he was ready to perish for want of a draught of water. Thus are the greatest slights often put upon those who do the greatest services. [[sources/commentaries_english/mhenry-concise/judges/chapter-15#Verses 18–20|Matthew Henry Concise on Judges 15]]
- Samson, when he courted an alliance with the Philistines, did but seek an occasion against them, Jdg 14:4.
