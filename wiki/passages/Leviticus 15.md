# Leviticus 15

## Summary

Matthew Henry Concise on Leviticus 15: Laws concerning ceremonial uncleanness. (1-23).

## Core claims

- We need not be curious in explaining these laws; but have reason to be thankful that we need fear no defilement, except that of sin, nor need ceremonial and burdensome purifications. These laws remind us that God sees all things, even those which escape the notice of men.
